# How to Recruit?

Hiring from IIT Gandhinagar is straightforward, structured and free of charge.

### How to recruit from IIT Gandhinagar?

Go to the Job Announcement Form (JAF) and/or the Internship Announcement Form (IAF).

01

#### Submit JAF / IAF

Register your full-time or internship requirement.

02

#### Share Profile

We circulate the role and coordinate PPTs / tests.

03

#### Schedule Drive

Up to three companies per day during the season.

04

#### Make Offers

Interview on-campus or virtually and onboard talent.
