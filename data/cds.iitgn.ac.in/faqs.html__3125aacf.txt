# Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions from recruiters and students about the recruitment process at IIT Gandhinagar.

Placement Hiring Season starts from the first week of December and the Internship Hiring Season starts from the first week of August. We encourage recruiters to conduct the interview process for full-time and internships (Summer, Winter and 6-month) simultaneously during their visit. Certain pre-placement processes (e.g. online tests, PPT) may be conducted before the scheduled interview date.

The Institute has developed strategic partnerships with leading universities, industries and R&D organizations within India and abroad for student and faculty exchanges and research collaborations — including the California Institute of Technology; École Nationale Supérieure d'Arts et Métiers, France; ICSTE, Switzerland; Institute Supérieur d'Électronique de Paris, France; Instituto Universitário de Lisboa, Portugal; Israel Institute of Technology Haifa; Notre Dame University; SAC-ISRO & Physical Research Laboratory Ahmedabad; Ricoh Corporation, Japan; Underwriters Laboratories, Chicago; University of Saskatchewan, Canada; and University of Strathclyde, UK, among others.

IITGN promotes excellence in science and technology, as well as the humanities and social sciences. The Institute only recruits candidates with excellent academic records, demonstrated potential for excellence in research, and broad academic outlooks for faculty positions.
