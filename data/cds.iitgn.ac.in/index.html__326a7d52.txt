About the Institute

## About IIT Gandhinagar

 IIT Gandhinagar was established in 2008 and is located near Palaj, Gandhinagar, Gujarat on the bank of river Sabarmati. Gandhinagar is in proximity to Ahmedabad — one of the oldest living cities in India, known for a rich cultural past as well as state-of-the-art infrastructure, thriving industries and many modern amenities.

About CDS

## About CDS

 The Career Development Services (CDS) office at IIT Gandhinagar is committed to empower students to discover their true potential and pursue their dreams in all possible immediate career verticals — including Entrepreneurship, Placement, Higher Study and Competitive Examinations. Students can avail CDS resources to explore and plan their career effectively. The office also conducts sessions on career counselling, resume preparation, screening tests, group discussion, interview process and personality development, among others.

 CDS provides a platform where the opportunity-taker and the opportunity-giver come together to explore avenues of a fruitful and synergistic alliance — through internships, industry visits and placements with participating organizations from India and abroad.
