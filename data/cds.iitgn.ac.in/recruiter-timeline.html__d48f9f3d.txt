# Timeline for the Process

Plan your hiring season around two clear recruitment windows.

Two clear hiring windows make scheduling simple.

- Internship season begins the first week of August.
- Placement season begins the first week of December.
-  Full-time & internship interviews can run simultaneously during your visit.
-  Pre-placement steps (online tests, PPTs) may precede the interview date.
