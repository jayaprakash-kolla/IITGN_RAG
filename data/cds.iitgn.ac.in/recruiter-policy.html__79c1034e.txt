# Recruitment Policy

General guidelines that ensure a fair and efficient hiring process for both students and recruiters.

Offer Limit: Students can secure a maximum of one job or internship offer through CDS. They may apply to multiple companies until they receive an offer.

Simultaneous Offers: Spot offers are not permitted. All offers made on a particular day will be announced together. If selected by multiple companies, students must choose one within 24 hours and inform CDS.

Pre-Placement Offers (PPOs): Accepted PPOs count as a job offer. If rejected, students remain eligible. PPO status must be communicated within two weeks post-internship.

Offer Acceptance & Rejection: Once a student receives an offer, they are considered placed, regardless of acceptance or rejection.

Concurrent Hiring Processes: If results of one company are delayed and another offer is received, the first declared result will be treated as the valid offer.

Application Interest: Students should apply only if genuinely interested. The first declared result among applied opportunities will be considered.

Automatic Restriction Post-Offer: Receiving one offer restricts students from further applications.

PPO Status Post-Internship: Companies should ideally communicate PPO status within two weeks after internship completion.

By following these guidelines, IIT Gandhinagar ensures a fair and efficient hiring process for both students and recruiters.
