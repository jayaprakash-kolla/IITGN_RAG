creativity at all levels.

CULTURAL FEST –
BLITHCHRON
Blithchron is the flagship cultural fest of IIT Gandhiagar, uniting
talented students from across India in a celebration of art, energy,
and expression. Known for its crowd-favourite events like
Antarangee (Street Play), Panache (Fashion Show), String Theory
(Rock Band Battle), Street Beat (Dance Battle), and Synchronize
(Dance–Off), the fest culminates in electrifying Promites featuring
sensational artists and unforgettable performances. Drawing over
22,000 attendees from all over India, Blithchron has grown into
one of the most awaited college cultural festivals in the region.

TECHNICAL FEST AMALTHEA
Amalthea is India’s first student-run Annual Technical Summit
organized by the students of IIT Gandhiagar since 2010. It is a
vibrant space where innovation, creativity, and technology
converge, bringing together students, industry leaders,
researchers, and academics to connect, collaborate, and
share ideas. From hands-on workshops and challenging
competitions to inspiring talks and a buzzing tech expo,
Amalthea offers a diverse range of experiences, designed to
ignite curiosity and spark innovation, making it one of the
most distinguished technical summits in the country.

MEAN MECHANICS | Robotics Club
Grounded in the philosophy of "Learning through Application," Mean
Mechanics inspires curiosity in robotics and mechanical engineering.

METIS | Coding Club
Metis is IITGN's software development club, focused on real-world project
building, open-source contributions, and modern programming practices.
Members work across web development, mobile applications, and system
programming, fostering a hands-on culture of building innovative tech
solutions.

ODYSSEY | Astronomy Club
Odyssey fosters a passion for astronomy, astrophysics, and cosmology
across disciplines. Through stargazing sessions, student-led talks,
workshops, and field trips, the club offers both theoretical foundations
and hands-on experience with telescopes and astronomical instruments
bringing the universe a little closer to campus.

EII | Entrepreneurship Initiative IIT Gandhinagar
EII is IITGN's student-run body dedicated to fostering entrepreneurial
thinking and innovation on campus. It bridges students with the

IIEC is IIT Gandhinagar's technology business incubator, established in
2012 as a Section 8 company. It supports startups from ideation to growth

connections, and IPR support. Its flagship programs include NIDHI Prayas,
NIDHI Seed Support, BUILD, and HGV for student, faculty, and alumni
startups.
