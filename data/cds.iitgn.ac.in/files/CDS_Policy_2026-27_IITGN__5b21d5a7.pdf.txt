FOREWORD
Career Development Services (CDS) earlier known as the Student Placement Office (SPO) is the officially
designated institutional body of IIT Gandhinagar responsible for administering and facilitating the campus
placement and internship ecosystem. CDS serves as the formal interface between IIT Gandhinagar and the
professional world, engaging with industry, research institutions, and academic organizations to provide
structured, credible, and equitable recruitment opportunities to eligible students.
The placement and internship landscape has grown considerably in scale, complexity, and competitiveness
over successive recruitment cycles. The diversity of recruiting organizations, the multiplicity of processes, the
increasing expectations of professionalism, and the institutional commitments involved require that CDS
operations be governed by a formal, comprehensive, and enforceable framework. This Policy has been
developed in response to that institutional requirement.
This Policy Framework is intended to serve three distinct but complementary purposes. First, it constitutes a
governance framework that protects the institutional interests of IIT Gandhinagar, defines the authority
relationships within CDS, and establishes clear accountability structures at every level of CDS operations.
Second, it functions as a professional operational code, setting out the standards of conduct, documentation,
communication, and compliance expected of all participants in CDS-administered processes. Third, it
provides the structural foundation for a recruitment ecosystem that is efficient, transparent, consistent, and
credible — both in the eyes of participating organizations and in the interests of the students it serves.
The CDS Student Team carries a significant operational responsibility in the daily execution of recruitment
activities. The quality of institutional engagement with recruiting organizations, the management of complex
logistics, and the maintenance of student conduct standards are functions that depend substantially on the
commitment, professionalism, and integrity of the CDS Student Team. This Policy formally recognizes that
contribution and establishes a governance architecture that enables effective student-led operations within a
framework of appropriate institutional oversight.
Participation in CDS processes is not a passive entitlement. It carries professional and ethical obligations —
to recruiting organizations, to fellow students, and to the institution. The credibility of IIT Gandhinagar in the
eyes of the professional world is an institutional asset that must be actively protected by every participant in
the recruitment ecosystem. This Policy gives operational expression to that expectation.
All registered students, members of the CDS Student Team, and other participants in CDS processes are
expected to familiarize themselves fully with this Policy and to comply with its provisions at all times. The
terms of this Policy shall be binding upon all participants from the moment of registration, and ignorance of
any provision shall not constitute a valid defense.
Career Development Services, IIT Gandhinagar

SECTION 1 — FOUNDATIONAL PROVISIONS
1.1 Purpose and Objectives
Career Development Services (CDS) — earlier known as the Student Placement Office (SPO) — at IIT
Gandhinagar is the officially designated institutional body responsible for facilitating, coordinating, and
administering the campus placement and internship processes for eligible students of IIT Gandhinagar. This
Policy Framework ('the Policy') is issued to establish a unified, comprehensive, and enforceable governance
and compliance framework governing all activities, processes, relationships, and obligations within the
operational domain of CDS.
The Policy is intended to:
1.1.1 Establish and formalize the governance structure, operational hierarchy, authority
relationships, and institutional roles within CDS;
1.1.2 Define the rights, powers, responsibilities, and obligations of all parties operating within or
interacting with the CDS ecosystem, including students, the CDS Student Team, CDS
Administration, the Chair CDS, and recruiting organizations;
1.1.3 Provide a clear, unambiguous, and enforceable framework for the conduct of placement and
internship processes, including registration, eligibility, selection, offer management, and cycle
completion;
1.1.4 Establish a robust disciplinary and compliance framework with clearly defined progressive
consequences for policy violations;
1.1.5 Protect the institutional integrity, operational authority, and reputational interests of CDS and
IIT Gandhinagar;
1.1.6 Standardize all operational procedures,
requirements, and conduct expectations;

communication

protocols,

documentation

1.1.7 Preserve institutional flexibility, discretionary authority, and emergency operational powers
necessary for effective cycle administration; and
1.1.8 Serve as the primary reference document for all CDS-related matters during the applicable
recruitment cycle.

1.2 Legal and Institutional Characterization of CDS
For the purposes of this Policy and all CDS activities, the following characterizations are mandatory and shall
govern interpretation of all related provisions:
1.2.1 CDS is a facilitation and coordination body only. CDS does not function as an employer,
recruiter, guarantor, placement assurance body, or contracting authority in relation to any
employment or internship relationship between a student and a recruiting organization.
1.2.2 CDS does not guarantee, represent, or warrant: (a) placement or internship; (b) compensation,
designation, or job role quality; (c) continuation or non-revocation of any offer; (d) the conduct,
decisions, timelines, or representations of any recruiting organization; or (e) any outcome of the
recruitment process.
1.2.3 The role of CDS is limited to providing a structured, administered platform through which
eligible students may access recruitment opportunities made available by participating organizations.
All decisions regarding selection, rejection, offer extension, offer revocation, and post-placement
matters vest entirely with the respective recruiting organizations.
1.2.4 Nothing in this Policy, and no communication, representation, or statement by any CDS
personnel, shall be construed as creating any contractual obligation, entitlement, or guarantee on
behalf of CDS or IIT Gandhinagar.

1.3 Scope of Applicability

1.3.1 This Policy applies to all students of IIT Gandhinagar who register or participate in any CDSadministered process, including but not limited to placement cycles, industrial internship cycles, and

1.3.2 This Policy applies to all members of the CDS Student Team, including the Student Placement

1.3.3 This Policy governs all communications, conduct, processes, documentation, and interactions

1.3.4 Provisions of this Policy that are specifically directed at particular categories of students,

1.4 Deemed Consent and Binding Effect
1.4.1 Registration with CDS — whether pre-registration or final registration — shall constitute
deemed acknowledgement, acceptance, and consent by the student to comply fully with this Policy
and all operational instructions issued by CDS or the CDS Student Team.
1.4.2 Participation in any CDS-administered process shall constitute deemed consent to this Policy,
irrespective of whether the student has fully read, partially read, or not read the Policy document.
1.4.3 Ignorance of this Policy or any provision thereof shall not constitute a valid defense in any
disciplinary, compliance, or grievance matter.
1.4.4 The obligation to remain informed of this Policy and all official communications issued by CDS
rests entirely with the student.

1.5 Communication Responsibility
Students are solely responsible for regularly reviewing all official communications issued by CDS, including
emails, WhatsApp messages, CDS Portal notices, Google Form notifications, and telephone calls. Failure to
review, access, or respond to any official communication shall not constitute a valid defense against any
disciplinary action, missed deadline, or compliance failure.

A research or academic engagement opportunity at a recognized national or international
university, research institute, or academic organization, facilitated by CDS, distinct from an
Industrial Internship.

The score calculated or derived by CDS using CPI data received from the Academic Office of

3.2 Warning Framework
3.2.1 A Warning is the least severe disciplinary action and is issued for minor procedural or
first-instance behavioural violations.
3.2.2 Grounds for Warning: A Warning shall be issued for: (a) absence from mandatory CDS
or PDC sessions; (b) absence from pre-placement talks (PPTs) of registered companies; (c)
first instance of non-compliance with the prescribed resume format; and (d) first instance of
minor behavioural misconduct.
3.2.3 One Warning Per Student: Only one formal Warning may be issued to any student
during the entirety of their association with CDS, across all cycles. Any subsequent violation
that would otherwise qualify for a Warning shall instead result in the direct issuance of a
Strike.
3.2.4 A Warning shall be issued in writing by the CDS Student Team or CDS Administration
and shall be placed on record.

3.3 Strike Framework
3.3.1 A Strike is a formal disciplinary record issued for a significant breach of CDS policy.
Strikes are cumulative across all CDS cycles, including both the internship and placement
cycles.
3.3.2 Upon accumulation of three Strikes, the student shall be permanently disqualified from
all ongoing and future CDS placement and internship processes. Permanent disqualification
under this provision shall not be subject to appeal.
3.3.3 Grounds for Strike: A Strike shall be issued for the following violations:
a) Failure to inform CDS of an External Offer within 24 hours of receiving the
selection communication;
b) Initiating any direct communication with HR representatives, companies, or
academic institutions at any stage of the recruitment process — including
communication regarding acceptance, rejection, or withdrawal of offers — without
routing it through CDS;
c) Failure to attend any pre-interview stage (including tests, group discussions,
assignments, or case submissions) for a company or opportunity for which the
student is registered;
d) Submission of a resume not complying with the format prescribed by CDS (after a

3.4 Penalty Framework
3.4.1 A Penalty is the most severe disciplinary action and shall result in disqualification from
all ongoing and future CDS processes. Depending on the gravity of the violation, the case
may be referred by CDS to the Senate Student Affairs Committee (SSAC) or the Dean of
Students' Affairs for further institutional review and action.
3.4.2 Grounds for Penalty: A Penalty shall be imposed for the following violations:
a) Attempting, facilitating, or engaging in cheating, fraud, or malpractice during any
stage of the selection process;
b) Violation of Operational Instructions given by CDS Administration, CDS Student
Team, or Company Representatives;
c) Displaying disrespectful, abusive, intimidating, or unprofessional behaviour
toward CDS Administration, Company Personnel, or CDS Student Team members;
d) Failure to attend any scheduled interview round(s) during the recruitment process
(No-Show);
e) Submission of a resume containing materially incorrect information with the
intention to misrepresent qualifications, background or any form directly relevant
to the credentials;
f) Violation of the institutional honor code of IIT Gandhinagar in the context of any
CDS activity;
g) Impersonation or unauthorized assistance during any recruitment process;
h) Unauthorized disclosure, misuse, or exploitation of Confidential Information; and
i) Any other act of serious misconduct determined by the Chair CDS to warrant a
Penalty.

3.5 Strike and Penalty Waiver
3.5.1 A Strike may be waived only in genuine circumstances, including a documented medical
emergency or an institute-approved commitment. The waiver request must be accompanied
by valid supporting documentation and must be submitted promptly.
3.5.2 All waiver requests shall be reviewed at the sole discretion of the Chair CDS. The
decision of the Chair CDS on any waiver request shall be final and non-appealable.
3.5.3 The absence of a waiver request shall authorise CDS to proceed with a disciplinary
action.

3.6 Procedural Fairness
3.6.1 Before issuance of a Penalty or 3rd Strike, the student shall be given an opportunity to
present their position to the CDS Administration or Chair CDS.
3.6.2 The CDS Student Team's factual observations, documented records,
communications shall form part of the official record in all disciplinary proceedings.

SECTION 4 — GOVERNANCE STRUCTURE AND INSTITUTIONAL
HIERARCHY
4.1 Governance Architecture
CDS operates within a three-tier governance architecture designed to ensure effective institutional
oversight, operational efficiency, and clear delineation of authority:
4.1.1 Tier I — Institutional Authority: Chair, Career Development Services (Chair CDS).
4.1.2 Tier II — Administrative Authority: CDS Administration (CDS Staff).
4.1.3 Tier III — Operational Authority: CDS Student Team (Core Team and Student Team).

4.2 Chair, Career Development Services (Chair CDS)
4.2.1 The Chair CDS shall be the supreme authority in all matters pertaining to CDS policies,
operations, disciplinary proceedings, and governance.
4.2.2 All decisions of the Chair CDS shall be final, binding, and not subject to revision or
revocation under any circumstance.
4.2.3 The Chair CDS shall possess the authority to: (a) alter, modify, amend, or repeal any
provision of this Policy; (b) resolve all disputes, inconsistencies, and undefined situations;
(c) approve or deny appeals and waiver requests; (d) impose or waive penalties in
exceptional circumstances; and (e) issue emergency directives.
4.2.4 The Chair CDS may delegate specific operational or administrative functions to the CDS
Administration or CDS Student Team as deemed appropriate.
4.2.5 All undefined situations — i.e., circumstances not expressly addressed by this Policy —
shall be resolved by the Chair CDS in consultation with the CDS Administration and the CDS
Student Core Team. The resolution so arrived at shall be binding.

4.3 CDS Administration
4.3.1 CDS Administration shall be responsible for the institutional, administrative, financial,
documentation, and regulatory functions of CDS.
4.3.2 CDS Administration shall serve as the official institutional interface between CDS and
external parties, including recruiting organizations and institutional bodies.
4.3.3 CDS Administration shall maintain all official records, including disciplinary records,
registration databases, offer records, and financial records.
4.3.4 CDS Administration shall provide operational oversight, support, and authorization for
major process decisions.

4.4 CDS Student Team — Institutional Status and Authority
4.4.1 The CDS Student Team is an officially constituted operational body of CDS, formally
recognized under this Policy. It is not a voluntary student organization but a structured,
institutionally embedded operational unit with defined responsibilities and formal
recognition, functioning under the authority of the Chair CDS.
4.4.2 The CDS Student Team shall serve as the primary operational interface between
students, recruiting organizations, CDS Administration, and the Chair CDS.
4.4.3 The CDS Student Team possesses formally recognized operational authority in relation
to process execution, recruiter coordination, assistance in logistics management, attendance
monitoring, communication management, conduct reporting, and operational supervision,
exercised under and accountable to the Chair CDS.
4.4.4 Operational instructions issued by authorized members of the CDS Student Team
during Active Recruitment Processes shall carry the same operational authority as

4.4.6 CDS Student Team members acting in good faith within their defined authority shall

6.1.1 CDS does not independently determine eligibility criteria for individual hiring processes.

SECTION 7 — PLACEMENT POLICY
7.1 One Student One Offer Policy
7.1.1 Each student may avail a maximum of one job offer through CDS. Upon receipt of the
first job offer through CDS — irrespective of acceptance or rejection of the offer — the
student shall be deemed 'placed' and shall be ineligible to continue in the recruitment
processes of any other organization, subject to the Day-0 exceptions specified in Section 7.2.
7.1.2 A student may apply to any number of companies until they receive one job offer
through CDS.

7.2 Multiple Offers and Selection Rules
7.2.1 Day-0 Exception: Where multiple organizations are scheduled to conduct processes and
declare results in the same slot on a designated Day-0 event, a student who receives multiple
offers within that slot may choose one offer as per their preference. The student's selection
decision must be communicated to the CDS Office before the commencement of the next
slot.
7.2.2 Same-Day Offers (Non-Day-0): If a student participates in processes of multiple
companies on the same day and receives an offer from one company while results of other
companies are pending at the end of the day, the offer received on that day shall be treated
as the student's first and final offer, rendering the student ineligible for further recruitment
processes.
7.2.3 Deferred Results on Same Date: If results of processes concluded on earlier date(s) are
declared on the same later date, the student shall be permitted to choose one offer from
among those declared on that date.

7.3 PPO and PPI
7.3.1 PPOs channelled through CDS, once accepted by the student, shall be counted as a job
offer and the One Student One Offer Policy shall apply.
7.3.2 If a student rejects a PPO, they shall remain eligible to use CDS placement services
under normal rules.
7.3.3 PPI shall be treated as a regular interview process, and all CDS eligibility and policy
rules shall apply.

7.4 Offer Revocation by Recruiting Organization
7.4.1 If a recruiting organization revokes an offer previously extended to a student, the
student must inform CDS within 24 hours of receiving notice of such revocation.
7.4.2 For off-campus revocations, the student must submit official documentation from the
recruiting organization confirming the revocation.
7.4.3 CDS shall review the case and based on its assessment, determine the student's
eligibility to apply for other opportunities within the same cycle.
7.4.4 Offer revocation by a recruiting organization shall not qualify the affected student for
deferred placements.
7.4.5 CDS reserves the authority to report any recruiting organization to the All IIT Placement
Council (AIPC) in cases involving unethical conduct, patterns of offer revocation, breach of
commitments made to students or institutions, recruiter misconduct, operational
misrepresentation, reputational concerns, or conduct adversely affecting students or the

SECTION 8 — INDUSTRIAL INTERNSHIP POLICY
8.1 One Internship Per Cycle Principle
8.1.1 A student may secure only one internship through CDS during an internship cycle,
irrespective of whether the internship is industrial or academic, Summer or Semester-Long,
on-campus or off-campus, or online.
8.1.2 Once a student receives, accepts, or is deemed to have secured an internship offer
through CDS, the student shall become immediately ineligible for all remaining internship
opportunities in that cycle through CDS, including industrial and academic internship
opportunities.
8.1.3 This principle is absolute and admits of no exceptions beyond those expressly provided
in this Policy (specifically, Section 6.4.6 permitting a second academic internship across
different cycles of a student's academic program, and not within the same cycle).

8.2 Selection and Offer Management
8.2.1 Day-0 Exception: Where multiple organizations are scheduled to conduct processes and
declare results in the same slot on a designated Day-0 event, a student who receives multiple
offers within that slot may choose one offer as per their preference. The student's selection
decision must be communicated to the CDS Office before the commencement of the next
slot.
8.2.2 Same-Day Offers (Non-Day-0): If a student participates in processes of multiple
companies on the same day and receives an offer from one company while results of other
companies are pending at the end of the day, the offer received on that day shall be treated
as the student's first and final offer, rendering the student ineligible for further recruitment
processes.
8.2.3 Deferred Results on Same Date: If results of processes concluded on earlier date(s) are
declared on the same later date, the student shall be permitted to choose one offer from
among those declared on that date.

8.3 Communication Protocol — Internship
8.3.1 Students must not directly communicate with recruiting organizations regarding
acceptance, rejection, or withdrawal of internship offers. All such communications must be
routed through CDS. Violation of this protocol shall result in disqualification from CDS
processes for that particular company and may attract additional disciplinary action.

8.4 Joining, Completion, and Withdrawal
8.4.1 Students who secure an internship through CDS are expected to join and complete the
internship as offered.
8.4.2 If a student declines, fails to join, or discontinues an internship secured through CDS
without prior approval from CDS, CDS may initiate disciplinary action including the issuance
of a Strike or Penalty, determined on a case-by-case basis.
8.4.3 In all cases of internship rejection or withdrawal — irrespective of when the rejection or
withdrawal occurs — the student shall be permanently ineligible to participate in future
placement or internship processes of that company through CDS.

SECTION 9 — ACADEMIC INTERNSHIP POLICY
9.1 Ranking and Selection Process
9.1.1 CDS shall arrive at a final ranking of applicants for academic internships using a
composite scoring methodology: 50% weight to the departmental score (as provided by the
respective Discipline Coordinator on a 100-point scale) and 50% weight to the APS derived
from official CPI data received from the Academic Office.
9.1.2 Rankings shall be shared individually with students and shall be treated as Confidential
Information.
9.1.3 The ranking and selection process is discipline-specific and shall be administered
separately for each discipline.

9.2 Academic Internship Limit and Financial Support
9.2.1 Subject to the One Internship Per Cycle Policy (Section 8.1), a student may undertake a
maximum of two academic internships through CDS across their entire academic program at
IIT Gandhinagar.
9.2.2 This provision is specifically intended to accommodate Dual Major students who may
benefit from research engagement during multiple stages of their academic program.
9.2.3 Institutional financial support may be availed for only one academic internship during a
student's academic program. No institutional financial support shall be available in respect of
a second academic internship, whether in the form of stipend, travel support,
accommodation, visa expenses, or soft loans.
9.2.4 The second academic internship, if pursued, shall be facilitated through CDS in the
same operational manner as the first, subject to all eligibility, process, and compliance
requirements.

9.3 Financial Support
9.3.1 CDS does not directly provide financial assistance for academic internships. The entire
supporting mechanism is administered and decided by the Student Affairs Office at IITGN.
9.3.2 Where a prior agreement exists between IITGN and the host institution, IITGN may
provide financial assistance in the form of stipends, travel allowances, visa fees, or
accommodation support. The nature and extent of such support shall be determined by the
Student Affairs Office.
9.3.3 Students who secure online academic internships via CDS do not typically receive
financial support.
9.3.4 Queries regarding financial support must be directed to the Student Affairs Office and
not to CDS.

9.4 Student Obligations — Academic Internships
9.4.1 Students must strictly adhere to all deadlines communicated by CDS.
9.4.2 Students must not directly contact faculty or representatives of host institutions during
the CDS selection process. CDS shall guide selected students regarding further
communication with host institutions.
9.4.3 Students selected for an academic internship through CDS are expected to join and
complete the internship.
9.4.4 Students are expected to submit bi-weekly progress updates to CDS for the entire
duration of the internship.
9.4.5 Students who complete academic internships are required to present their research
work to the IITGN community through at least one of the following forums: (a) Undergraduate
Research Conclave (UGRC); (b) SRIP Poster Session; or (c) Institute Symposium.

9.4.6 Students are expected to continue research initiated during the internship after return,
with the objective of producing a logical conclusion such as a publication.
9.4.7 Students are expected to apply for visa and other travel documentation well in advance
to avoid high last-minute costs. Students awaiting disbursement of financial support are
encouraged to secure soft loans to minimize delays.
9.4.8 Applicants for international academic internships must possess a valid passport at the

9.4.9 Failure to join or premature departure from an academic internship without genuine
documented reason, upon notification to CDS by the host institution, may result in
debarment from future CDS activities or a Strike, or both, at the discretion of the Chair CDS.

9.5 Off-Campus Application Control — Academic Internships
Students who intend to apply independently for off-campus academic internships are subject to the
following procedural requirements in the interest of maintaining institutional relationships,
preserving existing Memoranda of Understanding (MoUs), avoiding duplicate outreach, and
protecting the institutional reputation of IIT Gandhinagar.
9.5.1 Pre-Application Disclosure: Students intending to independently apply for off-campus
academic internships must first formally inform CDS of the institutions, universities,
laboratories, professors, or organizations to which they intend to apply, prior to initiating any
communication with such institutions.
9.5.2 Submission of Proposed List: Students shall submit the proposed list of institutions or
contacts through the process prescribed by CDS before initiating communication. CDS shall
notify students of the applicable submission process at the commencement of the relevant
cycle or upon request.
9.5.3 CDS Approval Authority: CDS reserves the authority to approve, deny, restrict, or
regulate independent applications to particular institutions in the interest of: (a) maintaining
institutional relationships and existing MoUs; (b) preventing duplicate or conflicting outreach
that may damage institutional credibility; (c) ensuring coordination of contacts already
managed by CDS; and (d) protecting the institutional reputation of IIT Gandhinagar.
9.5.4 Restricted Institutions: Students shall not independently contact institutions that have
been specifically restricted or reserved by CDS, whether for reasons of existing MoUs, active
negotiations, or institutional coordination requirements.
9.5.5 Violation: Non-compliance with the requirements of this Section shall constitute a policy
violation and may attract disciplinary consequences including a Strike, at the discretion of
the Chair CDS, depending on the nature and severity of the non-compliance.

internship cycle from interested students:
9.6.1 Updated Resume, formatted in accordance with CDS norms, with research projects
performed individually highlighted as priority items.
9.6.2 Academic Statement of Purpose, highlighting: (a) inclination towards research and
previous research work, if any; (b) areas of research interest; and (c) experience in individual
initiative and teamwork.
9.6.3 Valid passport and other required travel credentials.

and other students.

interviews — on weekdays and weekends, and at short notice, as the recruitment calendar

13.1 Emergency Operational Powers

requirement, or procedure during Active Recruitment Processes in the following circumstances:

13.1.2 Emergencies notified or caused by recruiting organizations;
13.1.3 Force Majeure Events;
13.1.4 Institutional disruptions including academic schedule changes, campus-wide
emergencies, or administrative exigencies; and

CDS, require immediate procedural modification.
13.1.6 Emergency directives issued by the Chair CDS or CDS Administration under
emergency powers shall be binding immediately upon communication and shall not require
prior notice or consultation.

13.2 Institutional Discretion
13.2.1 CDS retains broad discretionary authority in exceptional operational circumstances,
including the authority to: (a) modify standard procedures in the larger interest of students or
institutional reputation; (b) make case-by-case determinations where the Policy does not
prescribe a specific outcome; and (c) apply the spirit of this Policy in situations not
contemplated by its express provisions.
13.2.2 Exercises of institutional discretion shall not create precedents binding upon CDS in
future situations.

13.3 Policy Amendment Authority
13.3.1 The Chair CDS holds exclusive authority to alter, modify, amend, or repeal any
provision of this Policy at any time, with or without prior notice, in the larger interest of
students or the objectives of CDS.
13.3.2 Amendments shall be communicated through Official Communication Channels and
shall be effective upon communication.
13.3.3 Students shall be bound by the most current version of the Policy as communicated by
CDS.

SECTION 14 — LIMITATION OF LIABILITY, NON-GUARANTEE, AND
NON-RESPONSIBILITY CLAUSES
14.1 No Guarantee of Placement or Internship
CDS does not guarantee, represent, or warrant the placement or internship of any registered student.
The availability of opportunities, the extension of offers, the terms of any offer, and the decisions of
recruiting organizations are entirely beyond the control of CDS. No representation by any CDS
personnel shall be construed as a guarantee of any employment or internship outcome.

14.2 No Liability for Recruiter Actions
CDS shall not bear any liability for: (a) the actions, decisions, representations, or omissions of
recruiting organizations; (b) changes in job roles, compensation, or work environment post-joining;
(c) revocation of offers by recruiting organizations; (d) the quality, safety, or suitability of the work
environment; or (e) any post-selection dispute between a student and a recruiting organization.
Students are advised to conduct their own due diligence before accepting any offer.

14.3 No Vested Rights
Participation in any CDS process, completion of registration, or receipt of any prior benefit through
CDS shall not create any vested right, entitlement, or legitimate expectation of placement, internship,
financial support, or any other benefit in the current or any future cycle.

14.4 No Entitlement to Process Continuation
The initiation of any recruitment process does not create an entitlement in any student to the
continuation or completion of that process. CDS reserves the right to suspend, cancel, or modify any
process at any time in the exercise of its operational or emergency powers.

14.5 Limitation on Scope of Liability
To the maximum extent permissible under applicable institutional and legal norms, CDS, CDS
Administration, and the CDS Student Team shall not be liable for any direct, indirect, consequential,
or incidental harm arising from or in connection with the exercise of powers, functions, or
discretions under this Policy, provided such exercise is made in good faith.

Policy. Where this Policy is silent, ambiguous, or admits of more than one interpretation, the

ANNEXURE C

FORMALIZED FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQs)
The following FAQs are issued as authoritative interpretive guidance. Where any conflict exists
between the FAQs and the substantive provisions of this Policy, the substantive provisions shall
prevail.
Q1 Will I be permitted to participate in further recruitment processes if I have already received an
offer?
No. IIT Gandhinagar observes a strict One Student One Offer policy. Upon receipt of the first job
offer — irrespective of acceptance or rejection — the student is deemed placed and is ineligible
for further CDS recruitment processes. The only exception is the Day-0 scenario, where multiple
organizations declare results in the same slot; the student must choose one offer and
communicate the decision to CDS before the next slot commences.
Q2 What if multiple offers are declared on the same date following processes conducted on earlier
dates?
If the recruitment processes of multiple organizations were concluded on prior dates and results
are declared on the same subsequent date, the student shall be permitted to select one offer
from among those declared on that date.
Q3 May I secure an industrial internship and an academic internship in the same cycle?
No. The One Internship Per Cycle Policy is absolute. A student may secure only one internship —
whether industrial or academic, Summer or Semester-Long through CDS in any single internship
cycle. Upon securing an offer of any kind through CDS, the student becomes ineligible for all
remaining internship opportunities in that cycle.
Q4 Can I undertake more than one academic internship through CDS during my academic program?
Yes, subject to conditions. A student may undertake a maximum of two academic internships
through CDS during their entire academic program. This is specifically to accommodate Dual
Degree and Dual Major students. However, institutional financial support may be availed for only
one academic internship. In any single cycle, only one internship may be secured through CDS.
Q5 I have secured an External Offer. Am I required to inform CDS?
Yes, unconditionally. You must inform CDS within 24 hours of receiving the selection
communication. Failure to do so shall result in a Strike. If you accept the External Offer, you will
be automatically deregistered from the CDS cycle and the registration fee refunded. If you wish
to continue in the CDS process, you must formally reject the External Offer and share a copy of
the rejection with CDS. For further details you can refer to Section 7.5.
Q6 I applied for a company by mistake and wish to deregister. Is this permitted?
Deregistration from a specific company's process is permitted only before the application
deadline. After the deadline, you are required to participate in all subsequent stages of that
company's process. Failure to do so shall constitute a Process Violation.
Q7 Can I directly contact an organization to inquire about shortlists, results, or offer terms?
No. Direct communication with HR representatives or Company Personnel at any stage of the
recruitment process — including regarding shortlists, results, acceptance, rejection, or
withdrawal — without routing through CDS is a strict policy violation that shall result in a Strike.
Q8 What should I do if two interviews are scheduled simultaneously or if I encounter a technical
issue?
You must immediately inform the CDS Office with supporting evidence (e.g., screenshots of
technical errors or conflicting schedules). CDS will coordinate with the respective organizations
to the extent possible. You must not independently contact the company to reschedule or
resolve the issue.
Q9 What are the refund provisions for the placement registration fee?
Full refund: Deregistration during Phase I of the deregistration window (dates as notified). Partial
refund (50%): Deregistration during Phase II of the deregistration window. No refund: If you have
received a job offer through CDS, if deregistered due to a policy violation, or if you fail to submit
a deregistration request before the end of Phase II.
Q10 What happens if I arrive late to a scheduled interview or test?
Late entry is subject to the sole discretion of the recruiting organization and CDS. In most cases,
late entry is not permitted and the absence shall be treated as a No-Show attracting Penalty-level
disciplinary action. Students must arrive well in advance of all scheduled processes.
Q11 What happens if an organization revokes my job offer?

You must inform CDS within 24 hours and submit official documentation from the organization
confirming the revocation. CDS will review the matter and determine your eligibility to apply for
other opportunities within the same cycle. A revoked offer does not qualify you for deferred
placements.
Q12 What is the difference between a Warning, a Strike, and a Penalty?
Warning: Issued for minor procedural violations or first-instance behavioral misconduct. Only
one Warning may be issued per student. Any subsequent Warning-level violation directly results
in a Strike. Strike: A formal disciplinary record for a significant policy breach. Strikes are
cumulative across all cycles; three Strikes result in permanent deregistration. Penalty: The most
severe disciplinary action, resulting in immediate deregistration and potential institutional
escalation, reserved for grave offenses including cheating, serious misconduct, No-Shows, and
honor code violations.
Q13 Can a Strike be waived?
A Strike may be waived only in genuine documented circumstances — such as a medical
emergency or an institute-approved commitment — subject to submission of valid supporting
documentation. All such requests are reviewed at the sole discretion of the Chair CDS. The
decision shall be final and non-appealable.
Q14 What are the consequences of rejecting or withdrawing from an internship offer?
The consequences depend on whether the offer is a Day-0 or non-Day-0 offer: (a) Day-0 offer —
rejection before acceptance: 1 Strike; (b) Day-0 offer — acceptance followed by withdrawal: 2
Strikes; (c) Non-Day-0 offer — rejection before acceptance: No Strike; (d) Non-Day-0 offer —
acceptance followed by withdrawal: 1 Strike. In all four scenarios, the student is permanently
ineligible for future placement or internship processes of that company through CDS. Refer to
Section 8.5 for the complete matrix.
Q15 How do I update my Academic Performance Score (APS) in CDS records?
You must formally request the Academic Office (ar.acad@iitgn.ac.in) to communicate your
updated CPI directly to CDS Staff (cds@iitgn.ac.in). The updated APS may be used for CDS
purposes only after this official communication is received by CDS. IMS screenshots, studentforwarded emails, or any other informal modes of communication shall not be accepted.
Q16 Are zero-credit courses treated as backlogs for the purposes of APS computation?
No. Courses carrying zero credit — including Physical Education (PE), Foundation Programme
(FP), and Viva-Voce — are excluded from the computation of the Academic Performance Score
(APS).
Q17 Can I submit or modify my CV after the deadline?
No. Once the submission deadline has passed, late submissions or modifications to the CV shall
not be permitted under any circumstances. Students must ensure all information is accurate and
complete prior to the deadline.
Q18 What format should my resume file name follow?
Resume files must follow the prescribed naming convention: Firstname_LASTNAME (e.g., Rohit
SHARMA).
Q19 Can a Dual Major student pursue internships in both their primary and secondary disciplines?
Yes, within specified limits. Dual Major students may pursue internships in their primary
discipline from their third year onwards. Eligibility for internships in their secondary discipline
commences from the beginning of the fourth year. However, they may pursue only one
Semester-Long industrial internship through CDS during their entire program, either in the 7th
semester (primary discipline) or in the 9th semester (primary or secondary discipline). Refer to
Section 6.3.2(c).
Q20 Am I required to inform CDS before applying independently for an off-campus academic
internship?
Yes. Before initiating any independent communication with an off-campus academic institution,
university, laboratory, or professor for an internship, you must formally inform CDS of the
proposed institutions through the process prescribed by CDS. CDS may approve, restrict, or
regulate such applications in the interest of institutional relationships and coordination. Noncompliance may attract disciplinary consequences.
