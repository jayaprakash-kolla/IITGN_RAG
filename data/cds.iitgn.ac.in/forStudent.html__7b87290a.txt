# For Students

Everything you need to discover your potential, plan your career and take the next big step.

 Career Development Services office at IIT Gandhinagar is committed to empower the students to discover their true potential and help them to pursue their dreams in all possible immediate career verticals including Entrepreneurship, Placement, Higher Study and Competitive Examinations. Students can avail CDS resources that can help them to explore and plan their career effectively. The office also conducts various sessions that help students in career counselling, resume preparation, screening tests, group discussion, interview process and personality development among others.
