# Why Recruit?

Talent shaped for a global, interdisciplinary world.

Globalization: 40% UG students gain global exposure via semester abroad, research internships, and summer programs. 80% faculty have international experience; 15% are visiting globally.

Foundation Programme: A 5-week immersive program focusing on creativity, leadership, communication, ethics, social awareness, and fitness.

Project Oriented Learning: Learning by doing approach with 20% humanities exposure along with life sciences, design, and innovation.

Choice & Flexibility: Dual majors, minors, honors, flexible curriculum, short courses, and hands-on spaces like Tinkerer Lab & Makers Space.

Diversity: Students across India & abroad with strong female representation and international participation.

Interdisciplinarity: Cross-domain research, collaborative publications, and multi-disciplinary PhD guidance.

Student Empowerment: Flexible curriculum, branch change options, leadership opportunities, and strong extracurricular culture.

Self-Governance: Active student participation in academics, campus life, and institutional governance.

Explorer Fellowship: 6-week summer program to explore India's cultural diversity across multiple states.
