Why IIT Gandhinagar

## A collaborative institute in a strong regional ecosystem

 The institute emphasizes interdisciplinary education and research, supports new faculty through relocation support, start-up research grants, professional development allowance, and matching grants for externally funded projects as per institute norms.

 Gandhinagar and nearby Ahmedabad offer strong infrastructure, academic and research institutions, industry presence, schools, and family-friendly urban amenities.

Inclusive search

 IIT Gandhinagar strongly encourages applications from qualified women and reserved category candidates.
