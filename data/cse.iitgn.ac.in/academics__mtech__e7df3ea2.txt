## Overview

 The IITGN MTech programs in CSE, AI, and ICDT combine coursework with a project or thesis to build independent thinking and R&D capability. Students have flexibility to shape their academic path, including research beyond a narrow disciplinary boundary or in collaboration with industry.

IITGN offers MTech degrees in Computer Science & Engineering, Artificial Intelligence, Integrated Circuit Design & Technology, and several other disciplines.

The CSE MTech focus areas listed by IITGN include Computer Systems, Theoretical Computer Science, and Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning.

The ICDT MTech supports training in integrated circuit design and technology for semiconductor and electronics-system careers.

Program requirements include coursework, thesis credits, the postgraduate orientation programme Aarohan, and institute-level academic performance requirements.

MTech graduates are prepared for leadership paths in R&D, teaching, and industry.
