## Climbing Wall

We also have a climbing wall for the adventurous athletes of the community. It is considered to be a very good exercise for developing overall physical fitness.

- Wall climbing is strictly prohibited in absence of the instructor or without the proper safety equipment. Breaching the rule will result in disciplinary action.
- Wall will be operational only for a limited time as per the availability of the instructor.
