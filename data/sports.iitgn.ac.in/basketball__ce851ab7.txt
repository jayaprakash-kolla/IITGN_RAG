## Basketball

“The strength of the team is each individual member. The strength of each member is the team.” – Phil Jackson

Basketball is one of the fastest sports. To play this sport at a high level, physical conditioning is of utmost importance. Teamwork and playing with strategy is key. The game of basketball has always been a big part of the IITGn community.

IIT Gandhinagar has 1 indoor basketball court with polyurethane flooring and a viewing gallery of 40 spectators. Halls are prepared with echo-proof walls for avoiding noise of basketball hitting the ground. Additionally, we also house 3 outdoor Basketball courts with sufficient floodlights.

- Timings will be 6 AM to 10 AM & 5 PM to 10 PM on weekdays and 6 AM to 10 PM on weekends.
- Only clean non – marking shoes ( preferably high ankles) are allowed.
- Proper sports attire (i.e. Tracksuit, shorts & t-shirts) is a must
- Spectators will be allowed only in the viewing gallery.
- Any heavy objects (like table,chair, etc.) should not be brought inside the hall
- Food and beverages will not be allowed inside the hall.
