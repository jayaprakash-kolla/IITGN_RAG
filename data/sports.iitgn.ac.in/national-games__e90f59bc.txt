# 36th National Games

# The 2022 National Games of India will be held in Gujarat from September 27 - October 10, 2022. The country’s most prestigious multi-discipline sporting event returns after a gap of seven years. The 36th edition of the Games will be held across six cities — Ahmedabad, Gandhinagar, Surat, Vadodara, Rajkot and Bhavnagar. An estimated 8,000 athletes will take part across 36 sporting disciplines. Here’s the schedule of the National Games 2022 held at IIT Gandhinagar campus: ATHLETICS September 30 - October 4 SQUASH October 1 - 5 SOFTBALL October 7 - 11 TRIATHLON October 9 & 11
