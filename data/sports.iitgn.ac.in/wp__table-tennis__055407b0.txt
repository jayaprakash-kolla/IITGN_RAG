## Table Tennis

“Talk with your racquet, play with your heart!”

Besides being a fun activity to spend healthy time with your family and friends, there are many health and other benefits of playing table tennis. Table Tennis is a retro and fast-paced competitive sport and is the world’s best brain sport.

It’s a sport that students can enjoy playing at any time as indoor sports with all the required equipment available on the campus. Besides professional table tennis players, other students also enjoy practicing it and remain active which definitely helps to improve the sports culture of IITGN. IITGN has got outstanding Table tennis players over the past years whose performances in Inter IIT and many other tournaments keep motivating succeeding teams. This game has taught us the importance of perseverance, control and besides this, the value of having a strong team with dedicated and motivating teammates.

IITGN Community is fortunate to have Table Tennis facility on campus and it has helped form a friendly bond between the community members, be it faculties or staff members or students. We have a TT hall with Polyurethane flooring and 5 tables. The hall is well lit and has a spectators area with a capacity of 40 people. Additionally, if required, the hall can still accommodate ore tables.
