## Lawn Tennis

“You only live once, but you get to serve twice”

Tennis is always known for its profound history and its royalty. The game that originated in 1873 has made a permanent place in the hearts of every generation. Tennis shapes your personality physically, mentally and emotionally.

When on the court the IIT Gandhinagar team feels like a family. The team strives hard for getting better at the game each day. Each drop of sweat dripped on the court builds our character. We definitely aim at winning but most importantly we are inclined towards witnessing moments worth remembering till eternity.
