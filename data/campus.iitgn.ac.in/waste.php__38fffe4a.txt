### Waste Management Guidelines

-  Segregation of waste is compulsory (for residents and visitors)

##### Green Waste

Output:Electricity and Manure

- Goes directly to the biogas plant
- No paper, plastic, wrappers, disposable cups, etc.

##### Red Waste

Output:Landfill

- Goes to landfill and harms the environment
- If unsure, put waste in this category

##### Blue Waste

Output:Recycled Products

- Sent to recycling centers
- No wet or contaminated paper/plastic
