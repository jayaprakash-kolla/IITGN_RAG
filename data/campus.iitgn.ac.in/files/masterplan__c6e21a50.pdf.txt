GREEN CAMPUS DEVELOPMENT CONSORTIUM

The built form of the entire campus is ‘low-rise’ with elevatorfree buildings. Buildings in each functional zone have been
closely spaced around ‘courts’ with human scale. Few high rise
apartment blocks, proposed along the main open spaces give
definition to these spaces and also make the ‘skyline’ interesting.
Majority low rise buildings with optimum open spaces will
ensure natural airflow within the site and within the buildings.
