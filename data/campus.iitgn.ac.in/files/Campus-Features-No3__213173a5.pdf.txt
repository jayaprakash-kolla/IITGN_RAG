Campus Features – Auditorium Furniture

IIT Gandhinagar

Date: 16-01-2017

A modern approach to the traditional, is the way to describe our approach to auditorium furniture. Designed to last decades, it is warm,
simple, elegant and is naturally left-hander friendly (due to symmetry). Each piece is crafted using CNC machines and laser cutting
machines using state-of-the-art facilities, adding a modern, technological touch to the furniture.
