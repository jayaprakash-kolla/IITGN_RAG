Experimental Furniture in AB-7/106
In AB-7/106, we are experimenting with
Chair-a-Mez, a chair table combination that
is flexible and lets users sit in different
postures, works for right and left handers
and allows flexible layouts. Please see
further information here about how this can
be used. Please do share your feedback by
writing to campus@iitgn.ac.in.

Central Vista
The Central Vista work is completed and
there have been 400 trees planted. The Jal
Mandap and other seating areas are open for
use. Some of the trees that are planted in
the Central Vista are Indian Tree of Heaven
(Ailanthus excels), Neem, Kassod (Siamese
Senna), Gulmohar, Banyan, Fig Tree, Mahua

(Madhuca longifolia), Jambu, Tamarind and
Badam.

Open Air Theatre
The detailed design for Open Air Theatre is completed. The construction activities
will start soon. It is planed to have a capacity of 2000, and will be integrated with
a garden, thus facilitating large events and evening walks alike.

Research Park & Incubation Center

Both the IITGN research park and the incubation center activities will soon move to
the recently completed Academic Block 9. In the meantime, a significantly larger
research park facilty is being developed just north of the scenic drive. The
architectural deisgn activities are being concluded and the construction activities
will start in the next few months.

Day Care Center
The IITGN Day Care Center was started in March 2014 as a community initiative to
provide a safe, secure, and nurturing environment to the children from IITGN families.
In addition to daily care and activities, the center from time to time undertakes several
special activities such as kids-supported agriculture, summer camp, festival

celebrations, spots camps, various art, craft, and activity-workshops, and other such
initiatives. This center has been steadily growing and currently engages more than 45
of our children in various such activities.
