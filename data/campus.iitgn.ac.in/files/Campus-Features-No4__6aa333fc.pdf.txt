Campus Features – The Banyan Tree

IIT Gandhinagar

Date: 23-01-2017

The final finishing touches to a
campus often comes from the
landscape and the landscape is
also the slowest to mature.
Take for example the Banyan
tree that is to be a centrepiece
of the academic area. While
growing steadily, it certainly
has several years before it can
exert its grandeur on the
campus environment. The
same with the 20+ trees
planted in the Samwad area, or
the 12 Neem trees in the
Vidhyachaya area. Imagine the
atmosphere in another decade.
Can you spot where the Banyan
tree is?
