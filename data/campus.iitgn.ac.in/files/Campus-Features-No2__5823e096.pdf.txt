Campus Features – The Space Frame

IIT Gandhinagar

Date: 09-01-2017

The space frame over the
Samvad area frames a central
congregation space. In addition
to creating an aspirational
gesture,
it
also
draws
inspiration from the tradition
in Gujarat of flying kites during
Uttarayan (Makar Sankranti).
The pattern is an interpretation
of kites of different colours
dominating the sky.
