Campus Updates
Announcements and happenings on campus

Construction Updates
1) Entrance Gate Complex: Work has started at the Basan Gate & the Main Gate at
the scenic drive. The entrance gates consist of security offices and public
washrooms. The housing gate (that is currently being used as the main gate) will be
taken up once the other two gates are ready.
2) Boundary Wall: Foundation and concrete work are in progress, you might want
to be on the lookout for red exposed brickwork and grills that have already started
coming up in some portions of the wall.
3) Parking near Housing Block 01 and 30: A new parking facility is being created
next to housing blocks 30 and 1.
4) Guest House: The foundation work is in progress. The guest house will have 70
rooms, 12 long-stay suites and several additional facilities like a 100 person
capacity conference hall, multiple meeting rooms, and dining facilities.
5) Sports Complex & Central Arcade: The work for the sports complex, sports
grounds and central arcade has started, expect to see barricading work over the
next few days. The sports complex is expected to be one of the unique facilities on
campus and will include an olympic-size swimming pool, an indoor gym, squash

courts, badminton courts, table tennis tables, indoor basketball and volleyball
courts, a climbing wall, and outdoor football and cricket grounds along with a
synthetic athletics track and a jogging track. It is also worth noting that almost all
the full-grown trees on this site (except one) are being saved and some of them
come within the buildings and within fields, creating a unique sports facility.

Work Space for All
AB-06/407 has been furnished as a workspace with working tables and chairs. All are
welcome to use this space for studying,
working, collaborative discussions etc. Note
that none of the seats are assigned to
anybody and are available on a hot seat
basis. This facility is now open for use.

Housing for Married Students
About 100+ studio apartments with a compact living area, kitchenette, sleeping
area, and a small balcony are being planned and will surely come as a relief to
many married students. Along with the studio apartments, a handful of compact
1BHK and 2BHK are also being planned for various other needs and will come up
over the next couple of years across from the housing lake (on the Basan side).

Water Bodies on Campus
As you may have seen, both the lakes on
campus have recently been cleaned up and
are in the process of being tested. These are
great locations for morning and evening
strolls, especially with numerous birds and
wildlife that are attracted to these water
bodies.

Construction Workers' Housing
Construction workers housing for about 120
labourers for the Guest House project were
inaugurated on 23rd January. These are
'pukka' houses with clean drinking water and
dignified sanitation facilities.
