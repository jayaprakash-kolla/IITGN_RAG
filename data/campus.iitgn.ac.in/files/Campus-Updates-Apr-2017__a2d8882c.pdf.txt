Campus Updates
Announcements and happenings related to IITGN campus

Boundary Wall and Entrance
Gates
The construction work of the
boundary wall and the entrance
gates have been awarded by
CPWD and is likely to start in the
next few days. The boundary wall
is designed with a significant
amount of transparency and rhythmic patterns to allow outsiders a sense of the
campus from outside instead of a long monotonous wall.

New Hostels
The detailed designs for next set of 6 new hostels are nearing completion. The
construction activities will start soon. It is therefore time to start thinking of
names for the next set of 6 hostels. So please send your suggestions of names for
the new hostels following the philosophy of our previous hostel names
to campus@iitgn.ac.in.

Central Vista
The Central Vista work is in progress and the
plantation work will start soon. In the coming
monsoon we can expect extensive tree plantation
in the Central Vista region. Some of the trees
that will be planted in the Central Vista are Indian
Tree of Heaven (Ailanthus excels), Neem, Kassod
(Siamese Senna), Gulmohar, Banyan, Fig Tree, Mahua (Madhuca longifolia), Jambu,
Tamarind, Badam and Sandalwood.

Displaying Research Posters

Wouldn't it be good if our research more visible when
you are walking around the academic area?. With this

posters. These along with locations that are to be

Solar Carport
The Solar Carport work along with the shaded
walkaway is going on in full swing and is
expected to be completed in the coming
weeks.

River Promenade
The River Promenade along the western boundary
of the campus is one of the main conceptual
anchors of the proposed open space system. The
work is in progress behind Academic Buildings 9, 4
and 3, and will likely be completed in the next
several weeks. It is designed as a broad
landscaped walkway for movement and informal activity along the river.
