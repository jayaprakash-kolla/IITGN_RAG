Campus Features – Scenic Drive

IIT Gandhinagar

Date: 06-02-2017

The campus has a number
of
interesting
natural
features including the
river, deep ravines, a
couple of lakes and neem
tree plantations. The idea
behind
the
formal
entrance, also referred to
as the scenic drive, is that a
visitor should experience
the natural features of the
campus including the four
mentioned above and then
dramatically come upon
the main academic area.

While the scenic drive is
not yet fully commissioned,
it is a nice place for a
morning or evening walk
and is a must see on
campus.
