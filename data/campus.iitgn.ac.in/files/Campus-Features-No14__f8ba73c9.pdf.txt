Campus Features – Biogas Plant

IIT Gandhinagar

Date: 17-04-2017

As part of IITGN
waste
management initiatives, and to
minimize waste going to landfills,
the food & organic waste from all
across campus space(green bins)
go to the IITGN biogas plant. This
biogas
plant established in
collaboration with BARC generates
manure and electricity from the
food and organic waste with no
other by products. It is designedto
help save 1 tonne of waste from
going to the landfills each day and
in turn generate manure and
electricity from them.
