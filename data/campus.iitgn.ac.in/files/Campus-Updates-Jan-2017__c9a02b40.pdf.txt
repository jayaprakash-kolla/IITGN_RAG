Gutka‐free Campus
Please help make this campus a Gutka‐free and Pan‐free campus. We are ramping
up efforts towards achieving this goal, meanwhile if you spot any such incidents,
please feel free to warn the person responsible or warn the nearest security guard.

No Signs on Walls & Poles
Absolutely no posting of signs, bills, and announcements
on walls, poles or pillars, please. You may consider using
the new movable signage boards if they are available.

Coffee Machines
