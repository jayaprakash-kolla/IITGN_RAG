Campus Features – Jasubhai Memorial Auditorium

IIT Gandhinagar

Date: 13-02-2017

On 10th February, 2017 the 500 capacity Auditorium was named the Jasubhai Memorial Auditorium in memory of Mr Jasubhai Shah,
Founder President of Chemtech Foundation and the Jasubhai Group, a visionary leader with a passion for excellence in the areas of
technological innovation in science and industry. Mr. Jasubhai Shah’s son, Mr. Maulik Jasubhai has helped establish an endowment at IITGN
to support educational and research excellence at the Institute.
