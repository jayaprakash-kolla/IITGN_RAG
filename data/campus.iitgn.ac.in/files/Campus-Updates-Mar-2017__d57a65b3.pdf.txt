Campus Updates
Announcements and happenings related to IITGN campus

AESA Award 2017
The Academic Complex of our campus recently bagged the 'AESA Award 2017:
Outside Pune District'. AESA is a professional organisation of Architects Engineers
and Surveyors and has a long history in Pune.

Organic Farm
The organic farm started by a volunteer
group is growing steadily (quite literally).
In the last few weeks the farm has grown
and harvested potatoes, tomatoes,
spinach, beetroot, broccoli, capsicum,
carrots, pumpkins, cauliflowers, cabbage,
brinjals, among other things. The farm provides plenty of opportunities to
volunteer and engage including opportunities for students.

Sports Complex
An indoor sports facility and sports grounds
are being developed and are expected to
be completed in next 2 years. The sports
facility is currently planned to have an
olympic‐size swimming pool and a smaller
amateur pool, a table‐tennis hall, 3 squash
courts, 6 badminton courts, an indoor basketball court, an indoor volleyball court,
a gym and a yoga hall. These are in addition to external courts and grounds that
are being developed. This entire complex is being developed around four existing
trees on the site.

Solar Carport
A Solar Carport with a partially covered walkaway is being developed in the stretch
between the Hostels and Academic Area (in front of Lal Minar). The shaded
walkaway will be a relief to all of us walking between the Student Hostels and the
Academic Area in the Summer. The roof of the parking structure and walkaway will
consist of Solar PV panels. The work is expected to be completed in the next
several weeks.

No Signs on Walls & Poles
A gentle reminder not to post any signs, bills, or
announcements on walls, poles or pillars. All Community
members are requested to follow this strictly and be vigilant
and take appropriate action if you see others posting signs,
regardless of whether it is for student events, faculty events
or other institute events.

Smoking Zones?
We are working towards creating and enforcing smoking zones in the Academic
Area. Please share with us any suggestions in this regard at campus@iitgn.ac.in.
