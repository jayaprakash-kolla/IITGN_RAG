Campus Features – Vishwangan

IIT Gandhinagar

Date: 03-04-2017

The courtyard under the Lal Minar is Vishwangan and situated at one end
of the academic spine closest to the hostels. The three mysterious
symbols on pillars in Vishwangan that you may have wondered about
represent intellect (Pradnya), creativity (Pratibha) and inspiration
(Prerana).
