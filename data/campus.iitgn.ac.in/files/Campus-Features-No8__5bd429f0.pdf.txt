Campus Features – Lal Minar

IIT Gandhinagar

Date: 27-02-2017

Many of you might have glanced
at the face of Lal Minar and
wondered what the pattern is all
about. After much debate about
whether it should be IITGN logo
or a national symbol, the design
team came up with a suggestion
for an abstract design loosely
inspired from what many
believe to be an ancient sundial
in India that can read the time to
an accuracy of a minute. Did you
know that the intricate design
thus created and embossed in
concrete is quite possibly the
largest concrete embossing in
India and that too at that height?
