# Electrical Engineering

- Careers
Electrical Engineering

The department of Electrical Engineering at IITGN was started in 2009 with undergraduate and Ph.D. programs which was expanded in 2012 to include M.Tech. program in Electrical Engineering. The department currently hosts research activities which can be broadly classified into:

- Microelectronics & VLSI
- Power Electronics, Control & Power Systems
- Photonics
- Signal Processing & Computer Vision
- Biomedical Engineering
- Intelligent Human Computer Interaction
The research facilities, which are continously being upgraded, currently include:

- 3D Laser Scanner
- VLSI Tools:
 • Cadence design suite
 • Mentor Graphics (Calibre, Questa, etc)
 • High-end FPGA Boards
 • Conventorware for MEMS design
 • TCAD simulation software
 • MATLAB
- Device characterization facility (wafer probe station + B1500)
- Ear protector test module
- Realtime digital simulation test bed
In order to meet current and future research and teaching requirements, the department of Electrical Engineering at IIT Gandhinagar seeks outstanding applicants from all areas of Electrical Engineering to fill multiple positions at all levels.
