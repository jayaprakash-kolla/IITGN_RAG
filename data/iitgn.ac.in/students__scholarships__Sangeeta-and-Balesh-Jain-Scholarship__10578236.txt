## Sangeeta and Balesh Jain Scholarship

 Sangeeta and Balesh Jain Scholarship has been instituted in the year 2024 by Prof Sudhir K Jain, and is open to all UG (BTech) students. The scholarship amount is Rs. 1 lakh per student per year and is awarded to one UG (BTech) student every year in order to support internships (international or domestic), special projects and opportunities, financial needs, etc. UG (BTech) students with a minimum CPI of 6.5 are eligible.

 Prof. Sudhir K Jain was the founding Director of IIT Gandhinagar. He has set up this scholarship in honour of his sister, who is a home maker, and brother-in-law who is a corporate leader.
