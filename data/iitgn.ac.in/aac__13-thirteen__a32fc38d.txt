# Thirteen Academic Advisory Council

##### Academic Advisory Council Meetings

##### The Academic Advisory Council comprises of eminent leaders from academia and other allied domains, and meets every year to discuss the academic priorities of IIT Gandhinagar. The Academic Advisory Council provides strategic external inputs on issues of key importance as the Institute grows in academics and other spheres. On January 09, 2026, the thirteenth Council meeting will discuss Curriculum Adaptation for Emerging and Strategic Technologies; Enhancing Institute Research Productivity; Roadmap for Internationalization; and Sustaining Growth While Preserving Culture.
