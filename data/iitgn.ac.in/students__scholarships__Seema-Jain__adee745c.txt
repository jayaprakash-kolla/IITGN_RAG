## Seema Jain Scholarship

 Seema Jain Scholarship was instituted by Prof. Sudhir K Jain in the year 2019 and is open to all the B.Tech students. The scholarship amount is Rs. 1 lakh per student per year and is awarded every year to support internships (international or domestic), special projects and opportunities, financial needs, etc. B.Tech students with a minimum CPI of 6.5 are eligible.

 Prof. Sudhir K Jain was the founding Director of IIT Gandhinagar. He has set up this scholarship in the honour of his late sister Seema Jain, who as an alumna of IIT Roorkee.
