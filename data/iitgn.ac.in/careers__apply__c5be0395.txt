#### Register in Online Application Portal

 Please click on the “Apply Now” button below to access our online portal. New users will have to first register using a valid email address.

2

#### Submit Application

 Once registered, please login and fill out the required information. You will also have to upload the following as attachments:

Detailed curriculum vitae (single PDF file, 2 MB limit)
 Scanned transcripts/grade cards for all course work performed during undergraduate and postgraduate degree programs (single PDF file, 10 MB limit)
 Scanned degree certificates for all degrees received (single PDF file, 10 MB limit)
 Certificate of SC/ST/OBC/PwD, if applicable (single PDF file, 1 MB limit)
 Reprints of 3 best publications (single PDF file, 15 MB limit)
 A clear, JPEG image of yourself.
