## Massimo Vidale

Scholar-in-Residence, Humanities and Social Sciences

- MA: University of Padua, Faculty of Philosophy and Literature, 1980
- PhD: Istituto Universitario Orientale (IUO), Naples, 1987

Prof. Vidale has authored about 15 books, and 5 edited volumes, in English, Italian and other languages on ancient oriental civilizations. He has contributed to dozens of voices in scientific encyclopaedias and authored or co-authored about 350 articles on specialized archaeological journals, including major refereed journals. Among these latter he has published the results his research in Nature, Science, Antiquity, World Archaeology, Man and Environment, South Asian Studies, Paléorient, Iran, Iranica Antiqua, Archäologische Mitteilungen aus Iran und Turan, Praeistorische Zeitschrift. He is also deeply involved in publicizing the most recent results of archaeological research on popular archaeological jourbnals and magazines. He can provide a complete list of his publications on request.

- Associate Professor of Near Eastern Arcaheology and Archaeology of Craft Production Processes at the Dept. of Cultural Heritage (Present)
- Director of the LASERT (Laboratory of experimental archaeology), Department of Cultural Heritage, University of Padova (Present)
- Director of the School of Specialization in Archaeological Sciences, Department of Cultural Heritage, University of Padova (Present)

- ● Study of ancient craft technologies and their relevance to theunderstanding of early state formation processes
