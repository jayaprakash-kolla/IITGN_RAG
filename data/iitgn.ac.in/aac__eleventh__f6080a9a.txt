# Eleventh Academic Advisory Council

##### Academic Advisory Council Meetings

##### The Academic Advisory Council comprises of eminent leaders from academia and other allied domains, and meets every year to discuss the academic priorities of IIT Gandhinagar. The Academic Advisory Council provides strategic external inputs on issues of key importance as the Institute grows in academics and other spheres. On January 8, 2024, the eleventh Council meeting will discuss faculty evaluation and growth, fostering industry-academia partnership, holistic staff development, promoting student well-being, and new academic programmes.
