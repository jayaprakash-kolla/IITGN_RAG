## Prof. Nithin V. George

Prof. Nithin V. George is Professor of Electrical Engineering at IIT Gandhinagar. His group develops adaptive signal processing and machine learning algorithms for audio applications, with particular emphasis on active noise cancellation, microphone array systems, hearing aids, and hearables.

 He completed his BTech at the University of Kerala (2007), MTech at NIT Rourkela (2009), and PhD at IIT Bhubaneswar (2012). His early training included a stint as a Visiting Graduate Student at the University of Western Ontario, Canada (2008–09) under the DFAIT GSEP Fellowship, and postdoctoral work at IIT Bhubaneswar before he joined IIT Gandhinagar as Assistant Professor in November 2012. He was later a Visiting Research Fellow at Curtin University, Australia (2017), rose to Associate Professor in 2018, and was promoted to Professor in November 2024. He has held the TEOCO Chair at IIT Gandhinagar since 2018.

 His lab is equipped with a dedicated active noise control test bench, an extensive array of measurement and array microphones, noise-cancelling headphones and real-time audio controllers. He holds two Indian patents on acoustic feedback cancellation systems for assistive listening devices.

 Prof. George's contributions have been recognized with the Excellence in Research Award from IIT Gandhinagar (2022–23), the Excellence in Institution Building Award from IIT Gandhinagar (2017–18), the INSPIRE Faculty Award (2013–18), and the Indo-Australia Early and Mid-Career Researchers Fellowship (2016–17).

Research Interests: Active Noise Control · Acoustic Beamforming · Adaptive Signal Processing · Array Signal Processing · Assistive Listening Devices
