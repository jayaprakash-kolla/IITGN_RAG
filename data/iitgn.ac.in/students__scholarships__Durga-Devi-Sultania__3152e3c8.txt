## Durga Devi Sultania Scholarship

 Durga Devi Sultania Scholarship was instituted by Mr. Deepak Sultania in the year 2016 and is open to all B.Tech students. The scholarship amount is Rs. 1 lakh each and is awarded to one B.Tech student every year to support internships (international or domestic), special projects and opportunities, financial needs, etc. B.Tech students with a minimum CPI of 6.5 are eligible.

 Mr Deepak Sultania is the Director of Durga Bearings group, the largest distributor of Bearings in India. Starting at an early age in business, he has a rich and varied experience of 37 years in the bearing trade. With his great business acumen and modern management practice he has turned his company from a small trading company to become a market leader, distributing products of multinational bearing brands all over India. Mr Deepak has also been awarded the Samaj Ratna award for his philanthropic work in Mumbai, Rajasthan. He is father of an alumnus of IIT Gandhinagar and has set up these scholarships to honour his father Shri Mahabir Prasad Sultania (the founder of Durga Enterprises) and his mother Smt. Durga Devi Sultania.
