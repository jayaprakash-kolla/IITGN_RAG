# Civil Engineering

- Careers
Civil Engineering

The department of Civil Engineering at Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Gandhinagar is rapidly growing and has excellent research, teaching, and computational facilities. The department aims to make an impact at national and global scales through its research, teaching, and outreach activities. Applications are invited for faculty positions at all levels (Assistant, Associate, and Full Professor) from exceptional candidates who have demonstrated high-quality research and teaching and can create lasting societal impacts. The applicants can combine experimental, modeling, data-driven and other approaches rigorously with the domain-specific knowledge related to Civil Engineering. The applications will be considered in all the relevant areas including but not limited to:

- Transportation Engineering
- Geotechnical Engineering
- Water Resources Engineering
- Structural Engineering
- Geospatial Engineering (Geographical Information Systems (GIS), Remote Sensing, and Geomatics)
- Construction Engineering and Management
- Environmental Engineering
Candidates with a Ph.D. degree or who are in their advanced stages of Ph.D. program can apply electronically at https://www.iitgn.ac.in/faculty-recruitment.htm
