Guest Professor at Indian Institute of Technology Gandhinagar (Present)

Since 1986 I have been training Class X students for National Talent Search Examination (NTSE) conducted by NCERT. A total of 1178 students have been awarded this prestigious scholarship.

Resource person at IndSearch (affiliated to University of Pune) to teach World History and Science and Technology for ICWA/CA entrance examination foundation course from 1985 to 1995.

Resource person at Jnana Prabodhini's Competitive Examination Centre from 1986 to 2006. Teaching World History, Indian History, Civics and Science and Technology for UPSC and MPSC candidates.

Resource person at the National Institute of Construction Management and Research (NICMAR) teaching research methodology an quantitative methods from 1988 to 1995.

Resource person for M. Arch. (University of Pune) teaching Introduction to Archaeology from 2008.

Resource person for B. Journalism. (University of Pune) teaching World History from 2007-2010.

Resource person for M.A. (Tilak Maharashtra University, Pune) teaching Introduction to Archaeology, Science in Archaeology, Political History of India and Social-economic History of India from 2004 to 2011.

Teaching as a permanent faculty member at Deccan College following courses from 1995 to 2020.
