## Amalthea Scholarship

 The Amalthea Scholarships were instituted in the year 2016 and are open to all B.Tech students. The scholarship amount is upto Rs. 1 lakh per student per year to support internships (international or domestic), special projects and opportunities, financial needs, etc. B.Tech students with a minimum CPI of 6.5 are eligible.
