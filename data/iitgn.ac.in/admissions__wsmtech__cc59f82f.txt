# Work and Study MTech

 IIT Gandhinagar invites applications from highly motivated students for admission to its Work and Study MTech programme for the academic year 2026-27.

Work and Study MTech programme will be offered by the departments of

- ❑ Biological Engineering
- ❑ Chemical Engineering
- ❑ Civil Engineering
- ❑ Computer Science and Engineering
- ❑ Artificial Intelligence
- ❑ Earth Sciences
- ❑ Electrical Engineering
- ❑ Integrated Circuit Design and Technology
- ❑ Mechanical Engineering
- ❑ Materials Engineering
- ❑ Maritime Engineering

This programme will provide the students a unique opportunity to engage with sophisticated equipment, laboratories, and research facilities while pursuing MTech at IITGN.

The duration for this program will be three years.

MTech (Work and Study) is a full-time M.Tech. programme. It is not a part-time or distance-learning programme. Students admitted to this programme are required to pursue both the academic (study) and work components full-time at IIT Gandhinagar for the entire duration of the programme.

## Eligibility Criteria

 The minimum eligibility criteria for admission into the Work and Study MTech program is same as Regular MTech. The disciplines, however, may use additional criteria to shortlist the best candidates, which may be different from that for regular MTech program.

## Financial Support

Eligible candidates admitted to Work and Study MTech programme will receive a monthly scholarship of Rs. 18,000/pm.

 The normal duration of this course will be for three years.

## Admission Procedure

Admission is generally offered on the basis of an interview and a written test (if any). The Institute will invite a limited number of candidates for a written test (if any) and interview based on the academic records and/or GATE score. The Institute reserves the right to use its own judgment while determining the candidate's level of competency based on the available information.

 The written test (if any) is generally based on the topics related to general aptitude (logical reasoning, analytical and numerical ability, etc.), engineering mathematics, and basic courses in various disciplines up to 3rd year of undergraduate programme. The candidates who do not qualify in the written test need not appear for the interview. The reserved category candidates will be given due relaxation in cutoff marks as per the norms.

 The interview and the written test (if any) may be conducted via online platforms or in offline mode.

## Application Procedure

All applications must be submitted online. If you are applying for more than one discipline, please submit a separate application for each discipline. After successful submission of the application form, you will receive an application number and a link to download the application form.

Please save the application form. You need to furnish hardcopy of the application form and self-attested copies of all the relevant documents at the time of interview. Incomplete applications will be rejected.
