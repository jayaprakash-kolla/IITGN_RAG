# Archaeological Sciences Centre

- Careers
Archaeological Sciences Centre

In December 2012, IIT Gandhinagar (IITGN) set up an Archaeological Sciences Centre to promote and facilitate multidisciplinary research in archaeology and allied scientific disciplines. Several research projects are currently underway (https://asc.iitgn.ac.in/research.php).

IITGN invites applications from highly talented candidates for faculty positions in archaeological sciences, particularly in:

Computational Archaeology

Archaeological Chemistry

Archaeobotany/Geoarchaeology/Environmental Archaeology

For queries or information regarding faculty recruitment, specifically in archaeology at IITGN, please contact Prof. Sharada Channarayapatna ([email protected]). For general questions related to faculty recruitment at IITGN, please write to [email protected].

To apply to any of the above positions, or for further information, visit us at https://iitgn.ac.in/careers/faculty
