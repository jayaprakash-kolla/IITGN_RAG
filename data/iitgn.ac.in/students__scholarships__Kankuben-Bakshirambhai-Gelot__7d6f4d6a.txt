## Kankuben Bakshirambhai Gelot Scholarship

 Shri Gordhanbhai B Gelot, has a post graduate degree in Industrial Microbiology from Gujarat University. He has been running a Small Scale Industrial (SSI) unit for ready-to-use clinical microbial analytical tools at GIDC Industrial Estate in Umargam for the last 44 years. Prior to setting up the SSI, Shri Gordhanbhai B Gelot has also worked with American and French pharma multinational corporations. ‘Kankuben Bakshirambhai Gelot Scholarship’ is set up by him in the honor of his late mother, Smt Kankuben Bakshirambhai Gelot. The scholarship of Rs. 1 Lakh per year would support the female undergraduate student studying in any area of engineering and natural sciences at IITGN for their financial needs, funding internships (international or domestic), or special projects and opportunities, etc.
