## P P Sukumaran Scholarship

 The P P Sukumaran Scholarship is an endowed scholarship established by Jithin Prabha (BTech/ME/2016) in honor of his late father, Shri P P Sukumaran. The scholarship amount is Rs. 1 lakh per year and is awarded to one B.Tech student with a minimum CPI of 6.5.

 After losing his father at the age of fourteen and with no safety net of intergenerational wealth, Shri P P Sukumaran rose from hardship with perseverance, discipline, and an unshakable belief in education. Through quiet determination, he built a purposeful life and gave back generously to his community. Remembered for his boundless curiosity and convictions, he left an indelible mark through actions that spoke louder than words.

 Jithin Prabha graduated in 2016 from IIT Gandhinagar with a BTech degree in Mechanical Engineering. After graduation, he did his Master's at Purdue University and is currently working as a mechanical engineer at Lam Research, San Francisco.

 "To the students who carry big dreams on tired shoulders, who stay up late studying while the world sleeps, who believe in compassion, action, and the long road - this scholarship is for you. For there was once a boy, broken by loss at fourteen, who faced all odds with empty hands but a mind full of promise. He turned grief into grit, struggle into strength, and grew up to give quietly, think deeply, and love fiercely. Your paths may be hard, but you are not alone. Let this scholarship be a token of support in your journey." - Jithin Prabha
