## Life in Ahmedabad/Gandhinagar

 Gandhinagar is in proximity to Ahmedabad which is one of the oldest living cities in India. Both cities are known for state-of-the-art infrastructure, thriving industries, and many modern amenities.

#### Education Hub

 This region is a hub of prestigious academic and research institutes including the Indian Institute of Management, Ahmedabad, National Institute of Design, Physical Research Laboratory, Institute for Plasma Research and ISRO’s Space Applications Center, among several others.

#### Entrepreneurship Capital

 Gujarat’s long-standing mercantile and business tradition also nurtures entrepreneurship and business. The vibrant economy of the region with petrochemical and pharmaceutical companies, numerous manufacturing units, and dedicated finance and service industry zones such as GIFT city and Info-city, offers almost unlimited opportunities for spouses to find employment.

#### Infrastructure

 Gandhinagar is one of the few planned cities of modern India offering tier I city infrastructure but not the hustle and bustle of typical metro city. The excellent infrastructure, cultural richness, low cost of living and good schools in the region make it an ideal destination to support a family.
