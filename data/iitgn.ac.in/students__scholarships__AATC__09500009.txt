## Anjna and Anil Tara Chandra Scholarship

 Anjna and Anil Tara Chandra Scholarship has been instituted in the year 2020 and is open to all postgraduate students. The scholarship amount is Rs. 1 lakh per student per year and is awarded to one PG student every year in order to support internships (international or domestic), special projects and opportunities, financial needs, etc. PG students with a minimum CPI of 6.5 are eligible.
