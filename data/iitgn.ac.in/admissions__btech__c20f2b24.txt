 IIT Gandhinagar offers 4-year B.Tech. programme in eight Engineering disciplines (Artificial Intelligence, Chemical, Civil, Computer Science & Engineering, Electrical, Integrated Circuit Design and Technology, Mechanical and Materials Engineering). The admission to the course is through the IIT Joint Entrance Examination (JEE). For more information about JEE and total number of available seats at IITGN, please visit the page http://jeeadv.ac.in

Important Notification:
 Prospective BTech students who have been allotted admission to IIT Gandhinagar for the Academic Session 2026–27 through JoSAA 2026 are required to complete their on-campus registration on July 20, 2026 (Monday), from 10:00 AM (IST) onwards.

 Students are therefore advised to arrive on campus on or before 10:00 AM (IST) on July 20, 2026, to ensure timely completion of the physical registration and document verification process.

 Hostel accommodation for students will be available from July 18, 2026.
