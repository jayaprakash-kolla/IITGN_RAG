## Dr. Vilas Mujumdar Chair

 IIT Gandhinagar has setup this faculty chair through a donation by Dr. Vilas Mujumdar.

Dr. Vilas Mujumdar Chair at IIT Gandhinagar will support faculty members in all areas of engineering or natural sciences.

## Prof. Shanmuganathan Raman

Prof. Shanmuganathan Raman is a Professor of Electrical Engineering at IIT Gandhinagar, with a joint appointment as Professor of Computer Science and Engineering, where he currently serves as Head of the Department. He leads the Computer Vision, Imaging, and Graphics (CVIG) Lab, where his research spans computer vision, computational imaging, computer graphics, and deep learning.

 He received his B.E. in Electronics and Communication Engineering from PSG College of Technology in 2003, his M.Tech in Biomedical Engineering from IIT Bombay in 2006, and his Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering from IIT Bombay in 2011. His doctoral work, supervised by Prof. Subhasis Chaudhuri, developed low-dynamic-range solutions to the high-dynamic-range imaging problem and was recognized with the IIT Bombay Excellence in Ph.D. Thesis Work Award at the institute's 50th Convocation. He was awarded a Microsoft Research India Ph.D. Fellowship during his doctoral studies.

 Following a postdoctoral position at IISc Bangalore and a brief faculty appointment at IIT Jodhpur, he joined IIT Gandhinagar as an Assistant Professor in 2013. He was promoted to Associate Professor in 2018 and to Professor in 2024, and took charge as Head of Computer Science and Engineering in September 2025. He has previously held the Jibaben Patel Chair in Artificial Intelligence and received the Excellence in Research Award, both at IIT Gandhinagar. He also serves as an Independent Director on the boards of Gujarat Industrial Investments Corporation (GIIC) and Madhya Gujarat Vij Company Limited (MGVCL).

 Over 13 years at IIT Gandhinagar, he has supervised 10 Ph.D. scholars and taught a wide range of undergraduate and postgraduate courses in computer science, artificial intelligence, and electrical engineering. Work from his group appears regularly at premier venues in computer vision, graphics, and signal processing, on topics ranging from text-to-video synthesis and neural rendering to inverse scattering and computational imaging.

Research Interests: Computer Vision · Image & Video Processing · Computer Graphics · Deep Learning · Artificial Intelligence
