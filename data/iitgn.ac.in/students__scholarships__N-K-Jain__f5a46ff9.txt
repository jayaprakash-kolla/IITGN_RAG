## N K Jain Scholarship

 N K Jain Scholarship was instituted by Prof. Sudhir K Jain in the year 2019 and is open to all the B.Tech students. The scholarship amount is Rs. 1 lakh per student per year and is awarded every year to support internships (international or domestic), special projects and opportunities, financial needs, etc. B.Tech students with a minimum CPI of 6.5 are eligible.

 Prof. Sudhir K Jain was the founding Director of IIT Gandhinagar. He set up this Scholarship in the honour of his late father Sri NK Jain, who was an alumnus of University of Roorkee (now IIT Roorkee).
