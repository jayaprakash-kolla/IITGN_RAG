Computer Science and Engineering

The department of Computer Science and Engineering at IITGN was started in 2011 with a Ph.D. program. The department currently hosts research activities which can be broadly classified into:

- Algorithms
- Computational complexity
- Cryptography
- Data mining
- Machine learning
