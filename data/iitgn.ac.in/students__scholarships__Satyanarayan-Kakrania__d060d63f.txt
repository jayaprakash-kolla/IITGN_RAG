## Shri Satyanarayan Kakrania Scholarship

 Shri Satyanarayan Kakrania Scholarship has been instituted in the year 2021 and is open to all B.Tech students. The scholarship amount is Rs. 1 lakh per student per year and is awarded to one B.Tech student every year in order to support internships (international or domestic), special projects and opportunities, financial needs, etc. B.Tech students with a minimum CPI of 6.5 are eligible.

 Rishi Bubna is a B. Tech alumnus of 2017 batch with a major in Mechanical Engineering. After graduating from IITGN, he pursued his Master’s degree at Georgia Institute of Technology, USA, and is currently working as a Data Scientist at Amazon, USA. This scholarship is set up by him in honor of his maternal grandfather, Shri Satyanarayan Kakrania. The scholarship of Rs. 1 Lakh per year would support the undergraduate students for their financial needs, funding internships (international or domestic), or special projects and opportunities, etc.
