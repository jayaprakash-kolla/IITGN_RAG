## The Gratitude Grant Scholarship

 The Gratitude Grant Scholarship has been established by a collective group of IIT Gandhinagar alumni and well-wishers who are currently part of the Google community. The scholarship amount is Rs. 1 lakh per year and is awarded to one B.Tech student with a minimum CPI of 6.5. This initiative stems from a deep sense of appreciation for the institution and a genuine desire to give back to the ecosystem that shaped their growth.

 Team Message: "The Gratitude Grant Scholarship has been established by a collective group of IIT Gandhinagar alumni and supporters who are currently part of the Google community. This initiative stems from a deep sense of appreciation for the institution and a desire to give back to the ecosystem that played a pivotal role in their professional and personal growth."
