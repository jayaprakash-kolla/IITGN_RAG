# Naming Opportunities at IIT Gandhinagar

##### Back to Giving

A world-class university requires: concentration of talent, adequate resources, and favorable governance. The Institute supplements the Government of India grants with substantial philanthropic contributions to recruit and support outstanding faculty, PhD students and postdoctoral fellows while providing them with state-of-the-art facilities. Following are a few opportunities to become part of this journey towards excellence by naming scholarships, faculty chairs, infrastructure projects etc.

#### Programmes With Naming Opportunities

| Naming of academic entities and infrastructure | The Institute has an active program to name select academic entities (such as research centres and laboratories) and infrastructure (such as buildings, hostels, and classrooms) to honour major donors.
