## Shri Anandilal Bubna Scholarship

 Shri Anandilal Bubna Scholarship has been instituted in the year 2021 and is open to all B.Tech students. The scholarship amount is Rs. 1 lakh per student per year and is awarded to one B.Tech student every year in order to support internships (international or domestic), special projects and opportunities, financial needs, etc. B.Tech students with a minimum CPI of 6.5 are eligible.

 Ms Ngan Le is a friend of Mr. Rishi Bubna (B.Tech/ME/2017) and is setting up this scholarship in the honor of her friend’s (Rishi Bubna) paternal grandfather, Shri Anandilal Bubna.
