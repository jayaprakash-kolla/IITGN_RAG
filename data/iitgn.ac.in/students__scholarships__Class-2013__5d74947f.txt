## Class of 2013 Scholarship

 The Class-of-2013 Scholarships have been instituted by the Class of 2013 IIT Gandhinagar students and are open to all B.Tech students. The scholarship amount is upto Rs. 1 lakh per student per year to support internships (international or domestic), special projects and opportunities, financial needs, etc. B.Tech students with a minimum CPI of 6.5 are eligible.
