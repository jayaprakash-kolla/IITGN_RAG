THE IITGN HONOR CODE
We, the students of IIT
Gandhinagar, affirm that we
will uphold the highest principles
of honesty and integrity in all
our endeavors and foster an
atmosphere of mutual respect.
We will not take undue
advantage of another individual
of the IITGN community.
We will be personally and
academically honest and
treat others with respect and
courtesy. We will neither give
nor receive unauthorized aid
explicitly or implicitly for any
academic activities. We will not
only pledge our honesty to the
Institute and its community, but
we will also be responsible and
speak out against any unjust
practices among us.

ACADEMIC SUPPORT

FOR STUDENTS @IITGN
IIT Gandhinagar (IITGN) has created an environment that
enables each of you to aspire, work hard and excel in
academics. Your academic performance is influenced by a
variety of factors and the institute has created numerous
resources for you to avail of. The initiatives outlined below
are yours to use irrespective of your background.

Student Support Service (SSS)
The IIT Gandhinagar Student Support Service is driven
by a dedicated team of faculty members who offer
assistance to students with a broad range of concerns.
We look forward to working with you directly, and we
will either help connect you with the right group of
advisors or brainstorm our way to a solution. Please don’t
hesitate to reach out! This is for every single student of IIT
Gandhinagar. You are doing well and you can still come to
us for any advice.
We are here for you!

Faculty Advisor
Your faculty advisor is your first and most important point
of contact and will:
B Guide you in creating your graduation roadmap.
B Provide valuable inputs on suitable course load
and course options each semester.
B Conduct periodic meetings with all his/her
advisees.
You must meet the faculty advisor whenever the need
arises.

Academic Discussion Hours
Academic Discussion Hours at IITGN is a forum for you to:
B freely discuss course topics with other interested
students.
B clarify doubts with the help of tutors chosen from
senior batches.
B receive study tips; guidance on solving routine and
challenging problems.

PAL (Peer Assisted Learning)

GPS (Guided Progress Scheme)

The PAL programme at IITGN:
B assists all those of you who are facing difficulties in
using English as the medium of instruction in your
coursework.
B helps you to learn beyond the classroom and enjoy
your student life.
In a model PAL session, a senior student assists two
first year students by sharing his/her knowledge and
experience.

In this scheme:
B the student’s academic load is capped at a
maximum number of credits.
B his/her study plan is prepared in consultation with
the Faculty Advisor and a dedicated GPS advisor.
B timely completion of course requirements is
monitored in the GPS.
The GPS advisor will be in regular contact and will help
negotiate the work load.
