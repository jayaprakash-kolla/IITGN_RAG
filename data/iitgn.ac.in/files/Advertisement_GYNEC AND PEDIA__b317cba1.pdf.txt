Gynaecologist and Pediatrician Opportunities at IIT Gandhinagar
The Indian Institute of Technology Gandhinagar (IITGN) invites applications from dedicated and
qualified medical professionals for the positions of Gynecologist and Pediatrician. These are contractbased roles, initially for one year, with the possibility of extension based on performance and institutional
requirements.
About IIT Gandhinagar
IIT Gandhinagar is a leading institution recognized for excellence in education, research, and innovation.
The institute fosters an environment that encourages interdisciplinary learning and holistic development.
As a medical professional at IITGN, you will play a vital role in supporting the health and well-being of a
vibrant campus community of over 3,500 members.
Position Details

Positions Available: 1 Gynaecologist, 1 Paediatrician (contract basis)
Duration: One year, extendable based on performance and requirements
Remuneration: Fixed compensation of ₹30,000–₹35,000 per month, based on qualifications and
experience (for part-time visits twice a week, 1.5 hours per visit)
Location: IIT Gandhinagar campus
Age Limit: Up to 50 years (up to 60 years for retired government or ex-service medical
professionals)

Key Responsibilities








Provide medical care and consultation at the Institute’s Medical Centre
Offer expert diagnosis and treatment to students, faculty, and staff
Contribute to community health initiatives and outreach programs
Attend to emergency cases beyond regular duty hours, as required
Collaborate with the campus healthcare team to ensure comprehensive care
Conduct thorough physical examinations and gather medical histories
Prioritize patient care based on clinical urgency

Qualifications


General: MBBS degree with at least 60% marks or equivalent grade, successful completion of
compulsory rotating internship, and registration with the National Medical Commission (NMC).
First-class academic record for MBBS is required.





Paediatrician: MBBS with MCI/NMC-recognized Postgraduate Degree/DNB in Paediatrics, or
MCI/NMC-recognized Diploma in Paediatrics with a minimum 55% and Minimum 5 years’
experience.
Gynaecologist: MBBS with MCI/NMC-recognized MD/MS/DNB in Obstetrics & Gynaecology,
valid medical registration, and with a minimum 55% and Minimum 5 years’clinical experience
preferred.

Perks and Benefits


Access to campus facilities, including the library, sports complex, and campus events
