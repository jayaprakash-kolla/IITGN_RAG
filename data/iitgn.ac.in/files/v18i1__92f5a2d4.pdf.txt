IITGN has achieved outstanding recognition in the India TodayMDRA Survey 2025. IITGN has been ranked 1st among the Top 10
Emerging Government Engineering Colleges in India, underscoring
our commitment to academic excellence and innovation. The Institute
has climbed to the 8th position in the list of Top 10 Colleges of 2025, a
significant leap from 16th in 2020—making IITGN the most improved
institution in this category. Additionally, IITGN has secured the 2nd
rank among the Top Three Region-wise Engineering Colleges in Western
India. These achievements reflect our relentless pursuit of excellence and
inspire us to continue shaping the future of education and research in
India.

IITGN organised the ‘Sabarmati Alumni & Faculty Research & Outreach
Network (SAFRON) 2025’ events in Chicago on July 11–12 and in the Bay
Area on July 13–14, USA. The initiative brought together prospective
faculty candidates, distinguished academic and industry leaders, and
IITGN alumni. Participants engaged in insightful discussions with Prof
Rajat Moona, Director IITGN, along with deans, faculty members.

MoU WITH ETHIOPIA

Shri P P Sukumaran

Jithin Prabha

IITGN signed an MoU with the Federal Democratic
Republic of Ethiopia to launch the Multidisciplinary
International Quality Improvement Doctoral Degree
Program (MIQMDDP). The initiative aims to strengthen
academic exchange at the PhD level, providing Ethiopian scholars
opportunities for joint supervision, collaborative research, and up to 18
months of study at IITGN.

MoU WITH IOWA STATE UNIVERSITY

IITGN and Iowa State University of Science and Technology
(ISU) have signed an MoU to introduce a Dual Degree
Program, enabling students to earn a Bachelor’s degree from
IITGN and a Master’s degree from ISU. The qualifying BTech
students from IITGN will have the opportunity to continue
their academic journey in the US at ISU across cutting-edge disciplines.

COLLABORATION WITH L&T AND C-DAC

IITGN, L&T Semiconductor Technologies Ltd
(LTSCT), and C-DAC initiated a tripartite agreement at
SEMICON India 2025 on September 1, 2025, to develop
and commercialise fully indigenous secure integrated
circuit (IC) solutions, starting with electronic
passports. The collaboration aims to reduce import
dependence, strengthen India’s semiconductor
ecosystem, and advance the Make in India vision for
critical technologies. The agreement includes setting up a dedicated
research centre at IITGN with a five-year plan to drive innovation in
secure computer technologies.

IITGN LAUNCHES ODISI
PROJECT TO TRANSFORM
RURAL LEARNING

ODiSI (OSAAT Digital School Infrastructure)
Project was launched on Aug 11, 2025, to
enhance rural education through technology.
Implemented by NEEV IITGN under OSAAT
and supported by Samagra Shiksha Gujarat,
NTT DATA, and the Bhupen & Shruti Shah
Family Foundation, the initiative will benefit
1,175 students across eight government primary
schools in Gandhinagar district. Inaugurated in
the presence of Shri Ranjeetkumar J (IAS), Prof
Amit Prashant, Dean, External Communications,
IITGN and Mr Sudheer Hullemane, Vice
President, OSAAT, the project equips schools
with digital infrastructure and AI-based tools via
the G-Shala platform, promoting interactive and
tech-enabled learning in rural Gujarat.

‘MAKER’S CURIOSITY’

immersive program, ‘Maker’s Curiosity:

TRAINING PROGRAM
ON OPTIMISING POWER
DESPATCH

IITGN held a one-day training program on
‘Optimising Power Despatch: SCED Initiative
in Gujarat’ on August 26, 2025, at the IITGN
Research Park. Organised by GETRI (a GUVNL
subsidiary), the event brought together experts
from GUVNL and its companies, GETCO,
GSECL, and IITGN. Through interactive sessions
and hands-on exercises, participants explored
Gujarat’s power sector roadmap, the Merit
Order Despatch framework, and advanced
SCED optimisation using GAMS and Python.
