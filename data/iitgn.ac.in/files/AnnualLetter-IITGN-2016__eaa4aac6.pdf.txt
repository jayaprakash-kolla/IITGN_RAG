Dear IIT Gandhinagar Friends, Alumni, Parents, and Well-Wishers,
It gives me great pleasure to introduce our annual update letter, in which we wish to
reflect on the year that has gone by, and surmise what we are looking forward to in the
year to come. The past has been among the most momentous in the eight-year history of
IIT Gandhinagar, and I consider it a privilege to briefly review the activities during this
period.

We shifted into our permanent campus, starting with hostels and classes, which
had moved to Palaj in July 2015. Most laboratories, faculty offices and residences were
relocated to the new campus by March 2016. It is a truly remarkable achievement for the
Institute to move into its new campus within three years of awarding of the

construction contracts. Our students exhibited extraordinary leadership in helping
stabilize the brand new campus. The batch of 2016 has been a pioneer in this respect and
we are extremely proud of them.
IITGN’s Palaj campus is the first in the country to receive a 5-star Green Rating for

Integrated Habitat Assessment for Large Developments (GRIHA LD). The
rating system evaluates the environmental performance of large developments like
educational campuses, townships, etc.

The Institute has started several new academic programs this academic year —
B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering; B Tech-M.Tech Dual degree programmes;
Dual Major in B.Tech Programme; and M.Tech and PGDIIT in Biological Engineering, and
in Earth System Sciences.

The Institute was ranked 8th in the country by the National Institutional Ranking

Framework (NIRF). Though our overall ranking is 8th, our ranking is 4th in Teaching,
Learning and Resources (TLR), and 5th in Outreach and Inclusivity (OI).
The Institute received several other recognitions as well during the year. It was awarded
“University of the Year” at the Federation of Indian Chambers of Commerce and Industry (FICCI)
Higher Education Excellence Awards 2015 in the category of “institutes in existence for less
than 10 years.” The staff residences and student hostels in the new campus bagged the First
Prize at the HUDCO Design Awards 2015 in the category of “Cost Effective Rural/ Urban Housing
including Disaster Resistant Housing.”

Research, Education and Infrastructure Highlights
IIT Gandhinagar takes tremendous pride in its innovative curricula and the extraordinary
opportunities it provides to its student body for holistic development. Two of our innovative
activities, Peer-group Assisted Learning (PAL) and the Foundation Programme are
now receiving national recognition and are being adopted by other universities in the country.
A large number of our students choose to pursue higher studies or entrepreneurship. A
substantial number of the students that graduated this year have secured admissions in top
universities, such as Stanford, Duke, Toronto, Urbana, and Imperial College. The Institute offers
a deferred placement policy, which allows students to defer their placements for up to 2 years
to pursue alternative career paths, and the number of students availing it rose to 21 this year,
from 9 last year. Again, a substantial increase in the number of students seeking alternate
career paths is an indicator of the self-confidence of our students.
The Summer Research Internship Program (SRIP) for our own students and those from
other institutions is now well institutionalized. This summer, nearly 100 students from outside
IIT Gandhinagar participated in this program and conducted research at the Institute.
Since its inception, IITGN has believed that the future of an institution depends on the quality
of its faculty and we continue to recruit some of the most outstanding faculty members. As of
today, the Institute has 87 career faculty members, of which more than a dozen have
received prestigious national fellowships, such as Inspire, Ramanujam, and Ramalingaswamy.
IIT Gandhinagar is a global partner in the landmark scientific discovery of gravitational waves
arising from colliding black holes. A research group at IITGN lead by Dr. Anand Sengupta has
been a part of this discovery. Our faculty members are now attracting substantial sponsored
research funds. The Institute was recently granted a project on fire safety under Uchhatar
Avishkar Yojana (UAY) with funding of Rs 3.73 crores; a substantial part of it is coming from a
private company.

Another major milestone of the year is the approval from Govt. of India to fund a

Research Park at IIT Gandhinagar. Envisioned as a highly productive physical space,
the Park aims to foster powerful interplay between industry and academia, and thereby
push the boundaries of innovation and research. Further, in an important development,
the Indian Army signed an MoU with IIT Gandhinagar on 27 December 2016,
initiating a long-term collaboration wherein we will work closely together with Indian Army
to find solutions to challenges our forces undergo in hostile terrains.

International Partnerships
This year, we forged a strong partnership with Duke University, supported through

USAID (under an agreement with the Ministry of Human Resource Development,
Government of India) and assisted by RTI International. In January 2016, a 15-member
delegation from Duke, RTI and USAID spent four days at the institute, and in June 2016, a
6-member delegation from IITGN followed up with a 5-day visit to RTI and Duke. A number
of our students and faculty have also visited Duke during the year as a result of this
partnership.
Our international collaborations continue to grow. This year, several IITGN students
credited summer courses at the Parsons School of Design, under an MOU we have
forged with The New School in New York. The fifth edition of India Ki Khoj, held in
December 2016, was joined by a team of eight students from Japan Advanced

Institute of Science and Technology (JAIST), twelve students from the California
Institute of Technology (CalTech), and ten students from IIT Gandhinagar.
Of the students that graduated in the 2016 Convocation, 53 BTech, 10 MTech, 6

MA/MSc, and 6 PhD students, had received international experience through
summer internships and participation in international conferences.

Community Outreach
It is important for us to make a difference to our immediate neighborhood. Nurture &
Empower Entrepreneurial Ventures (NEEV), our community outreach program, took up
several major activities: an entrepreneurship development workshop at Khambhat,
Gujarat; a 2-month long training in stitching for women from Palaj village; several
awareness programs on entrepreneurship for people from neighbouring villages of Palaj,
Basan and Lavarpur; and a nine-week vocational skills training program for

young men from nearby villages in trades such as carpentry, welding and
wiring.

Some of the other outreach activities included a workshop for science teachers (sponsored
by Sarva Shiksha Abhiyan, Gujarat); Sanjeevani, a health mela and medical camp for more
than 1,400 persons from neighboring villages (sponsored by Desai Foundation); Science
Day celebrations, in which over 1,200 school children participated, and a number of events
with the Foundation for Excellence (FFE) to support students from low income families
studying engineering across the country.

An exhibition featuring the products made by the participants of the vocational
skills training program organized by NEEV and supported by the Desai foundation.

IITGN’s agenda of excellence critically depends on its ability to raise philanthropic funds,
and we have made good progress to this end. To date, the Institute has received more than
Rs. 24 crores from well-wishers, and an additional Rs 16 crores has been pledged. Even
more importantly, many of our young alumni have now started to make regular monthly
donations of modest amounts. In fact, we shall soon be awarding the first set of

scholarships to our students that are entirely funded by our alumni.
Philanthropic support is crucial to our ability to provide students with the experiences that
define our unique approach to education, which prepares them for making a difference
when they are ready for the world at large.
Building a new university is a great privilege and an enormous responsibility. The actions
of the initial years have a disproportionate impact on the Institute in the decades ahead.
Very few get an opportunity to contribute to building a new university and all of us
associated with the Institute are mindful of the singular privilege accorded to us. We must
do everything within our means to prove ourselves worthy of the trust that our country and
society has placed in us by investing enormous resources on the Institute.
IIT Gandhinagar has made exceptional strides over the past eight years, and yet, we
recognize that we have a long way to go. We look forward to your continued support as we
take on the challenges ahead of us!
Best wishes,
Sudhir K. Jain
