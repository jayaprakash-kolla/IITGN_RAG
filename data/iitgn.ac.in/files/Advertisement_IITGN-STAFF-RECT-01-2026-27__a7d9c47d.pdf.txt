सामा

शत / General Conditions :

1. The appointment of Registrar shall be for five-years contract or up to the age of
superannuation, whichever is earlier. The contract may be renewed for similar terms to the
five-year contract through the usual mode of recruitment prescribed for the post or up to the
age of superannuation, whichever is earlier.
2. Required experience in different Pay Levels mentioned in this advertisement is as per 7th CPC.
Any experience gained prior to implementation of 7th CPC or in any earlier CPC even after
implementation of 7th CPC may be counted as ‘equivalent’ provided such experience is gained
in corresponding Pay Scale or Grade pay.
3. Educational qualifications should be from a recognized board or university. Equivalency of any
qualification, degree, experience or pay etc. shall be decided by the Competent Authority
subject to production of relevant supporting documents. The decision of the competent
authority with regard to equivalence of qualification(s) and about recognition of
Board/University shall be final and binding.
4. If any document/certificate furnished is in a language other than Hindi or English, a transcript
of the same duly attested by a Gazetted Officer or Notary is to be submitted.
5. The upper age limit for the post is 58 years. However, for Direct recruitment, candidates at the
age of 57 years or less will be preferred provided they are otherwise eligible. Relaxation in age
will be admissible as per Government of India Rules.
6. Candidates employed in Govt./Semi-Govt. organizations/PSU/Autonomous Bodies/
Educational Institutions etc. should upload NOC and vigilance clearance certificates while
applying online along with all other relevant and self-attested supporting documents. In case
any candidate is not able to upload the NOC and vigilance clearance certificate at the time of
application, he/she must produce the same at the time of written test and/or interview etc.
failing which, he/she shall not be allowed to appear in the selection process.

8. Incomplete applications or applications without relevant supporting documents/ enclosures
such as academic degree certificates/ mark-sheet/ experience certificate etc. or applications
with illegible supporting documents shall summarily be rejected.
9. Eligible candidates interested in applying on deputation basis shall apply through proper
channel along with his/her APAR of preceding 5 years in addition to all supporting documents
and Vigilance Clearance Certificate. However, they will also have to apply online and online
application will be considered as advance copy only. In case of non-receipt of application along
with APAR through proper channel within 10 days from the closing date of receipt of online
application, their candidature shall not be considered. The appointment in case of deputation
will be on standard terms and conditions of deputation as per GoI norms.
10. No interim correspondence shall be entertained. Canvassing in any form and/or bringing of any
influence political or otherwise, will be treated as a disqualification for the post.
11. Merely fulfilling minimum eligibility criteria does not entail a call for written test and/or interview
etc. for the selection. Only those candidates, who will be shortlisted on the basis of better
qualification and quality of relevant experience, shall be called for written test and/or interview
etc. The authorities reserve all rights, not to call an applicant for selection process or not to fill
up a position through this round of selection process without assigning any reason or cancel
the advertisement at any stage of the selection process.
12. Please ensure to upload the salary slips/experience certificate from your employer towards
experience claimed in the online application.
13. There is No Application Fee for applying to this post.
