EAT RIGHT CAMPUS AWARD

Achieving one more feat, IITGN
has become the first educational
institute in the country to receive
the Eat Right Campus Award
with 5-star rating from the Food
Safety and Standards Authority
of India (FSSAI). The award
was presented at a function held
in FDA Bhawan, New Delhi,
on June 7, 2019. The Institute is
certified as ‘Eat Right Campus’
based on five parameters i.e. a)
Compliance to food safety and hygiene, b) Healthy diets, c) Food waste management, d) Promotion
of local and seasonal foods on campus, and e) Promotion and awareness of food safety and
healthy diets in and around campus.

MoU WITH CPWD

IITGN and the Central Public Works
Department (CPWD) have entered into an
agreement to collaborate for cutting-edge
research & development in the housing and
urban development sector. As per the MoU
signed, the CPWD will set up an R&D Cell at
the Research Park of IITGN. This significant
partnership between IITGN and CPWD
is aimed at fostering collaboration between
seasoned engineers of CPWD, faculty and
students of IITGN, to enable development
and verification of new technologies, and safer,
efficient, long-term solutions to the existing
problems of the construction industry.

Activities at IITGN

SUMMER RESEARCH INTERNSHIP
PROGRAM (SRIP)

A total of 144 students from various prestigious
institutes across the country were selected for
the Summer Research Internship Program
(SRIP) to spend eight weeks carrying out
their research at IITGN. The program offers
opportunities to students from India and
abroad to carry out research projects under the
mentorship of IITGN faculty members. This
time, about 44 faculty members of the Institute
guided the participating students on a wide
range of research projects.

JEE OPEN HOUSE

This year, IITGN organised JEE Open House
sessions at three different places viz. Delhi,
Gandhinagar, and Mumbai for the convenience
of JEE Advanced qualified students and their
parents. The programs aimed at addressing
queries about IIT admission, various disciplines,
academic activities and opportunities at IITs, as
well as about the IIT Gandhinagar ecosystem.

The sessions at Delhi (on June 22, 2019) and
Mumbai (on June 23, 2019) were conducted
by the Prof Harish PM, Dean of Student
Affairs and Prof Jaison Manjaly, Coordinator
of Alumni Relations, along with students and
alumni of IITGN. The event at IITGN on
June 23, 2019 included interactive sessions with
faculty members, and students and alumni. A
campus tour was also organized during the
event.

TEQIP III

The third phase of the Technical Education
Quality Improvement Programme (TEQIP)
was held at IITGN in three different batches
from May 20 to 24, May 27 to 31, and June
10 to 14, 2019. More than 200 faculty members
from engineering colleges from all over the
country participated in the program to learn
about Advanced Pedagogy and Digital Tools of
Learning.

RASANAYAM ‘19

The discipline of Chemistry hosted its first
ever Chemistry Festival, Rasayanam ‘19 during
April 6-7, 2019. The two-day extravaganza
was marked by a popular talk by Prof Uday
Maitra, an eminent chemist at IISc Bangalore,
and many other fun-filled events such as quiz,
debate, chem charades, dramatics, prayog, and
art attack.

WIN CONFERENCE

WIN (Wheels India Niswarth) Foundation,
together with Wheels Charitable Foundation,
IIT Gandhinagar and Indian Institute of Public
Health, Gandhinagar (IIPHG) organised the
first WIN Conference 2019 : “Innovate and
Empower to Accelerate Social Change” on
April 5-6, 2019 at the Institute. The conference
was aimed at providing a platform to various
stakeholders, with common objective to drive
and accelerate change for the under-served
communities. The conference focused on 3
domains: (i) Water and Sanitation, (ii) Maternal
and Child Health, and (iii) Smart Villages.

RFID SYSTEM AT LIBRARY

The Central Library of the Institute has
implemented RFID System, and the system
has gone live from May 15, 2019. Institute ID
Cards of all students, faculty and staff have
been enabled to use RFID system. With the
implementation of RFID system, issue and
return of library books, tracking books in the
library, detecting non-issued books being taken
out, inventory control, etc. will become much
easier and faster. Moreover, users would now be
able to perform self checkout and check-in of
library books by using a dedicated kiosk.
