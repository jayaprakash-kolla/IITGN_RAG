Facilitate MoUs and Agreements: Draft, process, and
coordinate Memoranda of Understanding (MoUs) and related
agreements with overseas and Indian universities,
institutions, and industry partners.
Liaison & Coordination: Serve as the primary point of
contact for international and national partner institutions;
coordinate discussions, queries, and follow-ups.
Partnership Development: Support the development,

joint programs.

delegations, and other external partners.
Program Support: Assist in administering international
programs, mobility processes, and partnership activities.
Documentation & Reporting: Maintain records of MoUs,
track partnership activities, and prepare brief reports as
needed.
