Signature Programs

IIT Gandhinagar is driven by a zeal to nurture and advance
our students’ unique capabilities, with several programs
that channel and develop their talents. The Foundation
Programme, where freshman students prove themselves
to be great thinkers and highly adaptive problem solvers in
all avenues of life, introduces them to a range of handson activities, interactions with eminent personalities, and
various opportunities to express themselves.

The Maker Bhavan and Invention Factory are novel
pedagogical initiatives that excite and encourage student
creativity and participation, while fostering highly beneficial
interdisciplinary interactions and promoting innovation.
The Leadership Development Initiative promotes
transformative leadership skills among IITGN students
through a series of sustained and progressive learning
experiences. ART at IITGN is an initiative that facilitates
liberal and inclusive artistic expressions and values.

Centres of Excellence

The IIT Gandhinagar Centres promote interdisciplinary research and make state-of-the-art technology accessible to industry
and academia.
⁙ Dr Kiran C Patel Centre for Sustainable
Development (KPCSD) undertakes cutting-edge
research on sustainability and related challenges of
high societal importance.
⁙ Centre for Safety Engineering throws light on and
advances a safe built environment in both the public
and private spheres of urbanised development.
⁙ Design & Innovation Centre fosters a
multidisciplinary approach to promote research and
educational initiatives in design and innovation.
⁙ Centre for Cognitive and Brain Sciences, through
highly innovative research programs, allows the

exploration of questions concerning the mind, brain,
and cognition.
⁙ Centre for Biomedical Engineering undertakes
advanced research in Diagnostic/Therapeutic Tools,
Automated Rehabilitation and Prosthetics, and Public
Health.
⁙ Centre for Creative Learning radically reinvigorates
the scientific temper and inherent creativity in school
students and teachers.
⁙ Archaeological Sciences Centre conducts
research in varied disciplines from materials science
to petrology, archaeometallurgy, and more.

World-Class Infrastructure

The state-of-the-art infrastructure on campus is designed to inspire excellence and eminence. The Jasubhai Memorial
Auditorium is a sustainably-designed 580-seater auditorium on campus, complemented by the amphitheatre
Rangmanch with a 2000 person-seating capacity. The advanced facilities of the Sports Complex were home to athletes
during the 36th National Games. The Institute Guest House is an 85-room facility, including 15 long-stay rooms, with
several additional facilities including a conference hall, executive meeting rooms, etc.
