Professor Sudhir K Jain at the helm

Professor Sudhir Kumar Jain joined as the first Director of
IITGN on June 4, 2009. Prof Jain received his Bachelor of
Engineering (Civil) from University of Roorkee in 1979,
the Master of Science from California Institute of
Technology (Caltech), Pasadena, USA in 1980, and the
Doctor of Philosophy, also from Caltech, in 1983. He
joined Civil Engineering faculty at IIT Kanpur in 1984 and
in June 2009 moved over to IIT Gandhinagar as its
Director.

Professor Jain is an active researcher in earthquake engineering. He has carried
out important studies after all the major earthquakes in India since 1988. He has
been instrumental in improving state-of-the-practice in the construction
industry in India, through development of Indian seismic codes and
commentaries, and extremely popular continuing education courses for
professional engineers. Professor Jain serves on the Board of Directors of the
International Association for Earthquake Engineering and is currently its
Executive Vice President. He is on the Board of Directors of the World Seismic
Safety Initiative, an organization that promotes earthquake risk reduction in
developing countries. Professor Jain was elected a Fellow of the Indian National

Academy of Engineering in 2003.
Campus and Facilities

IIT Gandhinagar is temporarily housed within the campus of Vishwakarma
Govt Engg College (VGEC), Chandkheda, in Ahmedabad. An academic
infrastructure comprising classrooms, laboratories, faculty offices, library,
medical facilities, etc., exclusively for the use of IIT Gandhinagar students and
faculty has been established in this campus. Student hostels have been created
in the multistory residential buildings across the street from the campus. The
living conditions at IIT Gandhinagar are of the highest quality, emphasizing the
well being of the students through a good mix of academic rigour and sporting
activities.

Academic Performance

The academic performance of the first batch of
students at the end of academic year 2008-09
was exemplary. 16 students out of 90 secured a
Cumulative Percentage Index (CPI) of 9.0 and
above.

Liberalization of Branch

Change Rules

In June 2009, the Academic Council decided to
liberalize the branch change rules at the end of
first year based on academic performance. This
will enable many more students to move to
their preferred branch and will further improve
their motivation for studies. This change
enabled 10 students to change their branch, as
compared to just one student who would have
been able to do so under the earlier rules.
