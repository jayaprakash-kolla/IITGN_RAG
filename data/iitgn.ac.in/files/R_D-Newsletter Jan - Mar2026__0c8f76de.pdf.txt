COVER STORY
As artificial intelligence increasingly shapes how societies respond to complex challenges, Indian Institute of Technology Gandhinagar (IITGN)
inaugurated the AI Resilience and Command (ARC) Centre at its Research Park to strengthen climate resilience. Developed through research at
the Machine Intelligence and Resilience (MIR) Lab, the centre integrates AI with flood modelling to deliver real-time predictive insights during
extreme weather events. Supported by the AI Centre of Excellence (AICoE) – Sustainable Cities, AIResQ ClimSols Pvt Ltd, and the Airawat Research
Foundation, it aims to help climate-stressed cities make faster, data-driven decisions for improved infrastructure and water-risk management.
Further advancing discussions at the intersection of AI and healthcare, IITGN hosted the NeuroAI Conclave 2026, bringing together
researchers, clinicians, and healthcare experts to explore AI-driven neuroscience and neurodegenerative disease research. The
conclave featured discussions on AI-enabled neuroimaging, generative AI for drug discovery, translational neuroscience, and ethical
AI deployment in clinical settings.
Continuing this momentum, AI Day at IITGN explored how India can move beyond using AI to actively shaping its development, governance,
and deployment. Supported by the Anusandhan National Research Foundation (ANRF), the Department of Computer Science and Engineering,
and IITGN’s Centre for AI-driven Innovations, the event featured discussions on foundational AI models, governance frameworks, biomedical
innovation, strategic technologies, and AI & society. Together, these initiatives reflected IITGN’s growing role in fostering responsible and
impactful AI innovation.
