ABOUT US
We at NEEV believe in strengthening and empowering our society by
helping people get education and develop skills. One of our core initiatives is empowering women by teaching them to stitch, cut, measure
and sew. The women make handmade cloth items and these items are
open for sale for everyone.
You will receive top quality products at affordable prices.
The items present in our catalog can be preordered. We also make
customized items on demand.
Stay Updated and Keep Browsing!
Our handcrafted Products are made with eco-friendly materials,
designed for daily use or as stylish accessories. Each Product is
meticulously stitched by women from the surrounding villages,
providing them with stable income while promoting sustainable living.
Perfect for conferences, workshops, or casual outings.
