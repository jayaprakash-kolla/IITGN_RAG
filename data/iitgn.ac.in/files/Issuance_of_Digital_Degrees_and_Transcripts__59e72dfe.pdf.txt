TO WHOM IT MAY CONCERN

This is to certify that Indian Institute of Technology Gandhinagar issues digitally
generated degree certificates and transcripts to its graduating students.

Beginning in 2023, the Institute has been issuing digitally signed degree certificates
and transcripts in Portable Document Format (PDF). These documents are
electronically signed by the authorized signatories in accordance with the provisions
of the Information Technology Act, 2000, and can be independently verified using
standard PDF viewers.

These digital documents are also accessible through DigiLocker.

The transition to digital degrees was approved by the Senate in its 63rd meeting held
on 17 March 2023. Prior to this, the Institute issued hard-copy degree certificates and
transcripts bearing physical signatures. For degrees awarded from 2023 onwards, the
Institute issues only digitally signed certificates and transcripts in PDF format and does
not issue hard-copy versions.
