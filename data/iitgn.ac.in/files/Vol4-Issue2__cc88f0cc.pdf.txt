Ph. D. fellowship for top undergraduate students
In order to attract students of IITs, NITs, and certain top
engineering colleges in the country to its Ph. D. programme, the
Institute is offering direct admission to fourth year B. Tech.
students regardless of whether they have qualified GATE. An
additional fellowship of Rs 10, 000 per month will be given to the
students who qualify GATE.
Children's Education Fund
In keeping with its strong sense of commitment to the well-being
of its community members the Institute has set up the Children's
Education Fund for children who are otherwise not eligible to
receive the Institute's support but whose parents provide essential
services to the effective functioning of the Institute. The
beneficiaries include daily wage labourers and persons employed
by the contractors. This fund provides Rs 5,000 to every family
per year to cover the cost of school education up to class 12.

Picnic to Thol Lake
The annual picnic of IITGN organized on November 6, 2011, saw
enthusiastic participation from faculty, staff and their family
members as they enjoyed an outing to Thol Lake which is 40 kms
north-west of Ahmedabad, in Mehsana District. The Thol Lake

and Bird Sanctuary is a picturesque place that is frequented by
avid bird-watchers for the nearly 200 species of birds that migrate
to this area particularly in winter months.

Reaching Out
Prof. Sudhir K. Jain and Prof. Subhash Deodhar represented the Institute in the Pan IIT Alumni Meet 2011 in New York (September
30 - October 2, 2011) and networked with eminent alumni of the IIT system. Prof. Jain also addressed graduate students and postdocs at
Columbia University and MIT with a view to seeking outstanding faculty candidates for the Institute. He visited Olin College to
explore opportunities for close cooperation between the two institutes considering the reputation for innovations in engineering that
Olin College has acquired in a relatively short period. Prof. Jain also participated in the Young Investigators Meeting (YIM), Boston
(October 8-10, 2011), an annual event to connect aspiring faculty candidates of Indian origin in the US with academic institutions in
India. IIT Gandhinagar was one of the main sponsors of the YIM this year.

Student and Staff Activities
Inter-IIT Gymkhana Summit 2011
The fourth Inter-IIT Gymkhana Summit was organized at
IITGN by the Student Gymkhana (October 22-23, 2011). Student
representatives of eight IITs (Guwahati, Rajasthan, Mandi,
Ropar, Indore, Madras, Hyderabad and Gandhinagar)
participated in the discussions at the summit. IIT Kanpur and IIT
Bombay also joined the sessions online. A major highlight of the
summit was a brain-storming session with Mr Sunil Parekh,
founder and head of Pan IIT Gujarat Association, and Prof.
Utpal Sharma, CEPT University.
Visit to Mundra Supercritical Power Station
Fourth year Mechanical Engineering students visited the Mundra
Power Station on October 29, 2011 to get a feel for the
complexities of operation of the most modern power plant in
India. The visit was coordinated by Prof. Vedanth Kadambi.

International Year of Forests
To mark the year 2011 as the International Year of Forests (IYF) a
community tree-plantation drive was organized around the IIT
Gandhinagar hostels on December 29, 2011 and about 60 trees
were planted. The chief guests were Prof. Sudhir K. Jain,
Director IIT Gandhinagar and Mr Pushkar Ganesh Vaidya,
Indian Astrobiology Research Centre and IYF programme head.
The tree-plantation was followed by a lecture by Mr Vadiya on
"Forests and Astrobiology - Why this Kolaveri Di?". Organizers
of this event were Susmitha Purnima Kotu (Campus
Ambassador-IYF 2011) and Blithchron Green Initiative 2012.
