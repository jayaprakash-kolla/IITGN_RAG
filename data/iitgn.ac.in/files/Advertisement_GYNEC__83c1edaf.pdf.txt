Gynaecologist Opportunities at IIT Gandhinagar
The Indian Institute of Technology Gandhinagar (IITGN) invites applications from dedicated and qualified
medical professionals for the position of Gynaecologist. This is contract-based and part-time role, initially
upto 31 December 2027, with the possibility of extension based on performance and institutional
requirements.
About IIT Gandhinagar
IIT Gandhinagar is a leading institution recognized for excellence in education, research, and innovation.
The institute fosters an environment that encourages interdisciplinary learning and holistic development.
As a medical professional at IITGN, you will play a vital role in supporting the health and well-being of
a vibrant campus community of over 3,500 members.
Position Details
•
•
•
•
•

Positions Available: 1 Gynaecologist, (part-time and contract basis)
Duration: Upto 31 December 2027, extendable based on performance and requirements
Remuneration: Fixed compensation of ₹35,000 per month, based on qualifications and
experience (for part-time visits twice a week, 1.5 hours per visit)
Location: IIT Gandhinagar campus
Age Limit: Up to 50 years (up to 60 years for retired government or ex-service medical
professionals)

Key Responsibilities
•
•
•
•
•
•
•

Provide medical care and consultation at the Institute’s Medical Centre
Offer expert diagnosis and treatment to students, faculty, and staff
Contribute to community health initiatives and outreach programs
Attend to emergency cases beyond regular duty hours, as required
Collaborate with the campus healthcare team to ensure comprehensive care
Conduct thorough physical examinations and gather medical histories
Prioritize patient care based on clinical urgency

Qualifications
•

Gynaecologist: MBBS with MCI/NMC-recognized MD/MS/DNB/DGO in Obstetrics &
Gynaecology, valid medical registration, and with a minimum 55% and Minimum 5 years’clinical
experience preferred.

Perks and Benefits
•

Access to campus facilities, including the library, sports complex, and campus events
