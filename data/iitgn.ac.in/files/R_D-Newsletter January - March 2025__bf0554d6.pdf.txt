COVER STORY
CoLab 2025
The first quarter of 2025 at IIT Gandhinagar was marked by a series of vibrant workshops,
symposia, and collaborative events, underscoring the institute’s steadfast commitment to
academic-industry partnerships. Continuing its tradition of fostering meaningful dialogue
and innovation, IITGN hosted the much-anticipated second edition of CoLab, the Institute’s
annual Industry Open House, on 22nd February, 2025.
Drawing over 200 industry leaders, researchers, and innovators, the event reaffirmed a simple yet
powerful belief: academia and industry are natural allies — each offering distinct strengths that, when
combined, shape a brighter, more sustainable future. CoLab 2025 served as a lively platform for knowledge
exchange and networking, featuring stimulating discussions and showcases across ten key areas,
including Artificial Intelligence, Quantum Technologies, Sustainability, and Advanced Manufacturing.
As collaborations spark new ideas and lasting partnerships, events like CoLab continue to reflect IITGN’s
vision of blending intellectual rigour with practical ingenuity for transformative impact.

CHIANG MAI UNIVERSITY, THAILAND

An MoU was signed between the Faculty of Engineering and the Faculty
of Science, IIT Gandhinagar, and the Faculty of Engineering, Chiang Mai
University, Thailand, to foster academic and research collaboration in
science and engineering. The partnership aims to promote joint research
ventures, exchange programs for faculty and students, shared academic
programs/workshops/conferences, entrepreneurial ventures, and industryacademia initiatives.

UNIVERSITY OF CALIFORNIA SAN DIEGO, USA
IITGN and the Halicioglu Data Science Institute at UC San Diego have signed
an MoU to promote collaboration in education and research. The partnership
covers faculty and student exchanges, joint research projects, shared
publications, material and information exchange, research internships, and
special programs such as workshops, virtual classrooms, and short-term
academic initiatives.

TATA ELECTRONICS PRIVATE LIMITED, BANGALORE
IITGN has signed an MoU with Tata Electronics Private Limited and
its subsidiary Tata Semiconductor Manufacturing Private Limited, to
establish a Centre of Excellence named SAMARTH — Silicon and Advanced
Semiconductor Manufacturing Research and Training Hub. This initiative
aims to meet the evolving needs of India’s electronics and microelectronics
industries and the global semiconductor sector. The collaboration will focus
on developing a highly skilled workforce to support the upcoming Dholera
semiconductor fabrication facility, creating educational and training
programs for technicians, engineers, and supply chain professionals, and
advancing research in next-generation semiconductor manufacturing by
leveraging IITGN’s scientific and engineering expertise.
