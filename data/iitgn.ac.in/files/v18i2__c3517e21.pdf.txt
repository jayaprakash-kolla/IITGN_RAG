GoG MoU FOR SEMICONDUCTOR RESEARCH HUB

Mehsana on Oct 9, 2025, IIT Gandhinagar (IITGN) entered into
a MoU with the Government of Gujarat to establish the Silicon
and Advanced Semiconductor Manufacturing Research and
Training Hub (SAMARTH) at IITGN. The MoU was signed by
Prof Rajat Moona, Director, IITGN and Ms Neha Kumari, IAS,
Mission Director, Gujarat State Electronics Mission.

VIETNAM ACADEMIC COLLABORATION

IITGN signed an MoU with Ho Chi Minh City University
of Economics and Finance (UEF) on Oct 11, 2025 to
enhance academic and research collaboration between
India and Vietnam. The partnership focuses on joint
research, student and faculty exchanges and innovation initiatives. The
ceremony featured remarks by Prof Amit Prashant, Dean, External
Relations and Prof S P Mehrotra, Dean, Faculty Affairs. The Vietnamese
delegation was led by Dr Nguyen Thanh Giang, President, UEF.

QUEEN’S UNIVERSITY BELFAST ACADEMIC
PARTNERSHIP

Strengthening its global academic collaborations,
IITGN entered into a Memorandum of

Belfast on Nov 3, 2025, to collaborate in education, research and
innovation. With Queen’s University establishing its international campus
at GIFT City, the partnership focuses on faculty and student exchanges,

IISc BANGALORE ACADEMIC LINKAGE

GMRICS / GMDC MOU ON CRITICAL MINERALS

minerals, IITGN entered into an MoU with the Gujarat Mineral Research
Industrial Consultancy Society (GMRICS), promoted by GMDC, on Nov 18,
2025. The partnership focuses on joint research, internships, training
programmes and knowledge exchange supporting India’s evolving critical
mineral ecosystem.
