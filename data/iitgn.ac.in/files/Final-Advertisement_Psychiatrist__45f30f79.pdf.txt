Part-Time Consultant Psychiatrist Position at IIT Gandhinagar
The Indian Institute of Technology Gandhinagar (IITGN) invites applications from
dedicated and qualified medical professionals for the position of Consultant
Psychiatrist. This is a contract-based and part-time role, initially for one year, with the
possibility of extension based on performance and institutional requirements.
About IIT Gandhinagar
IIT Gandhinagar is a leading institution recognized for excellence in education,
research, and innovation. The institute fosters an environment that encourages
interdisciplinary learning and holistic development. As a medical professional at
IITGN, you will play a vital role in supporting the health and well-being of a vibrant
campus community of over 3,500 members.
Position Details
•
•
•
•
•

Positions Available: 1 Psychiatrist (part-time and contract basis)
Period: One year, extendable based on performance and requirements
Consultation Hours: 2 hours, once a week (may be revised as needed based on
mutual consent)
Remuneration: Fixed compensation of ₹20,000–₹30,000 per month,
depending on qualifications and experience
Location: IIT Gandhinagar campus

Key Responsibilities
•

•
•
•
•
•
•
•

Provide psychiatric evaluation, diagnosis, and treatment for the institute
community (students, staff, and faculty and their family members), and advise
their family members when required
Conduct scheduled outpatient consultation hours on campus, ensuring timely
access for students and staff in need
Work collaboratively with counselors, student wellbeing team, and the medical
center to offer coordinated care for students
Offer crisis assessment and management for acute mental health situations
requiring immediate attention
Advise on referral pathways for individuals requiring specialized or inpatient
care outside the institute
Support institute-level wellbeing initiatives, including awareness programs or
workshops (as needed)
Maintain accurate clinical documentation in accordance with professional and
institutional standards
Ensure confidentiality and uphold ethical standards in all interactions with
students, staff, and faculty and their family members.

MBBS degree from an MCI/NMC-recognized medical college with at least

MD in Psychiatry
