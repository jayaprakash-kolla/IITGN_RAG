Dear well-wishers of IIT Gandhinagar,
As we bid farewell to 2024 and prepare to welcome a promising new year, it is
worthwhile to take a moment to reflect on 2024 and all that it brought for IIT

Gandhinagar. This year was marked by several milestones for the Institute
across various fronts. From setting new benchmarks in education and
innovation to fostering meaningful collaborations and driving societal impact,

2024 has been a year of growth, recognition, and impactful initiatives for the
Institute. With a vibrant community of over 2800 students, 176 faculty, and
more than 500 staff members, the Institute is on a growth path.

Campus Development

Marking a new chapter in IITGN’s growth, we were honoured to have Hon’ble

Prime Minister Shri Narendra Modi lay the foundation stone of student hostels
and staff residences of IITGN for Phase 2A construction of the Institute, via

video conference. I am pleased to share that the construction work under this
project is progressing smoothly.

The past year has been remarkable for IITGN, bringing numerous accolades
that highlight our achievements across various domains. Among these, we
bagged the prestigious Star Campus Award 2024 in water conservation and
management category. This accolade presented by Earth Day Network –

India, celebrates our innovation and sustainable practices in water
conservation and environmental stewardship. Furthermore, IITGN’s green

initiatives at its campus, under the theme of ‘Sanitation’ has been recognised
with the ‘HUDCO Award for Best Practices to Improve the Living Environment
2023-24.’ This achievement showcases our dedication to sustainability and
the green initiatives implemented on campus. Most recently, IITGN was

awarded the CII Award for Industry-Academia Partnership as the Gold

Winner in the Government Institute Category for 2024. Instituted by the

Confederation of Indian Industry (CII), this honour recognises institutions
that excel in fostering collaborations with industry to drive innovation and
societal progress.

In another proud moment, IIT Gandhinagar’s Guest House designed by
Architect Neeraj Manchanda won the Architectural Excellence Award of 2024

for its unique and novel design. Continuing our efforts in environmental
conservation, the IITGN community came together to plant 8,000 saplings
during the 2024 planting season in a tree plantation programme.

Our students and faculty continue to expand horizons and develop meaningful
and innovative solutions. In 2024, IITGN researchers developed ‘Ganga-1B’,

an AI language model designed specifically for Hindi. This pre-trained model,

created from scratch using Hindi datasets, supports both Hindi and English,

as well as a mix of the two languages. Additionally, a group of IITGN students
successfully developed a fully-functional autonomous vehicle capable of

operating without a driver. The students transformed an electric golf cart
into a cutting-edge driverless vehicle that can navigate the campus
independently, connecting the hostels to the academic area.

Exemplifying our responsiveness to the evolving needs of the industry, we

introduced new BTech/Mtech and PhD programmes in ‘Integrated Circuit
Design and Technology (ICDT)’ as part of the Electrical Engineering

department from the academic year 2024-2025. Additionally, the Institute
launched a new e-Master’s degree programme in Composites, which offers indepth exploration of composite materials, equipping professionals to excel in

aerospace, automotive, and other high-performance sectors. Furthermore,
IITGN in collaboration with the Asian Institute of Technology (AIT), Thailand
launched a Double Degree Master’s Programme (DDMP) in August 2024. The

collaboration offers students an opportunity to earn two separate master’s
degrees, one from IITGN and another from AIT.

We also hosted our 13th Convocation ceremony in 2024 which witnessed the

largest ever contingent of 530 students receiving their degrees. During the
convocation, IITGN also conferred the Institute Fellow awards to Mr Mahesh

R Patel, Former Principal, Vishwakarma Government Engineering College,
and Mr Ruyintan Mehta, Former President, IITGN Foundation in the United

States, for their lasting contributions to the advancement of the Institute.
Our students and faculty continue to excel, earning recognition for their
contributions to research and innovation.

Visitors at IITGN

We had the privilege of hosting several eminent personalities from India as

well as overseas this year which explored several opportunities for

collaboration. A special delegation from various Japanese organisations
visited IITGN on January 12, 2024, to explore possibilities for industry
research collaboration and understand innovation-related best practices at

the Institute. We also welcomed Consul General Mike Hankey from the US
Consulate General, Mumbai. His visit aimed to explore bilateral cooperation in

higher education between the USA and India. Another high-level delegation
from the Govt of Karnataka headed by Dr M C Sudhakar, Hon’ble Minister of

Higher Education, Govt of Karnataka, visited the Institute and held engaging

discussions on various aspects of academics and research. The officials were
also given a campus tour and explored the state-of-the-art sports facilities,
hostels, and academic area.

Additionally, we hosted Lt Governor of New Jersey, Tahesha Way, who was

accompanied by an esteemed delegation of guests from the New Jersey-India
Commission and the US Consulate Mumbai, along with noted professionals
from industry and academia.

Partnerships and Opportunities

This year we forged several meaningful partnerships and MoUs with renowned
universities across the globe and industries to foster collaboration in research,
education, and innovation.

In the beginning of 2024, IITGN signed an MoU with the University of San

Diego, USA, to collaborate in various academic, research, capacity building,

training, and faculty/staff development activities. We also partnered with the

Graduate School of Science, Tokyo Metropolitan University to enhance
collaboration in staff exchange, joint research, student exchange and

cooperation in various research activities like symposia, conferences,
information exchange, publication, and data sharing. In February 2024, we

joined hands with California Institute of Technology to establish a

comprehensive student exchange programme for 2024-2028. Adding to our
collaborations with global universities, IITGN inked an MoU with City

University of London. This agreement will facilitate the development of dual

degree MSc programmes in Cyber Security, and Financial Technology and
Systems.

Furthermore, IITGN signed an MoU with Japan Advanced Institute of

Science and Technology (JAIST) to develop educational and scientific
exchange and cooperation. Under the agreement, both the organisations will

conduct joint research projects, exchange academic publications and reports,
and will collaborate to organise joint symposia, workshops, lectures, and
conferences. Most recently, we collaborated with National Changhua

University of Education, National Yang Ming Chiao Tung University,
Taiwan, and Michigan State University East Lansing, Michigan, USA, to
promote academic exchanges and cooperation.

Expanding our reach, IITGN signed an MoU with Samagra Shiksha, Uttar

Pradesh to foster collaborative activities to enhance learning experience of

children attending PM Shri schools in Uttar Pradesh. As part of the agreement,
CCL, IITGN will provide STEM toys, models and activities for Science and

Maths, including out-of-the-box thinking puzzles and games. Furthermore,
with an MoU with the Desai Foundation Trust (DFT) we aim to establish long

term relationships to conduct programmes focussed on uplifting the health

Moreover, IITGN inked an MoU with Shah Bhogilal Jethalal and Bros. to
enhance the fire engineering research facilities at the Institute. As part of

this agreement, Shri Mukeshbhai Shah has generously pledged to fund the
construction of a new laboratory building dedicated to fire research.

IIT Gandhinagar is thrilled to partner with Gujarat International Finance

Tec-City (GIFT City), Ahmedabad University, and UC San Diego to launch
the GIFT International Fintech Institute (GIFT IFI). The launch of the GIFT

IFI brings an inspiring opportunity to redefine education, research, and
entrepreneurship in the Indian FinTech domain through curated skill
development and training programmes.

NEEV and Nyasa continue doing impactful work uplifting the lives of several
people around the campus and beyond. I am pleased to share that NEEV

recently celebrated 10 years of its inception. With a humble inception in 2014,

the programme now serves 6000 beneficiaries spread across over 40 villages

in Gujarat. Similarly, the Centre for Creative Learning (CCL) at IITGN has
achieved remarkable outreach through its Curiosity Programme. Now

spanning 12 states, it has positively influenced over 2,00,000 young girls from
Kasturba Gandhi Balika Vidyalayas (KGBVs). The programme features 80 live

biweekly online STEM sessions that make fundamental science and
mathematics concepts engaging, accessible, and enjoyable.

We take immense pride in the strides IITGN has made toward fostering

excellence, innovation, and inclusivity. The accomplishments of this year are

a testament to the unwavering dedication of our students, faculty, staff, and
supporters. Looking ahead, we remain committed to building on this
momentum, driving impactful change, and scaling new heights of success.
Wishing you all a prosperous new year!

Rajat Moona

Director, IIT Gandhinagar
