Outstanding Academic Performance to be Recognized
Every semester undergraduate students with outstanding
academic performance will be recognized by the Institute. A
student with an SPI of 8.5 or more, and also satisfying other
criteria set forth by the Academic Senate, will be placed on the
Dean's List. Each student on the Dean's List will receive a letter of
appreciation from the Dean of Academic Programmes and a
topical book.
Non-Degree Students in Post-Graduate Programme
The Institute has been providing opportunities to full time
students of other institutions to pursue course work or research
work for one or two semester at IITGN. Starting spring semester

2010-11, this opportunity has been made available both at UG and
PG levels to non-students as well. Interested persons can credit
one or two courses at a time, or can do one or two semesters of full
course load at IITGN.
Summer Research Opportunity for Faculty of Engineering
Institutions
The Institute is inviting faculty of engineering institutions and
providing them opportunities to (a) participate in the ongoing
research projects at IITGN, (b) be mentored by an IITGN faculty
and (c) participate in laboratory development and new elective
courses. This programme aims to help other institutes create
better teaching and research environments.

Support to the Institute
Shri Nandan Nilekani has donated Rs 25 lakhs to initiate the Nilekani Fund at IIT Gandhinagar to support initiatives of
excellence and pursue areas of high priority at the Institute. Shri Nilekani, a visionary, is an alumnus of IIT Bombay, and
currently heads the Unique Identification Authority of India (UIDAI) project of the Government of India. A co-founder of
NASSCOM and of Infosys Technologies Ltd, Shri Nilekani served as the CEO of Infosys from 2002-2007. In 2006, Shri
Nilekani was conferred with the Padma Bhushan by the Government of India.
Due to active support of Ms Asha Jadeja, wife of late Prof. Rajeev Motwani, the Rajiv Motwani Foundation
has initiated Motwani Fund at IITGN with a donation of 50,000 US Dollars. Prof. Motwani was an alumnus
of IIT Kanpur and did his Ph. D. at the University of California, Berkeley. As a professor of Computer Science
at Stanford University, he did pioneering research in the areas of web search, robotics, and theoretical
computer science. Ms Jadeja, originally from Ahmedabad, did her graduate studies at USC, UC Berkeley, and
Stanford University. She is actively involved in philanthropic initiatives in education in India and in USA. She also mentors many startups in the bay area. The Motwani Fund will support excellence and high-priority initiatives at the Institute.

Activities at IITGN
Counseling Service
The Institute has set-up a multi-tier Counseling Service with Prof.
Sharmita Lahiri as the Head of the Counseling Service, to help
students deal with the stresses of student life and for their
emotional and psychological well-being. Ms Jasbirkaur Thadani,
the full-time Psychological Counselor along with a number of
carefully selected Student Guides and Faculty Counselors ensure
that a student can avail this service without fear, reservations, or
prejudice. The discussions between the students and the
Psychological Counselor or any of the other counselors, is kept
strictly confidential.

Reaching Out
Prof. Sudhir K. Jain and Prof. Jaison Manjaly visited the ISCTE
– Lisbon University Institute (May 24-29, 2011) on the invitation
of the Rector. A number of academic activities of mutual interest
were discussed, and an MOU for academic collaborations signed.
In Lisbon, Prof. Jain, as the Executive Vice President of the
International Association for Earthquake Engineering, also met
with the organizing committee of the 15th World Conference on
Earthquake Engineering, scheduled to be held in Lisbon during
September 24-28, 2012 and reviewed preparations.

Prof. Sudhir K. Jain participated in the 19th CAETS Convention
on “Engineering Analysis and Management to Reduce Risks” in
Mexico City as a member of the delegation headed by the
President of the Indian National Academy of Engineering (June
27 - July 1, 2011). He also delivered a lecture on “The Road to
Seismic Safety with Particular Reference to the Developing
Countries”. Prof. Jain took this opportunity to visit University of
California at Berkeley and at Los Angeles, University of Southern
California, and the California Institute of Technology to develop
institutional collaborations and to attract faculty candidates. He
signed an MOU with Caltech on exchange of students. During

this visit, Prof. Jain also sought support of and partnerships with
several influential individuals and organizations in San Francisco,
Los Angeles, Mexico and Chicago.
Prof. Sudhir K. Jain has been chosen a member of the Working
Group on Technical Education to formulate the Twelfth Five
Year Plan (2012-17) by the Planning Commission.

Student and Staff Activities
IIT Gandhinagar initiates Summer Camp for B. Tech. Students
The Institute organized a four-week Summer Camp for IITGN undergraduate
students that started on June 15, 2011. This initiative gives the students a
platform to express their creativity and originality. Activities at the camp
include engineering design challenges such as designing a smart cane for the
blind, theatre and Salsa dancing classes, various sports activities, a reading
circle on books of historical significance, and workshops organized by
industry professionals.

Undergraduate Student Presents Paper in USA
Yogesh Goyal, a fourth year B. Tech. (Chemical Engineering) student presented a research paper Statistics-based model
for prediction of chemical biosynthesis yield from saccharomyces cerevisiae at the 111th general meeting of the
American Society for Microbiology, May 21-24, 2011, New Orleans, USA. His visit was supported by Department of
Science and Technology, IIT Gandhinagar, and MAGEEP, Washington University, St Louis.
Student Internship
Summer internships are considered an important part of education. The experience gained during the internships helps students in
planning their future and in getting a sense of the real world outside academia. The Institute actively helps students in finding
internships in industry and in educational institutes. During summer 2011, 89 students did their internships in industry and another 55
in reputed educational institutes. Twenty four students went abroad to places such as USA, France, Germany, Switzerland, UK,
Poland, and Singapore while several others are in Indian industries or scientific and educational institutes.

Halla Bol
Halla Bol, a unique all night intra-college sports festival (March 25, 2011 - April 3,
2011), attracted very enthusiastic participation of students with 1700 entries for 11 fun
games: Futsal, Touch Rugby, Half-Court Basketball, Kabbadi, Dodgeball, Gully
Cricket, 7 Stones, Scrabble, Anti-Chess, Kho-Kho and Tug of War. It was inaugurated
by the Director at the ceremony consisting of musical performance, march past and
lighting of the flame. A team of over 30 organizers and 60 volunteers worked hard for
the smooth running of the games, that went on until well past midnight. Halla Bol is
envisaged as an annual fun event in IITGN. The event was coordinated by Maisum
Ali Dairkee, Hare Lal Mahato, Bhargav Thadem and Mr Avik Bhandari.
