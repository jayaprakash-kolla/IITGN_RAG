Dear well-wishers of IIT Gandhinagar,
As we bid farewell to 2025 and prepare to welcome a promising new
year, it is worthwhile to take a moment to reflect on 2025 and all that
it brought for IIT Gandhinagar. This year was marked by several
significant milestones for the Institute across various fronts. From
setting new benchmarks in education and innovation to fostering
meaningful collaborations and driving societal impact, 2025 has been
a year of growth, recognition and impactful initiatives for the Institute.
With a vibrant community of over 3,200 students, including a rapidly
strengthening research ecosystem with 200+ new PhD scholars joining
the Institute last year and nearly 100 PhD students graduating in
2025, along with 148 career faculty members, 169 regular staff
members, 77 postdoctoral fellows and 24 newly advertised
administrative and support positions, the Institute continues on a
strong and inspiring growth path.

Campus Development

The past year marked an important phase in IITGN’s campus
development and sustainability journey. A key milestone was the
finalisation of the consultant for architectural planning and design of
academic and administrative buildings, the research park, student
hostels and associated infrastructure, strengthening the foundation
for the Institute’s future expansion.
IITGN received several prestigious national recognitions for its
campus initiatives. The Institute was awarded the Eat Right Campus
Award (Exemplary Category) by FSSAI, reaffirming its commitment to
health, hygiene, and sustainable food practices. The Student Hostel
Project was recognised for Architectural Excellence by PHDCCI,
highlighting design innovation in educational infrastructure.
Sustainability-led development remained a central focus, with the
Guest House, Hostels and Director’s Residence receiving a 4-Star
GRIHA provisional rating, underscoring IITGN’s emphasis on green
building practices. In the domain of water conservation, the Institute
achieved national recognition by securing the top rank (Joint Winner)
in the National Water Awards for excellence in inside-campus water
resource management.
Further enriching campus life and community engagement, IITGN
inaugurated Gujarat’s first Gen-Z themed Post Office on campus and
was honoured with the Bhumi Campus Award for its waste management
initiatives, reinforcing the Institute’s position as a model eco-friendly
campus.

Academics & Research

The Institute strengthened interdisciplinary learning and global
engagement through the launch of the BTech–MS Dual Degree
Programme with Iowa State University (ISU), the proposal of an
MTech in Digital Governance integrating technology, policy, ethics

areas, including the Master of Design (MDes) programme focused on
innovation and real-world impact. In line with NEP 2020, first-year

through MoUs with national and international institutions. IITGN

IITGN continues to strengthen its innovation and industry ecosystem
through the expanding Research Park and entrepreneurship
programmes. The Park hosts 22 companies, including four new
entrants—Firelynk Protech, Specific Fire Protection, SPEC Finance
(IFSC) and NOT DRONES INNOVATIONS—and is in discussions with
Tata Group and Adani Group. It supports sectors such as fire safety,
fintech, biotech, agritech, electronics, defence, energy, construction,
aerospace and Skill India 4.0. Startups under IIEC gained national
recognition, grants, collaborations, and funded projects. Key events
included the Deep Tech Venture Summit, Finnovate Hack, and
VidyutAI Hackathon, while entrepreneurship courses and workshops
saw strong participation. Under NIDHI–PRAYAS, over 100 innovators
participated, with six teams receiving grants. The Institute filed 28
patents, received 4 and added 10 more under Invent@IITGN 2025.
IITGN also launched ORBIT, its flagship bi-monthly industry meet,
with the inaugural edition focused on biomedical engineering.
IITGN celebrated its 14th Convocation Ceremony, conferring degrees
on 668 graduating students and recognising excellence across
academics, research, innovation, leadership, social service, sports,
arts, and culture through institutional awards and medals.

Visitors at IITGN

IITGN hosted several high-level international academic, industry,
and government delegations during the year to strengthen global
collaboration and research engagement. The Institute organised the
‘Japan Meets India’ Summit, bringing together faculty from leading
Japanese universities, representatives from JICA and JETRO, industry
leaders, and officials from the Department of Science and Technology
to explore partnerships in academics, research, and innovation.
Strengthening India–Japan engagement further, IITGN hosted the
Vice Governor of Iwate Prefecture, Japan, along with delegations
from industry and development agencies, to explore collaboration in
semiconductor research, skill development, student exchange, and
industry partnerships. The Institute also hosted the India–Japan
Talent Bridge Career Seminar, connecting students with leading
Japanese organisations and industry representatives.
Expanding industry and policy linkages, IITGN hosted Taiwan Day,
signing an MoU with the Taiwan Chamber of Commerce in Maharashtra
to collaborate on industrial research, innovation, internships, and

Philanthropic Support

We remain grateful to all our well-wishers whose generous support
continues to strengthen the Institute and contribute to the growth
and development of our students. During the year, IITGN received
philanthropic support exceeding ₹22 crore from individuals and
organisations, reflecting sustained trust in its mission. Key benefactors
include Dr Surendra C Sheth, Shri Parimal Karani, Dr Jagdish
Patel, Dr Hemant Kanakia, Shri Ruyintan Mehta, Mr Navin Doshi,
Shri B V Jagadeesh and the Desai Foundation, along with CSR
support from leading organisations across industry.
Alumni engagement remained strong with the establishment of several
new scholarships, including the Sugunamma and Sarojamma
Scholarship (Manikanta Mandlem), NeenJa Scholarship (Dr Suman
Kumari), SN Goenka and Elaichi Devi Goenka Scholarship (Naman
Bansal), Rita Kumari Sinha and Sanjay Kumar Scholarships
(Saurya Prakash Sinha) and the Sadhana and Mukesh Jain and
Sangeeta and Balesh Jain Scholarships instituted by Prof Sudhir K
Jain, Founding Director, IITGN.
Major philanthropic milestones include the renaming of Lecture Halls
101 and 102 as the Surendra C Sheth and Jayshree S Sheth Learning
Theatres, two classrooms in Academic Block 10 as the Jagdish Patel
and Harsha Patel Learning Theatres, the establishment of the P P
Sukumaran Scholarship by alumnus Jithin Prabha and the
renaming of the Sports Complex as the Hiralal R Karani Sports
Complex, supported by the Karani family.

Partnerships and Opportunities

This year, IITGN strengthened its global and national collaborations
through multiple MoUs and strategic partnerships to foster research,
education and innovation. IITGN signed an MoU with Homi Bhabha
National Institute (HBNI) to support co-supervision of MTech and
PhD students, joint coursework, faculty exchanges, and collaborative
research programs. Collaborations with Vietnamese universities—
Ho Chi Minh City University of Economics and Finance (UEF) and
Ho Chi Minh City University of Technology (HUTECH)—and Queen’s
University Belfast focused on academic exchanges, joint research
and innovation partnerships.
IITGN also advanced industry and defence collaboration through
MoUs with Canara Bank to promote entrepreneurship, Tata
Electronics Pvt Ltd for semiconductor R&D, CPWD for building
construction technology, iACE for automotive research, Bosch Global
for research and training and the Indian Army for defence innovation.
