Paramedic at IIT Gandhinagar
Indian Institute of Technology Gandhinagar invites applications from dedicated and skilled medical
professionals looking for an exciting opportunity to make a positive impact on a vibrant community.
The institute is offering the two positions of Paramedic staff (Program Assistant II) on contract
basis, initially for a period of one year, with the possibility of extension based on the incumbent’s
satisfactory performance and the institute’s requirements.
About IIT Gandhinagar:
IIT Gandhinagar is a premier institute dedicated to excellence in education, research, and innovation.
It is known for fostering an environment that encourages interdisciplinary learning, cutting-edge
research, and the holistic development of students. As a Paramedic, one will have the opportunity to
play a crucial role in supporting the health and well-being of the 3500+ campus community.
