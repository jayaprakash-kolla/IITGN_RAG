IIT Gandhinagar Foundation
IIT Gandhinagar Foundation, a charitable trust, has now been
registered in the USA. This was initiated by three US-based friends of
IIT Gandhinagar with the sole aim of supporting the Institute through
philanthropic financial contributions from US-based well-wishers.
The Foundation will enable them to make US tax-exempt donations to
IITGN. The three Directors of the Foundation are:

Shri Raj Mashruwala holds an MS from the University of
California, Berkeley (1976) and B.Tech. from IIT Bombay (1975).
He has co-founded a number of technology companies: TIBCO
Software Inc.; YieldUp International; Media Computer
Technology Inc.; and Consilium Inc. Mr Mashruwala is an active
angel investor through Mashruwala Investments and is mentor/advisor to
several early startup ventures. He is also a Director of IIT Bombay Heritage Fund,
Charter Member of The Indus Entrepreneur (TiE), and Commissioner, Public
Library, City of Palo Alto in USA.

Shri Bipin Shah holds an MS from the University of Wisconsin,
Madison, and a BEE (Honours) from Jadavpur University. He has
held senior positions in several high-tech companies, and has
been founder of some of them. He is currently the CEO and
Chairman of Kovair Software based in Milpitas, CA which has a
Development Center in Kolkata. He is active with a number of non-profit
organizations: The Indus Entrepreneurs (TiE); Ahimsa Center at the California
Polytechnic University, Pomona; Cupertino Education Endowment Foundation;
the Jain Center of Northern California.

Shri Abhay Bhushan is the Former President of IIT Kanpur
Alumni Association and was the first President of Pan IIT-USA. An
internet pioneer, he developed FTP and the early versions of email standards. A Silicon Valley entrepreneur, he founded several
companies, including Portola Communications and Yield UP
International, and mentored others. He received his B.Tech. from IIT Kanpur
(1965), and Master's degrees in Electrical Engineering and Management from
MIT. He is the joint holder of a dozen US patents.

S. C. Mehrotra Scholarships
Shri S. C. Mehrotra, the Managing Director of Mehro

First Major Gift to IIT Gandhinagar
Shri Raj Mashruwala, one of the Founder Directors of the IIT

Gandhinagar Foundation in the US, has made a personal gift of US$
100,000 to the Institute to support and encourage excellence among
the faculty and students. The Institute gratefully acknowledges the
gift from Shri Mashruwala. It also acknowledges the support from IIT
Bombay Heritage Fund and the IIT Bombay administration for
channeling this gift to IITGN.
