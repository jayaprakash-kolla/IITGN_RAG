INSTITUTE HIGHLIGHTS
161% INCREASE IN CSR SUPPORT

In a significant milestone for the advancement of education, research, and
community development, IITGN witnessed a remarkable 161% increase
in Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) support during FY 2023-24, as
compared to FY 2022-23. This substantial surge in CSR funding reflects a
growing recognition of IITGN’s commitment to excellence in education,
research, and societal impact. With the generous contributions from
corporate donors, IITGN continues to expand its reach and enhance its
programmes aimed at fostering innovation, entrepreneurship, and social
responsibility.

13TH CONVOCATION

IITGN hosted its 13th Convocation ceremony on June 29, 2024 at the
Mahatma Mandir Convention & Exhibition Centre in Gandhinagar. The
Institute conferred degrees to 530 students. Shri Rishad Premji, Executive
Chairman of Wipro Limited, graced the occasion as chief guest. Shri
Sanjiv Puri, Chairman, Board of Governors of IITGN, was also present
during the occasion.
This year, 56 students were awarded various medals, including 40 gold
medals and 17 silver medals for excellence in various categories such as
academics, outstanding research, innovation, leadership, social service,
sports, arts and culture, among others. Prior to the convocation, an Award
Ceremony was held on June 28, 2024. During this event, 42 medalists
received their medals. IITGN also conferred the Institute Fellow awards
to Mr Mahesh R Patel, Former Principal, Vishwakarma Government
Engineering College, and Mr Ruyintan Mehta, Former President, IITGN
Foundation in the United States. The Institute Fellow Award honours
individuals who have made significant and lasting contributions to the
advancement of IITGN.

MoU WITH CITY UNIVERSITY OF LONDON

IITGN and City University of London entered into a
Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) on May 20, 2024,
to foster academic and research collaborations between
the two Institutions. This agreement will facilitate the
development of dual degree MSc programmes in Cyber
Security, and Financial Technology and Systems. The
MoU outlines plans to explore opportunities for staff
and student exchanges. The MoU was signed by Prof
Elisabeth Hill, Deputy President and Provost, City, University of London
and Prof Rajat Moona, Director, IITGN at City University of London.

ACTIVITIES AT IITGN
IITGN STUDENTS DEVELOP FULLYAUTONOMOUS VEHICLE

A number of IITGN students developed a fully-functional autonomous
vehicle that can operate without a driver. The vehicle was developed as
part of a one-of-a-kind course on Autonomous Vehicles. The students
transformed an electric golf cart into a state-of-the-art driverless vehicle
capable of navigating the campus from the hostels to the academic area
autonomously. The vehicle is equipped with hardware and software
modifications to make it capable of fully-autonomous operations
including lane detection, obstacle detection, automatic steering control,
and automatic braking and acceleration.

WORKSHOP ON PYTHON PROGRAMMING

The department of Computer Science & Engineering, organised
an immersive one-day workshop on ‘Python programming and AI
applications’ on Apr 5, 2024. This hands-on workshop provided proficiency
in Python programming fundamentals, and its application in AI in a
structured and interactive environment to participants. The participants
also experienced hands-on guided exercises and case studies.

The Summer Research Internship Programme (SRIP) kicked off at IITGN
on May 5, 2024. The 14th edition of this eight-week flagship programme
saw participation from talented students from various prestigious
institutes across the country. The interns spent eight weeks at IITGN
and carried out 134 research projects under the mentorship of IITGN
faculty members. The interns concluded their internship with a poster
presentation competition on July 9, 2024.

ACS IGNITION:
LAUNCHING SCIENCE INTO FUTURE

‘ACS IGNITION: Launching Science into future’ on Apr 13, 2024. The

TEDxIITGANDHINAGAR

IITGN hosted the fifth edition of Invention Factory, a unique programme
in inventing. This intensive six-week long programme was held from
May 27-July 5, 2024 at IITGN, June 3-July 12 at IIT Bombay, and from June
3-July 14 at IIT Jammu. The students from various IITs and NITs spent
six-weeks to conceive, prototype, and pitch unique inventions to address
various societal and consumer needs.

JEE OPEN HOUSE

IITGN hosted an online ‘JEE Open House’ for prospective BTech students
and their parents on June 11, 2024. Institute authorities cleared the doubts
of prospective students and provided first-hand guidance on campus life,
admissions, programmes, career opportunities, and more.

Academic Affairs, Dean of Student Affairs, Heads of Departments, Faculty
in-charge of Counselling Services, Faculty in-charge of Internships,
Faculty in-charge of Higher Education, IITGN Students and Alumni.
