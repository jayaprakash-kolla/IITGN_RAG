FOUNDATION PROGRAMME
6TH CONVOCATION CEREMONY

IIT Gandhinagar held its sixth convocation on August 5, 2017 in
which 195 students were awarded degrees: 124 BTech, 47 MSc,
5 MA, 4 PGDIIT and 14 PhD. Mr Adil Zainulbhai, Chairman,
Quality Council of India was the Chief Guest at the event. The
Institute awarded medals and awards to recognize achievements in
academics, innovation, social services, research, sports, leadership,
arts, and culture. Rishab Anand was awarded the President’s Gold
Medal for BTech, Kamal Tiwari was awarded the President’s Gold
Medal for MTech, and Sonali Abhay Parekh received the President’s
Gold Medal for MSc and MA batches. The Director’s Gold Medal
for BTech went to Rishabh Jain for his outstanding academic
performance.

From sports to treasure hunt, IITGN used an array of innovative
methods to groom its incoming batch of undergraduate students
during the five-week Foundation Programme (July 24 to Aug 27,
2017). The activities included sessions on story-telling, photography,
sketching, music, painting, making comic books, yoga, sports, heritage
walk, theatre activities, street cleaning and tree-planting. Talks on
leadership, gender sensitivity, ethics and value education were also
organized to encourage the students to open up their minds. The
programme concluded with Eureka, a cultural evening organized
by freshers’ where they showcased their talent and potential to the
rest of the IITGN community with performances of dance, drama,
music, poetry recitation, and other cultural activities. The programme
was coordinated by Prof Krishna Kanti Dey, Prof Kaustubh
Rane, Prof Manas Paliwal, Prof Manu Awasthi and Ms Poonam
Mutha.

Support for the Institute
PROF K V VENKATESHA MURTHY SCHOLARSHIP

Mrs Usha Murthy has set up a scholarship of Rs
50,000 per year for BTech students of Electrical
Engineering in honour of her late husband Prof K
V V Murthy. Prof Murthy was one of the pioneer
faculty members of IIT Gandhinagar, who joined the
newly established institute on December 29, 2009. He was a passionate
teacher with an infectious enthusiasm for the subjects that he taught.

CLASS-OF-2015 SCHOLARSHIPS

The Class-of-2015 alumni have set up undergraduate scholarships
of Rs 20,000 per year. This scholarship will be awarded initially to
one undergraduate student starting in 2017-18. The number of
scholarships shall increase by one every year till it reaches to four
scholarships by the year 2020-21.

AMALTHEA SCHOLARSHIPS
The Amalthea Scholarships of Rs 50,000 per year have been set up
utilizing the savings of annual technical event Amalthea organized
by IITGN students. The scholarship has been awarded initially to
one undergraduate student starting in 2016-17. The number of
scholarships shall increase by one every year till it reaches to four
scholarships by the year 2019-20.

RESEARCH PARK AT IITGN

Gujarat Urja Vikas Nigam Ltd (GUVNL) became the first company
to start its R&D Unit at IITGN Research Park. The Research Park
has been established at IITGN campus to boost industry academiacollaboration and innovation. The institute plans to build around
200,000 sq ft space for the research park in next couple of years.
However, it has identified and earmarked around 26,000 sq ft of
existing space to start the activities even before the construction of
permanent buildings would start.

SOLAR POWER IN INDIA:
PAST, PRESENT AND 2022
A book on Solar Power in India: Past,
Present and 2022 was launched by SRIP
Solar study team at IITGN on July 14, 2017.
Prof Juzer M Vasi, IIT Bombay was the
distinguished visitor on this occasion. The
book was released by Prof Sudhir K Jain,
Director IITGN and was presented to Prof
Vasi. Responding to the needs of the nation
for solar energy and driven by the national
solar mission target of 100 GW installed
solar power by 2022, students worked on
this project during summer break as their
contribution to this national endeavor. A
team of 8 Students including 2 students
from KIIT Bhubaneswar and University
of Jaffna (Srilanka) were guided by Prof K
Chelvakumar.
