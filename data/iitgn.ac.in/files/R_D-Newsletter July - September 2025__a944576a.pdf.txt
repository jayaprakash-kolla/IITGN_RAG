COVER STORY
In the third quarter, IITGN hosted R&D events that bolstered industry-academia collaborations and networks. The Institute
hosted Mr SASAKI Jun, Vice Governor of Iwate Prefecture, Japan, along with officials from Japan International Cooperation
Agency, Iwate Industry Promotion Center, and Gujarat State Electronics Mission. The visit focused on collaborating in
semiconductor research, skill development, and innovation, with plans for student exchanges, joint research, and industry
partnerships.
IITGN also conducted the Netherlands Hydrogen Innovation Mission Workshop to foster dialogue and
collaboration in hydrogen technology. The event attracted domain experts, policymakers, and industry leaders
from both India and the Netherlands, including representatives from key Dutch organisations like TNO and
the Port of Rotterdam Authority. Mr Nabil Taouati, Consul General of the Kingdom of the Netherlands in
Mumbai, highlighted the growing Indo-Dutch partnership across various sectors, including energy transition,
healthcare, and innovation.
Another flagship industry event conducted was ORBIT, IITGN’s Industry Meet, which focuses on building partnerships
with stakeholders from MSMEs, startups, and multinational companies. The second edition’s themes centred on
Electric Vehicle (EV) technologies, with the attendees addressing challenges in India’s EV sector and proposing use
cases and collaborative pathways to facilitate clean mobility.
