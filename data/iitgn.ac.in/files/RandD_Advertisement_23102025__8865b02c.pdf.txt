Indian Institute of Technology Gandhinagar (IITGN)
Advertisement No. OTH/R&D/12276/Advt0140 dt. 23.10.2025
Position: Program Associate II (Patent Officer)
Consolidated Salary Range: Rs. 50,000/- to Rs. 78,000/- per month
Employment Type: Contractual (initially for 01 year, extendable based on performance)
Office: Research and Development Office, IIT Gandhinagar
Reporting to: Dean of Research and Development
Key Responsibilities:


Facilitation and Support: Providing end-to-end assistance for patent filing, including priorart search, patentability assessment, drafting, documentation, and coordination with patent
attorneys.



Expedited Processing: Establishing a streamlined process and standardized workflow to
ensure that potential inventions are evaluated and filed promptly.



Awareness and Training: Organizing regular workshops, seminars, and awareness
programs on intellectual property rights, patent procedures, and technology
commercialization.



Faculty Support: Assisting faculty in identifying patentable outcomes from sponsored
research and consultancy projects and ensuring timely filing of invention disclosures.



Database and Monitoring: Maintaining a comprehensive institutional database of filed and
granted patents and tracking the progress of each application.

Qualifications and Experience:




Essential:
o Master’s degree in Law or Science or Engineering or related discipline.
o At least 02 years of post-qualification experience in patent filing, IP management,
or technology transfer in academic or R&D organizations.
Desirable:
o Qualification in Intellectual Property Law or Patent Agent Certification.
o Experience in drafting patent specifications and handling IP databases.

Skills and Competencies:
Strong understanding of patent laws, filing procedures, and IPR policies.
Excellent communication, documentation, and coordination skills.
Ability to conduct patent searches and analyze patentability.
Familiarity with innovation ecosystems and technology transfer processes.
