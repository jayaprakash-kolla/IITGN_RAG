Minimum Eligibility Criteria for PhD Programmes

(Semester I, AY: 2026-27)

Institute Eligibility Criteria

“Minimum of 55% marks (50% marks for SC/ST/PwD) or 5.5 CPI (5.0 CPI for SC/ST/PwD) in
postgraduate degree programme or in 4-years bachelor degree programme in appropriate branches”.
Above\ mentioned eligibility criteria is minimum at Institute level. The department-wise eligibility
criteria is given in the table below. The departments may also use higher cut-off levels for shortlisting
esides giving due consideration to the way questions are answered in the application form. Candidates
belonging to SC/ST/PwD categories would be eligible for relaxations, as per norms, in the selection
criteria.
