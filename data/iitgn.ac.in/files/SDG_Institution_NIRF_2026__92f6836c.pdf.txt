Activity Details: IIT Gandhinagar has implemented several measures to reduce energy consumption across its campus, which holds a 5-Star Green Campus rating. All buildings use energy-efficient LED lighting. Street and corridor lights operate automatically
based on time schedules, while motion sensors ensure lights in washrooms and passages switch off when not in use. The campus cooling systems are designed to optimize energy use according to demand. Dining halls are cooled using Passive Downdraft
Evaporative Cooling (PDEC), which uses air movement and mist with minimal electricity consumption. Guesthouses and hostels use a key-card system that automatically cuts off power when rooms are vacant. A rooftop solar power system meets about 4–5% of
the campus’s electricity needs, and solar water heaters reduce reliance on electric geysers. Sewage is treated using root zone treatment technology, and the treated water is reused for gardening.
