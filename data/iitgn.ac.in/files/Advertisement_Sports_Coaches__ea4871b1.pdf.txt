INDIAN INSTITUTE OF TECHNOLOGY GANDHINAGAR

Part -Time Sports Coaches
The Indian Institute of Technology Gandhinagar (IITGN) invites applications from
passionate and highly motivated individuals for the position of part-time sports coaches
in Football (one position) and Swimming (two positions). The institute is offering these
three positions of part-time sports coaches on contract basis, initially for a period of
one year, with the possibility of extension based on the incumbent’s satisfactory
performance and the institute’s requirements.

Job Responsibilities/ Duties:
●
●
●
●
●
●
●

Sports coaching students and staff.
Preparing a sports coaching plan and its execution.
Conducting Physical Education (PE) Classes.
Responsible for maintenance of fields/courts and equipment.
Maintaining records related to the associated sports.
Proactive engagement of students and staff in sports activities.
Other works assigned to by the sports head.

Timing:
Swimming: 3-4 Hours in the morning (1 position)
3-4 Hours in the evening (1 position)
Football: 3-4 Hours in the evening (1 position)

Required Qualifications
‒

Bachelor’s degree in physical education preferably 02 years of relevant coaching
experience.

Desirable:
‒ Diploma in sports coaching from NSNIS
‒ Specialization in coaching for Football and Swimming.

General Information:

The position is offered for one year from the date of joining, extendable on a yearly basis
based on performance.
