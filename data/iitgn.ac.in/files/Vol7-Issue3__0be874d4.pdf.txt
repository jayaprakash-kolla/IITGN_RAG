IITGN Foundation Appoints
Executive Director

IIT Gandhinagar Foundation, a US-based charitable
trust, announced the appointment of Mr Ravi Mistry as
its first Executive Director.
Mr Mistry will primarily be responsible for
designing and managing the Foundation’s
worldwide capital campaign with primary focus on the US. He will also serve as a liaison
between donors and IITGN on important
initiatives supported by the donors. Mr Mistry was previously a member of the founding
team of Virident Systems, a technology
startup based in the Silicon Valley.

USAID support

A Joint Declaration of Intent was signed on
Jan 23, 2015, ahead of President Obama’s India visit, between the Ministry of Human Resource Development (MHRD) and the United
States Agency for International Development
(USAID) for providing support to Indian Institutes of Technology. The declaration calls
for intensifying collaborations in Research and
Development (R&D) and entrepreneurship and identifies IIT Gandhinagar as the initial IIT
for support. A team of three USAID representatives, comprising Mr Mitch Kirby, senior
education advisor; Dr Sheila E Desai, India senior science and technology advisor; and Dr
Eric M Johnson, senior research economist with RTI International, visited IITGN on Feb
10, 2015 to assess institutional needs and discuss strategies with senior IITGN administrators.
Their discussions covered a wide range of possible areas of support including strengthening research capabilities, student and faculty exchanges, leadership development, networking,
communication strategies, and entrepreneurship.

Academic Advisory Council

The 4th Academic Advisory Council meeting
was held on Jan 5, 2015. The agenda included
improving faculty research productivity, support for postgraduate students, moving to the
permanent campus, and developing strategic
relationships with foreign universities.
A delegation of four members from the
Japan Advanced Institute of Science and
Technology visited IITGN during Jan
16-17, 2015 to explore opportunities
of collaboration. Prof Tetsuo Asano,
President, JAIST said, “We are planning
to send a team of students to study at
IITGN and also look at its interdisciplinary studies.” Other members included Prof Hiroyuki Iida, Prof Shungo
Kawanishi, and Prof Ryo Maezono.
Prof Luís Antero Reto,
Rector, ISCTE, University Institute of Lisbon
visited the institute during Feb 15-20, 2015 to
strengthen academic and research relations between the two institutes.

Leadership Conclave

The 5th Leadership Conclave was held on Jan 6, 2015 to guide the Institute in its short-term
and long-term strategies. The conclave discussed ideas on fund raising initiatives by the Institute, engaging with professional bodies and organizations, and the creation of a research park
at IITGN’s permanent campus.
