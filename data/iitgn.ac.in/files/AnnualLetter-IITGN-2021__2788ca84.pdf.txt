Dear well-wishers of IIT Gandhinagar,
Welcome to the sixth edition of our annual update letter.
While this year began with a devastating second wave of COVID,
it was also one of hope and rebuilding. Our campus provides a

safe environment as almost all community members have

been vaccinated. Most of our students are back on campus.
While our default plan for the new year remains focused on

restoring to normal, we remain prepared to face any unexpected
turn of events as well.

The Second Wave

During the second wave, the Institute converted its newly

constructed guest house into an isolation-cum-COVID Care

Facility to treat COVID positive community members with mild to

moderate symptoms. Based on the defining institutional principles
of inclusivity and empathy, the facility provided isolation

and health support to the entire IITGN community,

including students, project staff, faculty, admin staff, contractual
workers such as drivers, mess staff, housekeeping staff,

gardeners, security personnel, and their family members. More

than 240 COVID patients, including 121 students, were treated
at the facility, with a peak of 95 in mid-April.

The facility provided basic medical services, such as temperature

and oxygen readings, advice from doctors and nurses, medicines,

RT-PCR tests, nutritious meals, as well as hard-to-access health
services such as oxygen concentrators and cylinders. Institute

medical staff and ambulance services were available 24x7. The
facility also helped patients find beds in local hospitals when

needed. Moreover, counselling services offered psychological
support to patients, medical staff, and their families. The

Institute released a widely-cited Whitepaper outlining
its experience and lessons from the development and

management of a community COVID Care Facility on the campus.
More recently, the institute has started medical camps at the

construction workers’ colonies, which are held on a weekly and
rotational basis. Every camp serves 40-50 workers and their
family members.

Partnerships and Opportunities

The Institute signed Memorandum of Understandings with

NIT Sikkim and NIT Hamirpur to facilitate student

exchange, academic cooperation, and research collaboration

among faculty members of both the institutes. The institute also

entered into a Memorandum of Understanding with the University
of Durham, United Kingdom, for cultural, educational and
scientific cooperation.

Having developed an excellent and interdisciplinary research

ecosystem, the Institute witnessed a significant increase

in faculty and postdoctoral researchers this year. In
particular, the proportion of postdoctoral researchers at the

Institute is currently almost 75% of the total faculty strength as
against ~50% in December 2020 and ~35% in 2019. We were

quick to adapt our faculty recruitment processes in the light of
the pandemic, as a result of which we were able to make

substantial progress in faculty recruitment this year. During the
year, 16 new career faculty members joined the Institute,

compared to an average of 8 new faculty additions per year in the
last 12 years.

The Institute has launched several programmes to encourage
young researchers and drive cutting-edge, collaborative and

interdisciplinary research. The Institute introduced the Early

Career Fellowship to encourage outstanding post-doctoral
applicants to join the Institute, and also initiated the Vikram
Sarabhai Research Fellowship, which provides students

who submit their thesis within four years an enhanced fellowship.
We have also substantially increased our science communication
efforts to make the research developments at the Institute

accessible to the general audience. This has culminated in over

150 updates across our social media platforms, and the launch of
the first edition of Research Spectra covering over 25 major
research stories.

Campus Development

Campus development is progressing well and the Guest

House, Central Arcade, Open Air Theatre, 1 BHK, 2 BHK, studio
apartments, and the new hostel blocks were completed. The

Bank, Post Office, and the Medical Centre are operational from
their new location in the Central Arcade. Construction of the

Research Park, and Sports Complex is also nearing completion.
