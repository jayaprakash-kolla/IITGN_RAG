LAUNCH OF GIFT INTERNATIONAL
FINTECH INSTITUTE

12TH ACADEMIC ADVISORY COUNCIL
AND 13TH LEADERSHIP COUNCIL

Gujarat International Finance Tec-City (GIFT City), in partnership with
Ahmedabad University, IITGN and UC San Diego, has launched the GIFT
International Fintech Institute (GIFT IFI) on Jan 17, 2025. This space aims
to cultivate training, research and innovation in the fintech ecosystem.
The inauguration was done by the Hon’ble Chief Minister of Gujarat,
Shri Bhupendra Patel, in the presence of dignitaries like Mr Tapan Ray,
Managing Director and Group CEO, GIFT City; Ms Mona K Khandhar,
Principal Secretary, Department of Science and Technology, Government
of Gujarat; Ms Aarti Mehra, Deputy Country Director, India Resident
Mission, ADB; Prof Rajat Moona, Director, IITGN; Prof Pankaj Chandra
Vice Chancellor and Chairman, Board of Management, Ahmedabad
University; Prof Rajesh Gupta, Interim Dean, School of Computing,
Information and Data Sciences, Distinguished Professor and HDSI
Founding Director, among others.

IITGN hosted the 12th Academic Advisory Council (AAC) on Jan 10, 2025.
The prestigious gatherings brought together around 53 distinguished
academicians, industry leaders and experts from various domains
globally, who engaged in discussions for shaping the future initiatives of
the Institute. The 13th Leadership Conclave (LC) of IITGN brought these
forces together under the same roof on Jan 11, 2025, to deliberate upon
the Institute’s short, medium and long-term strategic issues.

ACTIVITIES AT IITGN
CURIOSITY CARNIVAL
AND CONCLAVE

The Curiosity Conference and Carnival was
hosted on Jan 18-19, 2025, and was organised
by IITGN’s Curiosity Lab, in collaboration
with the Center for Curiosity, Art@IITGN, and
the Social Action and Policy Lab. The event
featured keynote talks, panel discussions,
poster presentations etc, and witnessed the
participation of around 3500 students from
schools in and around Gandhinagar.

SATELLITE EVENT OF PANIIT
WORLD OF TECHNOLOGY
(PIWOT) 2025

IITGN hosted the satellite event of the
prestigious PanIIT World of Technology
(PIWOT) 2025 on Jan 21, 2025, bringing together
100+ prominent industrialists. The event served
as a platform for innovation, collaboration, and
discussions on the role of technology in shaping
the future.

NATIONAL EDUCATION POLICY
2020: PERSPECTIVES AND BEST
PRACTICES

IITGN organised a high-impact meeting on
‘National Education Policy 2020: Perspectives
and Best Practices’ on Jan 18, 2025 aimed
at supporting Gujarat’s state universities
in effectively implementing NEP 2020. The
meeting had 16 attendees including Prof
Nithin George, Dean, Academic Affairs, IITGN,
representatives from the Commissionerate of
Technical Education, Government of Gujarat,
and participants from the various universities
from Gujarat.

VIRASAT 2025

IITGN in partnership with the Spark Minda
Foundation hosted a comprehensive Camp for
Empowerment of People with Disabilities from
Mar 5-9, 2025. The initiative aims to empower

certificates, among other services.

(CBSE) and the Centre for Creative Learning

3030 Eklavya Online Programme – an initiative
aimed to simplify complex concepts, ignite
curiosity, and inspire creativity in students and
teachers.

VACUUM DAY

IITGN, in partnership with Pfeiffer Vacuum,
organised ‘Vacuum Day’ on Feb 7, 2025. The
event emphasised on the importance of vacuum
technology, comprising theoretical lectures
and practical demonstrations. 100 participants
across educational institutions in Gujarat
participated in the event.

COLAB 2025

Feb 22, 2025. Aimed at fostering sustainable

NATIONAL SCIENCE DAY 2025

IITGN celebrated the National Science Day 2025
on Feb 28, 2025, marking the anniversary of the
discovery of the Raman Effect, and promoting
a spirit of scientific inquiry across the nation.
The event attracted more than 500 school
students from Gandhinagar and surrounding
areas, infusing the campus with infectious
enthusiasm.

IITGN hosted Virasat 2025 - a SPIC MACAY
event, organised by the SPIC MACAY Heritage
Club at IITGN, from Mar 22 - 30, 2025. As
part of this initiative, a series of concerts and
workshops were organised, including the
performances of Vidushi A Kanyakumari, Dr
Kaluram Bamniya, Pandit Ulhas Kashalkar,
Pandit Hariprasad Chaurasia and Vidushi
Ashwini Bhide Deshpande.

HACK THE FUTURE 2025

IITGN hosted the ‘Hack The Future –
Innovating Intelligent Data Insights’, a 36-hour
national hackathon designed to explore the
transformative power of data for addressing
societal challenges, from Mar 21-23, 2025. Held
as a collaborative venture between the Ministry
of Statistics and Programme Implementation
(MoSPI) and the IITGN Innovation and
Entrepreneurship Center (IIEC), this hackathon
brought together 19 student teams from among
1,000 applicants from across the country.
