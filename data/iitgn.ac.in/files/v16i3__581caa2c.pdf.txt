Devvrat, was the guest of honour for the event at IITGN. IITGN’s new
Academic Buildings house the Institute Library, classrooms, laboratories,
faculty chambers, and a Maker Space. One Jal Mandap and 4 lawn tennis

GENEROUS ALUMNI CONTRIBUTION

11TH AAC MEETING

The 11th Academic Advisory Council (AAC) of IITGN, held on Jan 8, 2024,
brought together around 20 academicians from India and overseas and
several faculty members from IITGN to debate and share their expertise
on significant matters for the Institute’s growth in the coming years. Some
focus areas included faculty evaluation and growth, promoting student
well-being, holistic staff development, new academic programmes and
initiatives, and fostering industry-academia partnerships.

MoU WITH UNIVERSITY OF SAN DIEGO

IITGN entered into a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) with
the University of San Diego, USA, on Jan 12, 2024, for collaboration in
various academic, research, capacity building, training, and faculty/staff
development activities. The MoU was signed and exchanged by Prof Chell
Roberts, Dean, Shiley-Marcos School of Engineering (SMSE), University
of San Diego, and Prof S P Mehrotra, Professor-in-Charge, External
Relations, IITGN, in the presence of Prof G B Singh, Academic Director,
University of San Diego, and Prof Rajat Moona, Director, IITGN.

COLLABORATION WITH AIT, THAILAND

IITGN signed three agreements with Asian Institute of Technology (AIT),
Thailand on Jan 8, 2024 to collaborate in academic, research, capacity
building, training, and faculty development activities. The institutes
will jointly develop Master’s and Doctoral double degree programmes,
joint academic programmes, research projects, organise seminars and
conferences, specialised trainings for staff and faculty, and short-term
internships for students.

PARTNERSHIP WITH NIT JAMSHEDPUR

IITGN has inked an agreement with the National Institute of Technology
(NIT) Jamshedpur on Mar 4, 2024 to facilitate academic and research
collaboration between the institutes. The agreement includes student
exchange for summer research internships, semester study opportunities
at IITGN for NIT Jamshedpur students, Start Early PhD programme
opportunity, research engagement for NIT Jamshedpur faculty at IITGN,
and other academic collaborations.

ACTIVITIES AT IITGN
IITGN AWARDED THE ACS
STUDENT CHAPTER

The American Chemical Society (ACS)
chartered IITGN as an ACS International

Student Chapter in Feb 2024. As members, IITGN students can organise
conferences and interact with renowned scientists. The student leaders
include Deepika Khasa, Anjila Siddiqui, Tarisha Gupta, Gaurav Rai,
Haritha D, and Snehanjali Behera. Prof Sivapriya Kirubakaran is the

the student chapter.

INDIA-JAPAN INNOVATION
SYMPOSIUM

WORKSHOP ON STUDENTS’
MENTAL WELL-BEING

IITGN hosted a two-day workshop on students’
mental well-being titled ‘Building Scalable
Systems for Student Wellbeing in Residential
Programs’ on Mar 4-5, 2024. The workshop
aimed to create robust systems in place in
higher education institutes to ensure early
identification of mental health issues, effective
strategies, and best practices to foster supportive
environments prioritising students’ mental
health and emotional well-being. Mental health
professionals, psychologists, psychotherapists,
neurologists, and academicians participated in
the workshop.

SCIENCE CAMP FOR GIRL
STUDENTS

IITGN organised a two-day residential science
camp for 91 girl students from Jawahar
Navodaya Vidyalayas (JNVs) of Gujarat region
on Jan 8-9, 2024. This programme was organised
as part of the DST-Vigyan Jyoti Initiative and
aimed to develop students’ experimental,
critical thinking, and problem-solving skills,
and inspire them to pursue their curiosity and
creativity in science.
