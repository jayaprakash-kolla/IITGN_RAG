IIT Gandhinagar
Visiting Students Research
Engagement Programme
Applications are invited for the
newly launched Visiting Students
Research Engagement Programme
of IIT Gandhinagar for Semester II of
Academic Year 2026-27. The programme
enables select final year students from IITs/
IISERs/IISc and NITs to pursue semester-long
internship at IIT Gandhinagar in the form of a research
project, working along with IIT Gandhinagar faculty.

SALIENT FEATURES

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA

● Selected students can spend
a semester at IIT Gandhinagar
working on a research project
with IIT Gandhinagar faculty
members.

● Final-year students in the BTech
(4-year programme)/BS (4-year
programme)/Integrated Master
of Science (5-year programmes)
from IITs/IISc or

● The students will be considered
visiting students at IIT
Gandhinagar and must register
for Research Engagement credits
at IIT Gandhinagar.

● Final-year students in the BTech
(4-year programme)/BS (4-year
programme)/BS-MS (5-year
programme) from NITs/IISERs
and

● Research Engagement credits
can be earned in 4-credit
modules (IN 497 - Research
Engagement), with a student
permitted to earn a maximum
of 4 credits in one quad and
a maximum of 16 credits of
IN 497 during their stay at IIT
Gandhinagar [A semester is
divided into 4 quads].

● having more than 8.0 CPI/CGPA
at the end of their pre-final year
are eligible to apply.

● IN 497 will be graded using the
usual letter grades by the host
department at IIT Gandhinagar
at the end of each quad.

Opportunity for
Semester-long
Internship at IIT
Gandhinagar for
final year students of
IITs/IISc/IISERs/NITs

FINANCIAL SUPPORT
● Students will be provided a
stipend of Rs. 5000/- for every
quad for which they have
secured a grade of B or above in
IN 497.

FEES
● Students selected for the Visiting
Students Research Engagement
Programme need NOT pay any
tuition fees at IIT Gandhinagar
for the registration in IN 497
(Research Engagement) during
their stay.
● Hostel and mess fees are
applicable and need to be paid
by the student.
