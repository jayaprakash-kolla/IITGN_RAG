CII AWARD FOR INDUSTRY-ACADEMIA
PARTNERSHIP

IITGN’s green initiatives at its campus, under the theme of ‘Sanitation’ has
been recognised with the ‘HUDCO Award for Best Practices to Improve
the Living Environment 2023-24.’ The award was presented on World
Habitat Day 2024 at Vigyan Bhawan, New Delhi, on Oct 9, 2024. This
achievement showcases the Institute’s dedication to sustainability and the
green initiatives implemented on campus.

NEW JERSEY LT GOVERNOR AT IITGN

IITGN was awarded the CII Award for Industry-Academia Partnership
as the Gold Winner in the Government Institute Category for 2024. This
prestigious honour, instituted by the Confederation of Indian Industry
(CII), recognised institutions that excel in fostering collaborations with
industry to drive innovation and societal progress. IITGN has consistently
focused on leveraging these collaborations to address critical societal
challenges, from advancing environmental sustainability to promoting
education for the underprivileged.

NEENJA FELLOWSHIP

Suman Kumari, a BTech alumnus of 2017, has set up
the NeenJa Scholarship in honour of her parents, Neena
Kumari and Janak Raj. The scholarship, worth Rs 1

for financial needs, funding internships (domestic

opportunities for students. Dr Suman Kumari is a BTech Chemical
Engineering graduate of the class of 2017. After a brief work experience
following her graduation, she pursued advanced studies and recently
completed her PhD at the University of Connecticut. She will soon join

ACTIVITIES AT IITGN

INDIA KI KHOJ

IITGN organised a conference on ‘Culinary
Histories and Cultures: Insights from South
Asia’ on Oct 5-6, 2024. The conference allowed
historians and archaeologists to share their
thoughts on different culinary histories and
traditions of a region, and explored how food
intersects with identity, caste, migration, and
other social dynamics.

IITGN organised ‘India Ki Khoj: In Search of
Society, Culture, Economy, Science and Beyond’
from Dec 16-22, 2024. Nine undergraduate
students from Caltech University, accompanied
by Athena Castro and Vincent Ton from
the administrative staff, traveled to India to
participate in this initiative. They were joined
by a group of 14 students from IITGN. Together,
they engaged in a series of lectures, interactive
sessions, workshops, and visits, fostering a rich
exchange of ideas and experiences.

YUVA SANGAM PHASE V

SWACHHATA PAKHWADA 2024

CONFERENCE ON CULINARY
HISTORIES

IITGN organised the flag-off ceremony for the
Gujarat Delegation of ‘Yuva Sangam Phase
V’ on Nov 29, 2024. It also hosted the Kerala
delegation from Dec 5-9, 2024. The Kerala
contingent was introduced to the vivid cultural
landscape of Gujarat, and shown a glimpse of
its technological advancements. They were
made to visit the Statue of Unity, Akshardham,
Modhera Sun temple, and more.

BUILD YOUR OWN STARTUP

The IITGN Innovation and Entrepreneurship
Center successfully hosted a five-day workshop
titled, ‘Build your Startup with BV Jagadeesh’.
The programme, held from Oct 18-22, 2024,
was led by Mr BV Jagadeesh, a renowned
entrepreneur and angel investor. Designed
for aspiring entrepreneurs, students, and
early-stage startups, the sixth edition of the
workshop provided essential insights into the
entrepreneurial journey, covering product
development, market fit, sales strategies,
marketing, and fundraising.

SMART INDIA HACKATHON
2024

IITGN organised a series of impactful
events focused on promoting cleanliness,
sustainability, and raising awareness about
responsible practices under the Swachh
Bharat Mission initiative, as part of Swachhata
Pakhwada 2024. This included a plogging drive
that brought together over 110 participants,
including students, staff, and faculty members.
A photography and reel-making competition
was also organised as part of the programme.

NATIONAL WILDLIFE WEEK

Dr Kiran C Patel Centre for Sustainable
Development (KPCSD) at IITGN celebrated
the National Wildlife Week from Oct 2-8,
2024 with a series of events. The celebration
aimed to raise awareness and promote wildlife
conservation through various educational and
engaging activities. More than 200 people,
including IITGN students, staff, residents,
and college students from the Ahmedabad &
Gandhinagar region, participated in the weeklong celebration.

ACTIVITIES BY NYASA

NYASA – the community outreach programme
of IITGN aimed at supporting the children of
migrant construction workers, organised a
winter camp on Dec 20, 2024 through which,
54 students of Dashela and Dharampur were
given an informative tour of the library, made
part of a session by IITGN’s Astronomy club,
and invited to an art session on Zentangle art.
The highlight of the camp was the observation
of the sun through a specialised Dobsonian
8-inch telescope equipped with a solar filter.
Besides this, NYASA also celebrated Diwali and
Children’s day with great fervour on Oct 31 and
Nov 14 respectively.

BOOK RELEASE

IITGN hosted the book release of ‘Illuminating
Worlds: An Anthology of Classical Indian
Literature’, by Prof Srinivas Reddy on Oct 19,
2024. The book is an anthology that spans two
and a half millennia of Indian literary history
and includes passages from significant texts
such as the Vedas, Mahabharata, and Sangam
poems.
