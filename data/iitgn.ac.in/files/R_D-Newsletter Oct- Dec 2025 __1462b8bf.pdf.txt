COVER STORY
In the fourth quarter, IIT Gandhinagar (IITGN) hosted several R&D initiatives that strengthened industry–academia
collaboration, most notably the National Basecamp of Bharat Innovates 2026, an initiative of the Ministry of Education.
The event brought together nearly 400 shortlisted startups from across India, offering a platform to engage with investors,
industry partners, and researchers through pitching sessions and product exhibitions. The National Basecamp aimed to
identify high-potential startups and support their progression to the International Showcase in Nice, France, in 2026.
IITGN also organised the Deep Tech Venture Summit 2025, enabling deep-tech startups to present their innovations and
connect with the investment community. Over 35 startups from IITGN’s incubation ecosystem and the wider regional
landscape showcased solutions across sectors such as HealthTech, AI/FinTech, Defence, and Sustainability. The summit
attracted more than 30 senior representatives from leading venture capital firms, family offices, and angel investor
networks across India, fostering meaningful networking and knowledge exchange.
Additionally, the third edition of ORBIT, hosted at IITGN, convened researchers, startups, Micro, Small & Medium Enterprises (MSMEs), and industry leaders to discuss
India’s evolving solar technology landscape. Discussions highlighted Gujarat’s leadership in renewable energy, the need to strengthen domestic solar manufacturing, and the
importance of skilled manpower, open data infrastructure, policy support, and joint R&D initiatives to accelerate innovation.

INDIAN INSTITUTE OF SCIENCE (IISC)
