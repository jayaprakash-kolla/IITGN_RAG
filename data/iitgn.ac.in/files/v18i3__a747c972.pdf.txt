PROJECT SAMARTH

IIT Gandhinagar has launched Project SAMARTH (Silicon and Advanced
Semiconductor Manufacturing Research and Training Hub), a joint
initiative with the Government of Gujarat, MeitY and industry partners.
The project focuses on developing a skilled semiconductor workforce
through training, indigenous R&D and advanced device and process
development.

AI RESILIENCE AND COMMAND (ARC) CENTRE

IITGN inaugurated the AI
Resilience and Command
(ARC) Centre at its Research
Park on Feb 5, 2026, to
strengthen
data-driven
climate-risk management and
urban resilience. Combining
physics-based modelling with
AI, it enables real-time flood
assessment, scenario analysis and decision-making, advancing safer,
future-ready cities in line with Viksit Bharat 2047.

CBSE SIGNS MoU TO EXTEND
NATIONAL STEM PROGRAMME

On Jan 29, 2026, IITGN and CBSE signed an MoU to
continue the nationally recognised 3030 STEM and 3030
Eklavya programmes for five more years, strengthening
STEM teacher training across India. Led by IITGN’s
Centre for Creative Learning, the NEP 2020-aligned
initiative has engaged over 73,800 CBSE teachers, with the Eklavya series
crossing 1.3 million YouTube views.
