IIT GANDHINAGAR IN NEWS 2024
IIT Gandhinagar has consistently been a beacon of educational innovation, a catalyst for impactful research,
and a nurturing ground for leadership development among its students and community. These efforts and
accomplishments regularly garner significant attention from the media. This compendium, "IITGN in News"
released on January 26, 2025, offers a curated glimpse into the Institute's extensive news coverage throughout
2024. It highlights the achievements, programs, initiatives, collaborations, and pioneering research featured
in regional, national, and international print and digital media.

about iitgn
Indian Institute of Technology Gandhinagar (IITGN),
founded in 2008 and located in Palaj, Gandhinagar,
Gujarat, offers a distinctive undergraduate and graduate
education with innovative curricula that emphasise
on critical thinking, interdisciplinary knowledge, and
a liberal arts approach. The institute's student-centric
philosophy fosters a safe, nurturing, and empowering
environment, promotes industry-oriented learning to
tackle problems of socital relevance. IITGN's five-week
Foundation Programme for new undergraduates earned the
World Education Award 2013 for innovations in engineering
education. Over 40% of students gain international study
experience, reflecting IITGN's commitment to excellence
in science, technology, humanities and social sciences.
As India's first 5-star GRIHA LD (Green) campus and the
first 5-star campus for food safety and healthy eating,
IITGN also upholds exemplary safety standards for its
construction workforce, with practices recommended by
the IIT Council for adoption across all IITs. To know more,
please visit www.iitgn.ac.in
