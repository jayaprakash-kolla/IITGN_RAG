Institute Activities
BOARD OF GOVERNORS MEETING

At its 23rd meeting on December 20, the Board
approved setting up of a Writing Studio as a
service centre for honing the writing skills of
students. The Board also approved a motion
to upgrade & rename the Creative Learning
Initiative (CLI) as the Centre and renamed as
Centre for Creative Learning (CCL). The Board
approved the establishment of four endowed
Chairs in Sanskrit, Urdu, Classical Indian Music
and Indian Performing Arts.

DEDICATION OF THE CAMPUS TO THE NATION

Prime Minister of India Shri Narendra Modi visited the institute on October 7 and
dedicated its campus to the nation. He also launched the Pradhan Mantri Gramin Digital
Saksharta Abhiyaan (PMGDSA) from the institute. Present on the occasion were Chief
Minister of Gujarat Shri Vijaybhai Rupani, Union Minister of Human Resource
Development Shri Prakash Javadekar, Union Minister of Electronics & IT and Law &
Justice Shri Ravi Shankar Prasad and other dignitaries.

FIRE ENGINEERING LABORATORY

The William Henry Merrill Fire Engineering Laboratory was
inaugurated by Prof Sudhir K Jain, Director, IITGN and Mr Suresh
Sugavanam, VP & MD, Underwriters Laboratories South Asia on
December 7. The laboratory reinforces the strong commitment
of IITGN and Underwriters Laboratories towards enhancing fire

safety within and outside
India. The Centre for Safety

fifth, full-scale fire test on
a three-storey building with
glass facade on the occasion.

Support for the Institute
MAULANA ABUL KALAM AZAD CHAIR

Mrs Hamida Banu Chopra has established a
generous endowment to create the Maulana Abul
Kalam Azad Chair at IITGN for the teaching of
Urdu. Maulana Azad, a visionary and a scholar, was
the first education minister of independent India and
played a critical role in the creation of the IIT system.

Mrs Chopra, who holds a MA in Philosophy from Udaipur University
(1961) and Adeeb Kamil from Jamia Urdu Aligarh University (1971),
has taught Urdu at the University of California at Berkeley and IIT
Gandhinagar. She has published several English translations and
lectured extensively on seminal Urdu poets, such as Sahir Ludhanvi,
Ghalib, Faiz Ahmad Faiz, etc.
The Institute invites other well-wishers to contribute to this endowment
and is seeking support to create similar endowments for the teaching
of Sanskrit, Classical Indian Music, and Indian Performing Arts.

BHALODIA FUND AT CALTECH

Dr Mohan A Bhalodia and Mrs
Rasila M Bhalodia have set up a Fund
to support two IITGN students per
year for the next ten years to conduct
summer research at the California
Institute of Technology (Caltech).
Since 2011, between 6 to 8 IITGN
students have participated every year
in the Summer Undergraduate Research Fellowship (SURF)
at Caltech and this Fund will serve to strengthen this exchange. Dr
Bhalodia is a veteran professional of refinery operations and control,
and retired from ExxonMobil. He obtained a BE in Chemical
Engineering (1968) from M S University, Baroda, and a PhD (1973)
from the State University of New York.

INDIA KI KHOJ

The sixth edition of India Ki Khoj was organised during December
11-18. Six students from California Institute of Technology
(CalTech), seven from JAIST and 17 from IITGN participated in the
eight-day programme. The event featured lectures on diverse topics,
field visits to heritage sites in and around the city of Ahmedabad
and cultural performances. The lectures dealt with the topics of
culture, economics, art, music and sociology. The themes central to
discussions ranged from Indian architectural heritage and museology
to the Harappan civilisation and the uniqueness of Indian Islam,
among various others.

HOMECOMING

A two-day alumni meet - Homecoming 2017 - was held at IITGN
during December 23-24. Organised by the Office of Alumni Relations,
the event hosted 120 alumni from India and overseas. Over the two
days, the alumni revisited their college days and took part in facultyalumni & student-alumni interactions, Halla Bol (an interdepartmental
sports contest unique to IITGN) and various cultural events.

RASHTRIYA EKTA DIWAS

The institute celebrated the Rashtriya Ekta Diwas (National Unity
Day) with a ‘Run for Unity’ on October 31. The day was celebrated
to commemorate the 143rd birth anniversary of Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel.

CONSTITUTION DAY

The institute observed Constitution Day (Samvidhan Divas) to
commemorate the adoption of Constitution of India on November
26. Prof Mona Mehta spoke on the fundamental duties and their
significance.
