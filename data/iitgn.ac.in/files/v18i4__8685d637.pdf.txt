INSTITUTE HIGHLIGHTS
HON’BLE MINISTER DR JITENDRA SINGH
INAUGURATES MAJOR RESEARCH INITIATIVES

On June 11, 2026, IITGN hosted the inauguration of several major research
initiatives, presided over by Dr Jitendra Singh, Hon’ble Union Minister of
State (Independent Charge) for Science & Technology and Earth Sciences.
During the programme, the Institute formally launched three
interdisciplinary Research Clusters—Advanced Materials, Energy, and
Healthcare & MedTech, and inaugurated the Central Instrumentation
Facility (CIF).

15TH CONVOCATION

IITGN hosted its 15th Convocation on June 20, 2026, conferring degrees
upon 733 students—the largest graduating batch in the Institute's history.

Shri Neelkanth Mishra, Chief Economist at Axis Bank and Head of Global
Research at Axis Capital, graced the ceremony as the Chief Guest, while
Prof Pai-Chi Li, President of the Asian Institute of Technology (AIT),
Thailand, attended as the Guest of Honour. The ceremony was presided
over by Shri Sanjiv Puri, Chairman, Board of Governors, IITGN. The
graduating cohort included the pioneer batches of the Executive Master's
Programme, MTech in Integrated Circuit Design and Technology, and
MTech in Artificial Intelligence. The Institute also conferred the
prestigious Institute Fellow Awards upon Prof D P Roy and Prof G K
Sharma. Prior to the Convocation, an Awards Ceremony was held on June
19, 2026, where 39 students received medals and honours, including 4
Director's Gold Medals and 3 President's Gold Medals. The ceremony was
graced by Dr L N Dorairajan, Executive Director of AIIMS Rajkot, as the
Chief Guest.
