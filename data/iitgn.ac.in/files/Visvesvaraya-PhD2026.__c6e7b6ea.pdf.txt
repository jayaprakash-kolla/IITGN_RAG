ABOUT THE SCHEME

APPLICATION & ADMISSION

The Visvesvaraya PhD Scheme for Electronics and IT:
Phase II supports successful completion of PhD by
candidates enrolled against allocated seats, following
the scheme guidelines and directions of the PhD Cell,
Digital India Corporation, MeitY.

Admission and enrolment will be carried out in
accordance with the Visvesvaraya PhD Scheme
guidelines and IIT Gandhinagar requirements.

01 | ONE FULL-TIME PhD SEAT
PhD Seat -

01 Full-Time seat allocated under the Chip to Start-up programme

Fellowship -

₹46,250/month in Years I & II; ₹52,500/month in Years III–V

Rent Reimbursement - Reimbursement of Rent (RoR) as per scheme norms.
Research Contingency - ₹1,20,000 per year for the PhD candidate
Duration -

60 months or completion of PhD, whichever is earlier
