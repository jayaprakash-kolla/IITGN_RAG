3

Subsidy in terms of waiver and concessions in tuition fees are being provided to the students
from the reserved categories and the low-income group as per eligibility.
●​ The SC/ST/PH students shall receive a complete tuition fee waiver.
●​ The most economically backward students, whose family income is less than Rs. 1.0 lakh
per annum, shall receive full remission of the tuition fee.
●​ The other economically backward students, whose family income is between Rs. 1.0 lakh
to Rs. 2.5 lakhs per annum, shall also receive full remission of the tuition fee. 1/3rd of this
tuition fee waiver is supported thorough Student Benevolent & Welfare Fund or from the
Excellence Funds in the Endowment of the Institute.
●​ The other economically backward students, whose family income is between Rs. 2.5 lakh
to Rs. 5.0 lakhs per annum, shall receive remission of 2/3rd of the tuition fee.
