Dear well-wishers of IIT Gandhinagar,
Welcome to the fifth edition of our annual update letter.
Much as it upended life globally, the ongoing

pandemic has
defined and shaped many institutional developments
during the past year. The IITGN community has risen to the
occasion and displayed extraordinary leadership, empathy
and engagement that has enabled us not just to overcome the
pressing challenges it posed, but to reinvent, innovate and grow in ways
that will endure and make us better and stronger.

Safety First
The newly-constituted Crisis Management Group, the Special Volunteer
Force and the Covid-19 Medical Response Team, as well as the entire
community have played critical and invaluable roles in ensuring a safe
campus and managing and supporting each other during this crisis.

The Institute received exceptional support from state and local
authorities. The state government facilitated

Rapid Antigen

Testing of over 2700 members of the community in October
2020 and the Ahmedabad Municipal Corporation helped us establish a

special arrangement with three major hospitals in Ahmedabad to
reserve 30 beds for COVID patients from IITGN.

2020 REVIEW

New Pedagogy for the New Normal
The Dean (Student Affairs) and Dean (Academic Affairs) and their teams
have worked diligently to engage with students, keep them safe, and

provide them with exceptional financial support and educational
experiences. When the pandemic disrupted our regular semester in

March, we advanced the summer term and ran a few courses online in
experimental mode to gain experience with online teaching. We also
strengthened our IT and classroom infrastructure and introduced an
emergency grading system for the disrupted term. We were the first

IIT to hold a virtual Annual Convocation. Notably, the

number of PhDs awarded at this year’s Convocation more than doubled

from last year, signaling the rising development of our research
ecosystem.

The first term for the new batch of UG students started only on Nov 12

due to the delayed JEE Advanced test. The six-week term had courses

on Computing, Writing, Learning How to Learn, and a Foundation

Programme reimagined for the online setting. The

introductory term is designed to aid incoming students in adapting to

their new environment and help align their academic schedule with
other batches starting in January.

Research
IITGN is committed to building the best research environment and

developing outstanding research facilities. With the next set of academic
buildings, presently under construction, we expect to significantly scale

up our experimental facilities. The Research Park is nearing

completion and will lead to unprecedented opportunities for industry

partnerships. The Department of Science and Technology sanctioned
the establishment of a Nidhi-Prayas Centre to support young innovators
turn their ideas into proofs-of-concept at the Innovation and
Entrepreneurship Centre (IIEC).

Partnerships and New Talent
We are grateful for the generosity of major donors who supported

several new initiatives during the year. Shri Gordhanbhai B Gelot
established the Kankuben Bakshirambhai Gelot Chair in

memory of his late mother as well as the Gordhanbhai B Gelot

Laboratory for Artificial Intelligence and Data Science.
Dr. Dinesh O Shah has endowed the Dr. Dinesh O Shah Chair.

Dr. Hemant Kanakia and the Maker Bhavan Foundation helped

establish the Kanakia Young Researcher Seminar Series
at the Institute.

With the help of the IITGN Foundation in the United States, we

raised nearly Rs 1.4 crores in Empathy Funds to benefit students

and community members severely affected by the pandemic. The

support enabled the Institute to provide liberal aid to 160 financially
impacted students, including grants for the purchase of laptops, and
tuition fee waivers.

Campus Development
Constructions of our guest house and the Central Arcade are

a state-of-the-art sports complex is nearing

Outreach and Social Impact
We are especially proud of the collective response and support for the
most vulnerable members of our community and neighboring villages.

The Shramik Kalyan Samiti collaborated with CPWD to ensure the

health, safety and well-being of construction workers during the

pandemic. The Padosi Gaam Sahayog Samiti strengthened our
relationship with the neighbouring villages by organising distribution of
rations and safety-related supplies.

The Centre for Creative Learning (CCL), in collaboration with CBSE and
IISER Pune, launched an online education programme 30-30 STEM

to bring out the joy and inherent beauty in the curriculum that is all
around us. The program

served over 4 lakh registered

participants, recording over 3 million total views. The Centre also
conducted two online sessions on The Joy of Engineering for first
year students of nearly 150 engineering colleges affiliated with the APJ
Abdul Kalam (Kerala) Technological University.

The Spirit of IITGN
The world is experiencing extraordinary turmoil, but the community’s
remarkable response to this crisis throughout this year is powerful
evidence of IITGN’s outstanding ethos and culture. As we emerge from

the shadows of the Covid-19 pandemic that has so preoccupied and
altered life over the past several months, we feel tested, but also

empowered by the lessons and learnings forced upon us and look ahead
with renewed hope and optimism to new beginnings in the upcoming
year.
