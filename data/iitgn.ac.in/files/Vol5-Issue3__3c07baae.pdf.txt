Institute News
BoG Approves New Masters Programmes
The BoG approved the establishment of a
two-year MSc Programme in Cognitive
Science and a two-year Master of Arts programme in Society & Culture at its meeting on March 28, 2013. To strengthen the
Children's Education Fund scheme for
contractual staff members, the Board
enhanced the reimbursable limit from Rs.
7,500 to Rs. 10,000 per year per family.
The BoG also sanctioned a scheme for
interest-free soft advance for new staff
members. To incentivize undergraduate
and postgraduate student research, the
BoG also approved a cash award scheme
for papers published in refereed journals.
The Board also endorsed the norms to provide overseas research experience to the
PhD students. It also approved a plan for
the creation of a R&D Park at the permanent campus.

152 Students Compete in Electrical Vehicle Contest
Team 1

Team 2

152 students participated in a research contest sponsored by Underwriters Laboratory
(UL), Northbrook, USA, and IITGN to develop an electrical vehicle platform with the
aim of better understanding safety challenges for electrical vehicles in Indian conditions. An external review committee of EV industry experts comprising of Mr. Vikram
Gulati, (Director, National Automotive Testing and R&D Infrastructure Project,
NATRIP), Mr. Mukesh Bhandari (Founder, Chairman and CTO, Electrotherm), Mr.
Chetan Maini (Chief of Strategy & Technology, Reva-Mahindra Reva) and Dr.
Pravinray Gandhi (Director of Corporate Research, UL) selected two winning teams,
who will spend time at UL labs in Chicago during the summer of 2013 on developing
working prototypes. The members of the two winning teams are:
Team 1: Mr. Madan Taldevkar, Mr. Vaibhav Gandhi, Ms. Kimaya Kale, and
Mr. Prathamesh Bhat.
Team 2: Mr. Kishan Suthar, Mr. Shubham Gupta, Mr. Varun Gupta, and
Mr. Shreyas Vaidya.

IITGN Selected as 2013 CUDA Teaching Center
IIT Gandhinagar has been selected as a
CUDA Teaching Center for the year 2013.
CUDA Teaching Centers have integrated
GPU computing techniques into their mainstream computer programming curriculum. Students and other members of the Institute
will have access to CUDA enabled systems for hands-on experience of CUDA C/C++ development, debugging and experimentation.
Currently CUDA Research and Teaching centers are available in 42 countries worldwide.

Support for the Institute
GMDC Chair at IIT Gandhinagar
Gujarat Mineral Development Corporation Ltd (GMDC), a 50-year-old company with primary interests in minerals, lignite, and
energy, has set up a chair at IITGN in the areas of Materials, Minerals, Metallurgical Engineering and Earth Sciences. The GMDC Chair
will enable the Institute to provide additional financial support to outstanding faculty and students beyond what is possible through the
government grants.

Capital Season League (CSL)
With the joint effort of the sports departments of IIT Gandhinagar, Pandit
Deendayal Petroleum University (PDPU)
and Gujarat National Law University, the
first edition of CSL was held from March
11-18, 2013, featuring volleyball and basketball. IIT Gandhinagar won the basketball
tournament while in volleyball it won second place, behind PDPU. IITGN students
Mr. Ravi Kumar and Mr. Endla Naveen
bagged the title of Player of Tournament in
both basketball and volleyball respectively.
Mr. Wasim Khan (sports secretary) and
Mr. Aditya Samant (basketball secretary)
facilitated the smooth conduct of CSL.
