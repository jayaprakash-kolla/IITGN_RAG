Institute News
First Board of Governors of IITGN Formed

The Board of Governors of the Institute is now constituted and consists of very eminent academics and researchers,
industry leaders, and administrators. The Institute is singularly fortunate to have such eminent persons guide and supervise
its growth in the early critical years.
Dr R A Mashelkar, FRS Chairs the Board. A renowned academician, Dr. Mashelkar is presently the
Chairman of National Innovation Foundation and President of Global Research Alliance. He is
credited with the famous transformation of the Council of Scientific and Industrial Research (CSIR)
in India, as its Director General. He was the President of Indian National Science Academy (INSA).
Dr Mashelkar has received 27 Honorary Doctorates from the institutions of higher learning around
the world, besides numerous fellowships, prizes, awards and top civilian honours: Padmashri (1991)
and Padmabhushan (2000).
Three very eminent industrialists of India are on the Board: Mr. Suresh Neotia (Chairman
Emeritus,
Ambuja Cements), Mr. Deepak Puri (Chairman & Managing Director, Moser Baer India
Dr R A Mashelkar
Ltd.), and Mr. Malvinder Mohan Singh (Group Chairman, Religare & Fortis Healthcare Ltd.). Two seasoned
administrators, Shri A K Joti (Chief Secretary, Government of Gujarat) and Sri Ashok Thakur (Additional Secretary, in
Ministry of Human Resource Development, Govt of India) are included in the first Board. Prof D V Pai and Prof S L N
Murthy are the faculty representatives on the Board, while Prof Sudhir K Jain, the Director is an ex – officio member. Until
IIT Gandhinagar has its own Registrar, Sri B S Punalkar (Registrar, IIT Bombay) will act as the Secretary of the Board.
First Meeting of the Board of Governors

The IITGN Board held its first meeting in the temporary
premises of the Institute on December 18, 2009. The
Chairman Dr Mashelkar decided to start the Board
meeting ahead of time and the meeting closed 15
minutes before the stipulated time; symbolic of the
Institute's resolve to complete its major projects ahead
of time. Besides discussing routine matters, the Board
emphasized its commitment to academic excellence at
the Institute. To cultivate and support excellence by the
faculty and students, the Board approved several
proposals including a Faculty Development Plan,
provisions to encourage young faculty obtain exposure
to the reputed international institutes of teaching and research, liberal rules for faculty and students to participate in
national and international scientific gatherings etc.
Distinguished Honorary Professors

To help the Institute in its tryst with excellence, India's two distinguished academics Prof S P Sukhatme and Prof J B Joshi
have joined the Institute as Distinguished Honorary Professors.
Prof S P Sukhatme did his BSc (Engg) from Banaras Hindu University (1958), and S.M. (1961) and Sc.D. (1964)
both from MIT. He taught at IIT Bombay from 1965 to 2000 and was its Director (1995 – 2000). He was also the
Chairman of the Atomic Energy Regulatory Board (2000 – 2005). He was honoured with “Padmashri” in the
year 2001 and received the prestigious S. S. Bhatnagar Prize in the year 1983.

Prof J B Joshi received his B.Chem.Engg (1971) and Ph.D. (1977) both from the Mumbai University. A
renowned Chemical Engineer, Prof Joshi taught at the Institute of Chemical Technology, Mumbai until
recently and was its Director from 1999 to 2009. He has received numerous awards and fellowships,
including the prestigious S. S. Bhatnagar Prize in the year 1991. Prof Joshi holds a unique Indian record engineer with the largest number of citations (more than 4000).

to immediately initiate fund raising efforts to support these
programmes.

Short Courses for Undergraduate Students

ISEA Workshop on Cyber Security

The Institute has introduced the provision of 1-unit (8
lectures) “short courses” to be delivered by either an
IITGN faculty or a visitor. The courses are designed to
expose the students to different topics that are not
normally covered through the routine curriculum. These
will be Pass/No Pass (P/NP) courses and will appear in
the grade card of the students.

A workshop on Cyber Security (October 01, 2009) was
jointly organized by IIT Gandhinagar, NIT Surat, VGEC
Chandkheda and was sponsored by C-DAC Hyderabad.
Mr Devesh Jinwala, Asst Professor, NIT Surat and Prof
Dhiren Patel, Visiting Professor, IIT Gandhinagar
conducted the workshop at the Institute. Further details are
available at http://www.iitgn.ac.in/ws-isea.htm.

Green Gang

The Green Gang, a club of students interested in positive contributions to the environment has started with a strength
of 86 members. It promotes the idea of creating an environment friendly biosphere, free from anxiety of energy crisis.
The Club focuses on development of non-conventional energy resources. The Club has conducted a survey and
distributed pamphlets in the nearby areas educating the people about the importance of non-conventional resources.
The Club has also held talks with counterparts from Caltech where sustainability issues were discussed at great lengths.
