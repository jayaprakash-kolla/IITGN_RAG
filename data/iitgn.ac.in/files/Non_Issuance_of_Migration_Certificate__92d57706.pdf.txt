TO WHOM IT MAY CONCERN

This is to inform all concerned that the Indian Institute of Technology Gandhinagar
does not issue any Migration Certificate / No Objection Certificate (NOC)

The Institute awards a Degree Certificate only after a student has successfully
completed all academic requirements. The Degree Certificate itself is sufficient
confirmation that the Institute has no objection to the student pursuing further studies
or admission in any other college or university in India or abroad.

Accordingly, there is no separate provision for the issuance of a Migration Certificate
/NOC.

This is issued for the information of all concerned.
