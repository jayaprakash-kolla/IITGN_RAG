IIT GANDHINAGAR IN NEWS 2025
IIT Gandhinagar continues to stand at the forefront of academic excellence, research innovation, and societal engagement.
Throughout 2025, the Institute’s academic initiatives, research breakthroughs, institutional collaborations, student achievements,
and outreach activities attracted wide attention across regional, national, and international media platforms. This edition of
“IITGN in News,” released on January 26, 2026, presents a curated compilation of print, digital, as well as a few electronic
media coverages from January to December 2025, reflecting the Institute’s growing impact and visibility in shaping knowledge,
innovation, and leadership.
