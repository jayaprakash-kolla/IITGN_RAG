14TH CONVOCATION

IITGN celebrated its 14th Convocation Ceremony on June 21, 2025 at the
Mahatma Mandir Convention & Exhibition Centre in Gandhinagar. The
ceremony commenced in the presence of Mr Aditya Ghosh, Co-founder of
Akasa Air, who served as the Chief Guest. The Institute conferred degrees
to 668 graduating students, recognising their academic excellence as well
as their all-round achievements. Prior to the convocation, an Award
Ceremony was held on June 21, 2025 in the presence of Shri SP Shukla,
Chairman, Mahindra Agri Solutions Ltd, Mahindra EPC Irrigation Ltd &
CIE Automotive India Ltd. This year, 56 students were awarded various
medals, including 40 gold medals and 17 silver medals for excellence in
various categories such as academics, outstanding research, innovation,
leadership, social service, sports, arts and culture, among others.

INVENTX

The six-week InventX 2025 program was successfully concluded at IITGN
on July 4, 2025. Thirty students from IITs, NITs, IIITs, and top design and
engineering institutes participated in the innovation-focused program
hosted at Maker Bhavan, IITGN’s academic makerspace. Participants,
including 21 male and 9 female students, developed functional prototypes
of patentable inventions through hands-on learning. The top three teams
were awarded prizes of Rs 2 lakh, ₹1 lakh, and ₹50,000 respectively. The
program was organised with the generous support of the Maker Bhavan
Foundation, USA.

IITGN launched its flagship bi-monthly Industry Meet, ORBIT, on June 28,
2025, bringing together Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs),
startups, multinational companies, and IITGN stakeholders, to foster
strategic ties, understand industry needs, and advance technology
development in high-growth areas. The first edition of ORBIT (Outreach,
Research, Breakthrough, Innovation, and Technology) discussed
industry-academia collaboration avenues, funding mechanisms,
and regulatory challenges in the biomedical engineering domain.
Additionally, the need for support, policy pipelines, structural changes
to tackle challenges in testing, productisation, infrastructure access, and
sustainable partnerships was also deliberated.

IITGN SIGNS MoU WITH CPWD

IITGN signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU)
with the Central Public Works Department (CPWD) on
April 7, 2025, in New Delhi. The focus of the collaboration
is on joint research and innovation in Building Construction
Technology, with the goal of enhancing the quality,
efficiency, and sustainability of public infrastructure. The partnership
will explore the application of emerging technologies such as Artificial
Intelligence (AI), Internet of Things (IoT), Building Information Modelling
(BIM), and green construction methods.

IITGN SIGNS MoU WITH iACE

IITGN has entered into a strategic partnership with the
International Automotive Center of Excellence (iACE)
through the signing of a Memorandum of Understanding
(MoU) on May 29, 2025. The partnership aims to foster joint research
projects that address real-world industrial challenges, develop integrated
training programs and hands-on workshops, and enable shared access
to laboratories for innovation and product development. It also seeks to

explore emerging technologies such as electric vehicles, hybrid systems,
and advanced automotive solutions.

IITGN SIGNS MoU WITH BOSCH GLOBAL

IITGN signed a Memorandum of Understanding
(MoU) with Bosch Global Software Technologies Pvt
Limited in the domain of research projects, collaboration in short-term
training programs and CSR activities.

ACTIVITIES AT IITGN
cNARMADA CONFERENCE

The Centre for Narmada river basin management (cNarmada) at IITGN
organised a two-day focused conference titled ‘Traditions of Science:
Narmada and Water Management’ on April 7–8, 2025. The conference
brought together a distinguished group of scholars, researchers, and
practitioners from across India to explore the historical, cultural, and
scientific traditions associated with the Narmada river and indigenous
water management systems. Through a series of curated talks and
presentations, the event aimed to bridge traditional knowledge with

contemporary perspectives on ecological sustainability and riverine
wisdom. The conference concluded with a valedictory address by Dr
Kalyan Kumar Chakravarty, renowned scholar and cultural historian.

SUMMER RESEARCH INTERNSHIP PROGRAMME

The Summer Research Internship Programme (SRIP) kicked off at

The Springer Nature RISE Summit 2025 at IITGN focused on Research
Integrity in Science and Education (RISE), an initiative promoting ethical
research practices, transparency, and accountability. The summit
included insightful talks by Mr Steven Inchcoombe, President - Research,
Springer Nature (SN); Prof Rajat Moona, Director, IITGN; Prof Dr Rajul K
Gajjar, Vice Chancellor, Gujarat Technological University; Prof Shailendra
Saraf, Director, NIPER Ahmedabad, along with several professionals
from the SN India team. Topics covered included indexing platforms
and evaluation metrics, peer-review pipelines, open-access publications,
predatory journal issues, and industry collaboration, among others.

COMICS CONCLAVE 3.0

IITGN concluded the Comics Conclave 3.0 on April 6, 2025. The two-day
event, a flagship initiative of the institute, brought together a distinguished
gathering of artists, writers, scholars, and art enthusiasts from across the
country to explore the intersection of visual and graphic narratives in
education, research, and social discourse. It was organised by Prof Argha
Manna, Artists-in-Residence, Humanities and Social Sciences, and Prof
Jaison Manjaly, Jasubhai Memorial Chair Professor, Humanities and Social
Sciences (Jointly with the Cognitive and Brain Sciences department). This
year’s event focused on regional storytelling, sparking rich conversations
surrounding politics, memory, and environmental justice.

UPDATES FROM NYASA

Nyasa conducted a series of impactful activities and workshops aimed at

the Green Club. The quarter concluded with an On-Campus Distribution
