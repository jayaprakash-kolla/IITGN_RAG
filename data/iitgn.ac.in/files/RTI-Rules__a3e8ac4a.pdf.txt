G.S.R........, In exercise of the powers conferred by clauses (b) and (c) of
sub-section (2) of section 27 of the Right to Information Act, 2005 (22 of
2005), the Central Government hereby makes the following rules,
namely :-

41. Short title and commencement - (1) These rules may be called
the Right to Information (Regulation of Fee and Cost) Rules, 2005.

(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the
Official Gazette.

2. Definitions - In the rules, unless the context otherwise requires, -
(a) ‘Act’ means the Right to Information Act, 2005;
(b) ‘section’ means section of the Act;

(c) all other words and expressions used herein but not defined and
defined in the Act shall have the meanings assigned to them in the Act.

3. A request for obtaining information under sub-section (1) of section
6 shall be accompanied by an application fee of rupees ten by way of
cash against proper receipt or by demand draft or bankers cheque
payable to the Accounts Officer of the public authority.

4. For providing the information under sub-section (1) of section 7,
the fee shall be charged by way of cash against proper receipt or by
demand draft or bankers cheque payable to the Accounts Officer of the
public authority at the following rates:-

(a) rupees two for each page (in A-4 or A-3 size paper) created or
copied;
