Dear well-wishers of IIT Gandhinagar,
Welcome to the fourth edition of our annual update letter.
I am pleased to share that IITGN was ranked 7th in India and
among the top 600 institutions worldwide in the 2020 Times Higher

Education (THE) World University Rankings released recently. IITGN
has also been ranked 4th at the Swachhata Ranking Awards

2019 in the ‘residential university – All India Council of Technical
Education (AICTE)’ category. IITGN received a five-star rating in

the Eat Right Campus audit, for ensuring food safety and
promoting healthy eating, and became India's first educational
institute to receive a 5-star rating from FSSAI.

Shifts in Pedagogy: New Programs
The academic programs at the Institute saw several major
advancements this year. The

learning-by-doing paradigm of

teaching is being steadily ingrained in the academic fabric of the
Institute through various initiatives. We are also developing a 3-year

pilot project on leadership development among students,
thanks to a significant gift from Mr. Avi Nash, a well-wisher of IITGN.

IITGN wishes to significantly enhance writing skills of its students. We
have created a “Writing Studio” with dedicated staff to support

students by reviewing, editing and giving feedback. We have recently

started a compulsory course on “Writing” for all our

postgraduate students, and a certification program in scientific writing
is underway as well.

Outreach and Social Impact
The

Centre for Creative Learning is proactively promoting

experiential education in schools and colleges. The

Centre was

awarded the first prize at the Vibrant Gujarat STEM

conference, by the Union Minister of Education, for their
work on experiential STEM education. More than 6,000 school

teachers and engineering college professors and over 50,000 students
from all over the country have been exposed to CCL’s new teaching
pedagogy.

Through its community outreach programs, IITGN is committed to the
upliftment of neighbouring communities. NEEV, one of the community

outreach programs of IIT Gandhinagar, continues to leverage the
resources of IIT Gandhinagar by offering comprehensive training and
mentoring to facilitate livelihood related opportunities for village
women in its surrounding communities. Our students contribute to the
welfare of underprivileged children through ‘Nyasa’.

IITGN: The next five years
Expansion.
We expect to reach about 2,500-student strength, achieve increased
graduation rates, witness an increase in post-doctoral researchers,
accommodate more non-degree students, and welcome more
international students.

Campus Development.
The second phase of constructions, including hostels, guest house,

sports complex and central arcade, and academic buildings, will soon
be completed. Our focus will be to better integrate infrastructure with
human use and soft dimensions, and to create a robust campus
management system across users and managers.

Alumni Engagement.
Several of our initiatives towards excellence are funded through
philanthropic funds. Currently, more than 99% of the money being
raised by IITGN is from non-alumni. However, we are building a strong

culture of alumni engagement. This financial year, 27% of our alumni
made a donation to the Institute, and we are challenging ourselves to
raise it to 40% alumni engagement.

Industry Partnerships.
We expect to fully commission our Research Park with 90% occupancy.

This will significantly deepen our engagement with the industry and
enable our faculty and students to carry out impactful research.

I was honored to be reappointed as director for the third term, and am

looking forward to working with the IITGN fraternity over the next five
years on the next phase of our evolution as an institute of national and

global repute. IITGN’s success thus far is a tribute to the

collaborative work of its dedicated faculty, staff, students,

alumni and friends of the Institute. I am excited about our
future as we collectively push the envelope to raise our beloved
Institute to ever greater heights.
