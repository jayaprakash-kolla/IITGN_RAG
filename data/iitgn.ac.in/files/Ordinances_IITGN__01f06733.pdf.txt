(SECTIONS 28 AND 29)
Section 28 Ordinances

Subject to the provisions of this Act and the Statutes, the Ordinances of
each Institute may provide for all or any of the following matters,
namely:
a) the admission of the Students to the Institute;
b) the courses of study to be laid down for all degrees and diplomas
of the Institute;
c) the conditions under which Students shall be admitted to the
degree or diploma courses and to the examinations of the
Institute, and shall be eligible for degrees and diplomas;
d) the conditions of award of the fellowships, scholarships,
exhibitions, medals and prizes;
e) the conditions and mode of appointment and duties of examining
bodies, examiners and moderators;
f) the conduct of examinations;
g) the maintenance of discipline among the Students of the Institute,
and
h) any other matter which by this Act or the Statute is to be or may
be provided for by the Ordinances.

Section 29 Ordinances
How Made

1) Save as otherwise provided in this section, Ordinances shall be
made by the Senate.
2) All Ordinances made by the Senate shall have effect from such
dates as it may direct, but every Ordinance so made shall be
submitted, as soon as may be, to the Board of Governors and shall
be considered by the Board at its next succeeding meeting.
3) The Board shall have power by resolution to modify or cancel any
such Ordinance, and such Ordinance shall from the date of such
resolution stand modified accordingly or cancelled, as the case
maybe.

ORDINANCE NO. 1
INTRODUCTORY

1.1

The constituents of the Senate and its functions are as defined in the Act and Statutes of the
Institute. The Senate shall primarily be concerned with the academic affairs of the Institute
encompassing instructional matters.

1.2

The Senate shall have the control and general regulation and be responsible for the
maintenance of standards of instruction, education and examinations in the Institute.

1.3

The Senate shall sanction academic programs and courses, approve their contents and any
changes thereof, and oversee their conduct.

1.4

The Senate shall set the criteria for the award of academic degrees and honors and approve
each individual award.

1.5

The Senate shall set the criteria for the termination of Students’ programs and approve each
termination with due deliberation.

1.6

The Senate shall empower constituent permanent and other sub-committees, appoint their
members and set their powers that shall not equal or exceed the powers of the Senate as a
whole.

ORDINANCE NO. 2
DEPARTMENTS, DISCIPLINES & OTHER UNITS

2.1

The Board of Governors may from time to time create, continue, combine or close any
academic units such as Departments, Centres or other such units on the recommendation of
the Senate.

2.2

Each Department or such unit shall be placed in charge of a Head or an In-Charge who shall
be selected and appointed by the Director from among the academic staff of the Institute.

2.3

The Head shall be responsible for the activities under his or her charge subject to the direction
and general control of the Director.

2.4

A Department may have one or more active academic units called Disciplines. A Discipline
may also be shared with multiple departments.

2.5

Activities of a Discipline shall be managed by a Discipline Coordinator appointed by the
Director from amongst the academic staff or technical staff of the Institute.

2.6

Department shall be responsible for academic administration including sharing of
responsibilities among faculty members, while the Discipline(s) shall cater to the academics
and the academic management of Students of the Discipline(s).

2.7

The Academic Programmes of Degrees shall be executed by the Discipline(s). The Students
shall be admitted to the Programme in a Discipline. The Senate may provide for multidisciplinary Academic Programmes such as Double Major, Minor etc.

2.8

Each Academic Department shall have at least one Discipline which in turn shall run at least
one Academic Programme.

2.9

The Head and Discipline Coordinator shall ensure that the instructional activities pertaining
to his or her charge fulfil the decisions of the Authorities of the Institute and of Director.

ORDINANCE NO. 3
ACADEMIC PROGRAMMES

3.1

The Senate shall facilitate introduction of new Academic Programmes based on their need
and alignment with the broad goals as set forth in the Act and Statutes of the Institute.

3.2

The Senate shall examine each proposal for the introduction of new Academic Programmes
and make suitable recommendations for approval by the Board of Governors of the Institute.

3.3

The Senate shall formulate the minimum admission standards and the duration of each
Academic Programme.

3.4

The Senate shall prescribe the Discipline-specific Curriculum or necessary Courses of study for
successful completion of the Academic Programmes in that Discipline. Any change in the
approved curriculum shall require prior approval of the Senate.

3.5

Each Course of study shall carry an appropriate number of Credits as approved by Senate that
reflect a weighted measure of academic engagement typically comprising of lectures (L),
tutorials (T), practicals (P), and number of engagement hours expected (thesis, independent
projects, independent study etc.).

3.6

The Senate may modify the Credits / structure of academic engagement components as per
the process formulated by the Senate.

3.7

A Course of study may have 0 (zero) Credits. Such zero-credit Courses are expected to impart
learning that is beyond the framework of a standard instructional paradigm.

3.8

The performance of Students in each Course of study shall be measured by Letter Grades
hereafter referred to as Grades. Each Grade shall either represent a numerical equivalent, or
convey a satisfactory or unsatisfactory performance, or convey a passing or failing
performance. The Grades to be used and their numerical equivalents and connotations shall
be decided by the Senate from time to time.

3.9

The Senate shall formulate the total number of Credits that need to be successfully completed
for fulfilling requirements of each Academic Programme in Disciplines.

ORDINANCE NO. 4
ACADEMIC CALENDAR

4.1

The Academic Year shall commence on the first of July every calendar year.

4.2

The Academic Year shall consist of two regular Semester Terms, a Summer Term and a Winter
Term, apart from vacations, and mid-Semester recesses as follows.
(a) Semester 1 normally starting in end of July in the Academic Year and of about fifteen weeks
in duration.
(b) Winter Term normally starting in second week of December in the Academic Year and of
about three weeks in duration.
(c) Semester 2 normally starting in end of December in the Academic Year and of about fifteen
weeks in duration.
(d) Summer Term normally starting in second week of May in the Academic Year and of about
eight weeks in duration.

4.3

Semester 1 and Semester 2 shall normally consist of fourteen weeks of classes and about one
week of mid-semester recess.

4.4

Each Term shall further be divided into Quads as follows.
(a) Semester 1 and Semester 2 shall have four Quads.
(b) Summer Term shall have two Quads.
(c) Winter Terms shall have one Quad.

4.5

The Senate may restructure, rename or redesignate Terms as deemed appropriate.

4.6

The institute shall publish Senate-approved Academic Calendar each year giving exact dates
for the important academic events scheduled during the Academic Year. All attempts shall be
made to follow this Calendar except in rare exceptional conditions.

4.7

The Academic Calendar, or any change therein during the year, shall require prior approval of
the Senate.

ORDINANCE NO. 5
ADMISSIONS

5.1

The admission to all Academic Programmes of the Institute shall be open to all persons
without regard to their gender, race, caste, class, religion, profession, region etc.
Notwithstanding the criteria set aside by the Senate, admission to all Academic Programmes
shall be on the basis of academic merit alone.

5.2

The number of admissions that can be made to any Academic Programme of the Institute
shall be as decided by the Senate from time to time. Reservation of seats for various
categories shall be implemented as per the guidelines of IIT Council / Board of Governors.

5.3

Foreign Nationals can be admitted to various Academic Programmes as per procedures laid
down by the Senate.

5.4

Normally, BTech Students shall be admitted only to the first year of the Academic Programme.
However, under exceptional circumstances, the Senate may admit a Student with advanced
standing on the basis of partial completion of a similar Academic Programme elsewhere.

5.5

Students admitted to the BTech Programme in a Discipline shall normally pursue the
Academic Programme in the same Discipline initially allocated to them at the time of
admission. The Senate may permit a limited number of Students to change their Discipline
and / or Academic Programme as per the "Norms for Branch Change" laid down by the Senate.

5.6

Students of the BTech Programme may opt for enhanced Academic Programmes such as Dual
Degree Programmes, joint degree programmes etc. as per rules and procedures laid down by
the Senate.

5.7

The eligibility requirements and frequency of admission to various Academic Programmes of
the institute shall be decided by the Senate from time to time.

5.8

For admissions into the Academic Programmes of the Institute which require a qualifying
degree, those candidates pursuing their last year of studies shall be considered for
provisional admission provided they satisfy all other requirements prescribed by the Senate.

5.9

All provisional admissions must be confirmed by producing qualifying degree certificate (or,
provisional degree certificate and such other documents as specified by Senate) by the period
prescribed by the Senate. The provisional admission of any Student who either does not
submit the required documents by the stipulated date or fails to meet any other stipulated
requirement for admission, can be cancelled by the Senate.

5.10

The Disciplines may stipulate additional requirements over and above the minimum
admission requirements stipulated by the Senate for admission to Academic Programmes
within the Discipline. However, such additional requirements must be approved by the
Chairman Senate a-priori.
Page 8 of 24

5.11

The admission procedure and requirements, for all categories of Students, for the Academic
Programmes shall be laid down by the Senate from time to time and may include Disciplinespecific requirements which must be approved by the Chairman Senate a-priori.

5.12

The admission of any Student may also be cancelled by the Senate, at any later time, if it is
found that the Student had supplied false information, or suppressed some relevant facts
while seeking admission.

5.13

If the qualifying degree / certificate is withdrawn by the qualifying degree-giving authority,
the admission to the Academic Programme in the institute may be cancelled by the Senate.
In such a case when the degree was already awarded, it may be withdrawn by the Senate.

5.14

The decision of the Senate in all cases of admissions to Academic Programmes shall be final
and binding.

ORDINANCE NO. 6
REGISTRATION

6.1

Before the commencement of each Term, every Student, unless otherwise exempted by the
Senate, shall register for the prescribed Courses and on thesis work to be pursued during that
Term as per the registration dates specified in the Academic Calendar.

6.2

The Senate shall lay down norms for registration of Students pursuing their Academic
Programmes.

6.3

The registration for various Terms in an Academic Year shall be subject to the conditions that
may be laid down by the Senate from time to time.

6.4

Payment of prescribed fees, and all other dues outstanding against the name of the Student
to the Institute, the Hostel or any other recognized unit of the Institute shall ordinarily be a
precondition for registration.

6.5

Students may be permitted by the Dean of Academic Affairs to register late in a Term on the
date prescribed in the Academic Calendar based on bonafide reasons and upon payment of
prescribed late registration fee. The Senate shall lay down the conditions and procedure for
such late registration. Any other exceptional case of registration requires approval of the
Senate.

6.6

The registration of any Student may be cancelled or changed during the Terms, partly or
wholly, according to the norms laid down by the Senate.

6.7

Students who are duly registered for the entire duration of a Course of study are eligible to
receive Grades reflecting their performance in that Course of study.

ORDINANCE NO. 7
TEACHING AND EVALUATION

7.1

The normal medium of instruction shall be the English Language. Other languages may also
be considered by Senate.

7.2

Each Course, along with its Credits, shall require approval of the Chairman Senate upon
recommendation of the appropriate Standing Committee of the Senate according to the
Course approval procedures laid down by the Senate. Only approved Courses can be offered
during any Term.

7.3

The list of all Courses, along with the Instructor(s)-in-Charge, Instructors and Tutors, to be
offered by a Discipline, during any Term, shall be finalized before the beginning of the Term,
taking into consideration all the departmental requirements and the recommendations of the
Standing Committees of the Senate.

7.4

Each approved Course, whenever offered, shall be conducted by the assigned Instructor-inCharge with the assistance of the required number of Instructors / Tutors. The Instructor-inCharge shall be responsible for conducting the Course, holding the examinations,
performance evaluation of the Students and awarding the Grades at the end of the Term.

7.5

Students registered in Courses shall be evaluated continuously through mechanisms including
but not limited to quizzes, examinations, viva, projects, presentations, assignments etc. The
frequency, type and nature of evaluation / examination shall be carried out as per procedures
/ timetable decided by the Senate. As far as possible, all the evaluations and examinations
such as Exam 1 and Exam 2 shall be conducted during the prescribed examination periods as
per the Academic Calendar. In rare exceptional circumstances when the evaluation needs to
be done in deviation of the Academic Calendar, Dean Academic Affairs’ permission shall be
necessary who shall be the sole authority to permit such deviations.

7.6

Students who fail to appear in any examination, due to bonafide reasons, may be permitted
by the Dean Academic Affairs to appear in the Make-up Examination as per the procedures
laid down by the Senate.

7.7

Each Student, registered for a Course, shall be awarded a letter grade by the concerned
Instructor-in-Charge. The Grades to be used and their numerical equivalents shall be decided
by the Senate from time to time. The letter Grades that are deemed unsatisfactory shall be
decided by the Senate from time to time.

7.8

The Grade awarded to a Student may be based upon the Student’s performance across
various evaluation mechanisms such as examinations, quizzes, home assignments, laboratory
work and regularity in his / her attendance, with weightage of each component of assessment
as per discretion of the Instructor-in-Charge.

7.11

A Student may be awarded Grade "I" (Incomplete) in a Course, if he / she has missed a minor
part of the Course requirement but has done all other parts satisfactorily. A Student is not
entitled for "I" Grade simply because he / she has failed to appear in some examinations. An
"I" Grade cannot be given for research units. An "I" grade awarded to any Student must be
converted by the Instructor-in-charge to an appropriate letter Grade before the date
announced in the Academic Calendar. Any "I" Grade outstanding after the last date for grade
submission of Make-up examination shall automatically be converted to the Fail Grade.

7.12

A Grade once awarded by the Instructor-in-Charge cannot be changed unless the request for
change of Grade by the Instructor-in-Charge is approved by the Chairman Senate. Any request
for change of Grade must be made within six weeks of the start of the next Term and must be
justified with reasons for recommending the change of Grade.

7.13

Each Discipline shall evolve a procedure and use it for award of Grades in all project Courses.

7.14

The Senate shall prescribe the norms for maintaining the Academic Transcript for each
Student that reflects their performance in each Course accurately. The Academic Transcript
will contain Grades and details of all Courses taken towards meeting graduation
requirements. This shall include Courses which are part of Minor(s), Honours, Double Major,
Dual Degree and Courses which have been repeated or substituted for grade improvement.
The Fail Grades in Courses counted towards graduation requirements shall be retained in the
Academic Transcript even when they are subsequently replaced.

7.15

The Senate shall facilitate Students to explore Courses in areas in which they have little
background and promote additional learning among Students through Courses beyond
minimum graduation requirements. The Senate shall develop provisions against the decline
in overall performance indices of the Students.

7.16

Students who opt for any additional learning Courses shall be provided a Proficiency
Transcript. The Proficiency Transcript will cover Grades for successfully completed additional
learning Courses only. The Grades for such Courses shall be reported as Pass (P) irrespective
of the actual level of performance in the Course. Additional learning Courses shall include
Short Courses, Elective Courses and additional project Courses. Courses that are chosen for
inclusion in the Proficiency Transcript cannot be considered for inclusion in the Academic
Transcript. The Instructor shall be the sole authority to define criteria for successful
completion of the Course.

7.17

A Student can submit requests to convert their earlier registered Course(s) with passing
grade(s) to Pass / Fail option. The Course tagged with this option will be dropped from CPI
calculation.

7.18

The Senate shall formulate the conditions of using the Pass / Fail option in different Academic
Programmes. The Senate may revise the conditions for using the Pass / Fail option in different
Academic Programmes from time to time.

7.19

The Senate shall formulate provisions for repeating / substitution of Courses in different
Academic Programmes. The Senate may revise the provisions for repeating / substitution of
Courses in different Academic Programmes from time to time.

7.20

The Senate shall make guidelines for the following:
(a) guidance of students in need of additional support towards their academics.
(b) continuation of students in their Academic Programme.
(c) termination of Academic Programme of a Student, and right of Student to appeal against
termination.
(d) restart of Academic Programme of a Student.

7.21

The Senate shall prescribe the duration within which each Academic Programme must be
completed. The Senate shall provide guidelines for expiry of accumulated Course Credits of
Students who have failed to complete their Academic Programme within the Senateprescribed duration.

ORDINANCE NO. 8
PROJECT AND THESIS EVALUATION

8.1

Every Postgraduate Student shall usually have at least one Thesis / Project supervisor from
amongst the faculty members of the Institute. However, another co-supervisor from outside
the Institute, if necessary, may be appointed subject to approval by the Chairman Senate.

8.2

Each Discipline shall evolve modalities for the appointment of supervisors based on alignment
of the Student's aspirations and the research interests of the faculty. These modalities shall
also include the procedures to be followed for change of supervisors.

8.3

In case a supervisor proceeds on leave for a prolonged period, resigns, superannuates, or
otherwise ceases to be a faculty member of the Institute before submission of the thesis, a
new supervisor or a co-supervisor, as the case may be, may be appointed as per procedures
laid down by the Senate. Senate shall formulate this policy on the basis of the stage at which
the thesis has proceeded, feasibility of online mentorship and the nature of planned absence
of the supervisor.

8.4

The Senate shall formulate provisions for establishment of Doctoral Studies Committee (DSC)
for PhD Students and may revise such provisions from time to time. The DSC shall be tasked
with providing academic feedback and monitoring the progress of the PhD Student.

8.5

Every PhD Student must pass a Qualifying Examination. The procedure and timeline for the
conduct of the Qualifying Examination shall be as prescribed by the Senate. The Head of the
Department and Coordinator of the Discipline shall ensure timely completion of the Qualifying
Examination.

8.6

Every PhD Student must clear a Proposal Defense as per the procedure outlined by the Senate.

8.7

Each PhD Student must deliver a "Thesis Seminar" wherein the thesis work shall be presented.
This seminar shall be delivered when the Student is almost ready for thesis submission.

8.8

The Senate shall provide detailed norms for the following: PhD thesis review, decision
framework to be applied upon receipt of thesis reviews, constitution of Final Defense
Committee (FDC), conduct of Final Defense, and criterion for passing the Final Defense.

8.9

The Senate shall provide detailed guidelines for the review of thesis of other Postgraduate
Students (such as MTech, MDes etc.), constitution of thesis defense committee, conduct of
thesis defense, and criterion for passing the thesis defense.

8.10

The guidelines established by the Senate as per Ordinance 8.8 and 8.9 shall be rigorous,
transparent, and impartial, and help in maintaining the highest standards of academic
integrity.

ORDINANCE NO. 9
RESIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

9.1

The Senate shall prescribe the minimum residence requirements (duration) for all approved
Academic Programmes.

9.2

The residence requirement of PhD Students may vary depending on the attributes of the
Student as per the following:
A. Primary institutional affiliation of the Student
(i) Internal
(ii) External
B. Commitment to the PhD Programme
(i) Full-time
(ii) Part-time
C. Source of Support including Financial Support to the Student
(i) Institutional
(ii) Non-institutional

9.3

The Senate may formulate different residence requirements for PhD Students based on their
attributes A-C as per Ordinance 9.2. The Senate may waive the residence requirements for
PhD students partially or completely on a case-to-case basis.

9.4

The residence requirement of one Semester Term is equivalent to that of registration of at
least minimum load of Courses / Credits / research during the Term.

ORDINANCE NO. 10
LEAVE OF ABSENCE

10.1

Absence during the Terms shall be discouraged for all registered Students. Based on bonafide
reasons, a Student may be granted leave of absence by the competent authority as per
provisions made for such absence in the Ordinances.

10.2

Applications for leave shall be made as per the designated proforma of the Institute. Leave
may be sanctioned by the Chairman Senate or by a member of the academic staff to whom
the power has been delegated by the Chairman Senate.

10.3

Leave ordinarily begins from the date on which leave as such is actually availed of and ends
on the day preceding the one on which duty is resumed. Saturdays, Sundays and other
holidays or the vacation may be prefixed as well as suffixed to the leave, subject to any limit
of absence on leave prescribed under each category of leave.

10.4

Undergraduate Students shall be eligible to avail the vacations and the mid-Semester recesses
as provided in the Academic Calendar. In addition, the Undergraduate Students may be
allowed leave of absence during a Term, as per rules and procedures laid down by the Senate.

10.5

Postgraduate Students shall be eligible to the kinds of leave of absence without loss of
financial assistance, as per rules and procedures laid down by the Senate.

10.6

Female Students shall be eligible to maternity leave as per rules and procedures laid down by
the Senate.

10.7

Male Students shall be eligible to paternity leave as per rules and procedures laid down by
the Senate.

10.8

Leave of absence beyond that provided in the Ordinances 10.4 to 10.7 may also be granted.
However, such leave of absence shall usually entail loss of financial assistance, if any.

10.9

Without prejudice to the provision made for leave of absence in the Ordinances, a
Postgraduate Student absent unauthorised for a period of four weeks during a Semester
Term, or two weeks during the Summer Term, is liable for termination from his / her Academic
Programme.

10.10 Students are expected to complete their Academic Programme without any break. However,
for bonafide reasons, Students may be granted leave of absence from the Academic
Programme. But such absence(s) shall ordinarily not exceed two Semester Terms with or
without break, excluding Maternity Leave for female Students. Leave of absence beyond two
Semester Terms would require approval from Chairman Senate.

ORDINANCE NO. 11
GRADUATION REQUIREMENTS AND GRANT OF DEGREE / DIPLOMA

11.1

All Students must complete the prescribed number of Credits as prescribed for their
respective Academic Programmes.

11.2

The Senate shall formulate minimum CPI requirements for each Academic Programme and
may revise such requirements from time to time.

11.3

A Student shall be deemed to have completed the graduation requirements if the Student
has:
a) earned all the prescribed Credits;
b) attained the minimum required CPI, if applicable;
c) satisfied the minimum academic and residence requirements;
d) satisfied all the requirements specified by the concerned Department, if any;
e) satisfied all the requirements specified by the Senate and the Ordinances.
f) should have paid all the dues to the Institute, and, should have no pending case of
indiscipline.

11.4

The Senate shall approve the award of appropriate Degree / Diploma to all such Students who
have completed the graduation requirements.

11.5

The Senate shall approve the format of each Degree / Diploma to be awarded to a Student
after successful completion of the Academic Programme. Any revision in the format of a
Degree / Diploma shall require approval of the Senate.

11.6

Conferment of an Honorary Degree of the Institute shall be in accordance with Statute 27.
The Senate shall approve the format of the Honorary Degree in each case.

11.7

In rare circumstances, the Senate may formulate provisions for altering details in previously
awarded degrees and may also revise such provisions from time to time.

11.8

Under extremely exceptional circumstances, where gross violation of the graduation
requirements is detected at any later stage, the Senate may recommend to the Board of
Governors to withdraw the Degree / Diploma, already awarded.

ORDINANCE NO. 12
SCHOLARSHIPS / PRIZES, MEDALS AND FINANCIAL-ASSISTANCE

12.1

Scholarships, Freeships, Prizes, Awards and Certificates of Merit shall be awarded to eligible
Undergraduate and Postgraduate Students according to the rules laid down by the Senate
and provisions of Statutes.

12.2

Scholarships and Freeships are liable to be withdrawn, partially or wholly, in case of
misconduct, deliberate concealment of material facts and / or supply of false information or
as per the approved Senate Student Affairs Committee (SSAC) sanctions. SSAC shall be
constituted by the Chairman Senate.

12.3

Students leaving the Institute on their own accord without completing the Academic
Programme of study may be required to refund the amount of scholarship received during
that year.

12.4

No Student can receive two scholarships simultaneously, except for the recipients of the
Excellence Scholarships. Further, in cases where the excellence scholarship awardee is also a
recipient of any other scholarship of equal or higher value, then he / she is eligible only for a
one-time receipt of Rupees Five Thousand only towards the excellence scholarship.

12.5

Scholarships will not be paid after the month in which a Student completes all the prescribed
academic requirements of the Academic Programme.

12.6

To promote and recognize academic excellence, constructive leadership and overall growth
and development of Students, the Senate shall award Prizes / Medals / Scholarships to be
Instituted either by the Institute or through endowment / grants of donors. The norms and
conditions for the institution of such Scholarships / Prizes / Medals shall require approval of
the Senate.

12.7

The Institute provides financial assistance by means of interest-free loans, financial aid to the
needy Students to support their academic expenses. Students who are admitted as nonsponsored, regular, full-time Postgraduate Students, are also eligible for financial assistance
according to the norms approved by the Board of Governors.

12.8

Postgraduate Students can also receive financial assistance from Government, Public Sector
Organizations, private industrial and commercial organizations, and research schemes
directed by the faculty members of the Institute.

ORDINANCE NO. 13
STUDENT CONDUCT AND DISCIPLINE

13.1

Each Student shall conduct himself / herself, both within and outside the campus of the Institute,
in a manner befitting a Student of an Institute of National importance. No Student is expected to
indulge in any activity which may bring down the prestige of the Institute. Each Student shall show
due respect and courtesy to the faculty, staff, and fellow Students of the Institute. They should
also pay due attention and courtesy to the visitors and residents of the campus.

13.2

The Institute shall maintain an Honour Code for the Students to follow. The Senate may
modify the same from time to time. The Honour Code shall not be in violation of the
Constitution of the Republic of India and all laws related to education campuses shall be
applicable.

13.3

Disciplinary action shall be taken against Students for violation of the Student Honour Code.

13.4

The Senate Standing Committee namely SSAC shall enquire into each alleged violation and
make its recommendation to the Chairman Senate. Penal actions may include one or more of
the following:
(a) Warning
(b) Probation on disciplinary ground which shall debar participation in internships and
placements arranged by the Institute, consideration for awards and recognitions from the
Institute, participation in Student governance activities etc.
(c) Withdrawal of facilities such as hostel facility, sports facility, mess facility, incubation
facility etc.
(d) Financial penalty in case the misconduct included financial loss to the Institute or to an
individual.
(e) Suspension of scholarships for a duration specified.
(f) Suspension of academic attendance for a duration specified.
(g) Withdrawal of a registration in a Course or Courses as specified.
(h) Reduction of Grade in a Course or Courses by specified number of steps.
(i) Conversion of Grade in a Courses or Courses to a Failing Grade.
(j) Withholding the award of Degree for a specific period of time.
(k) Termination of studentship without giving any Degree / Diploma.
(l) Withdrawal of Degree / Diploma.

13.5

Violation of the Honour Code by a Student can be referred to Dean Student Affairs by any
Student, faculty, staff or any other functionary of the Institute.

13.6

Ragging and sexual harassment, in any form, are strictly prohibited. The Committees formed
for addressing such specific matters (such as the Internal Complaints Committee mandated
by the Prevention of Sexual Harassment Act, 2013) will have the authority to investigate and
recommend appropriate actions for any cases of reported violations, and Chairman Senate
will have the authority to approve appropriate penalties.

13.7

Appeals against penalties, if any, shall be made to Chairman Senate who shall be the appellate
authority.

ORDINANCE NO. 14
HOSTELS

14.1

IIT Gandhinagar is a residential Institute and, therefore, every registered Student shall reside
in a room assigned to him / her in one of the Hostels / Residences on campus. In exceptional
cases, the Director / Dean Student Affairs may permit a Student to reside under their own
arrangement. Such Students shall, however, be required to seek advance exemption from
Dean Student Affairs for their hostel, mess and laundry charges for the respective Term. Part
time Students may be given accommodation in the Hostels/Residences with a-priori approval
of the Dean Student Affairs.

14.2

There shall be Wardens in charge of various operations in hostels and mess facilities as may
be determined by the Senate from time to time. The office of the Wardens shall be held by
the members of the academic staff of the Institute. These appointments shall be made by the
Dean, Student Affairs.

14.3

Every Student residing in a hostel shall be allotted one of the Hostel Mess. The Students shall
have the option of indicating their preference for a particular mess through the Mess
Allocation Process initiated by the Student Mess Council (on First come, First Served Basis)
from time to time. However, in case a Student seeks exemption from mess facility for a
specific period due to special circumstances such as medical grounds, he / she may submit an
application for approval of Dean Student Affairs through the appropriate Warden along with
supporting documents such as recommendations of the Institute Doctor / Medical Officer.

14.4

During Institute vacations, messes shall usually function on cafeteria mode, wherein the
Students can avail the messing facilities on walk-in rates basis. However, the Students can
avail the subsidised mess rates (as applicable during the Term), in case they are willing to avail
the mess facility for a continuous extended period. All such Students can deposit the
applicable mess charges in advance to the respective mess manager and avail of this facility
during the Semester recesses.

14.5

The various facilities within the hostel premises such as shops, cafes and canteens will be
managed by appropriate Committees appointed by the Director.

14.6

Every resident shall be personally responsible for the safe upkeep of the furniture and other
items supplied to the resident and will be charged for any damage or loss caused by design or
negligence during the occupancy of the room.

14.7

Every resident shall comply with the hostel rules and regulations in effect at the time. The
hostel regulations may be framed by Wardens and Dean, Student Affairs and may be
amended from time to time. These regulations may contain norms on visiting hours, policy
for owning and parking of vehicles, waste segregation, usage of various resources including
fair usage policy of electricity, internet, air conditioning, etc.

If a Student violates the hostel regulations or various policies in effect, Dean, Student Affairs
may take necessary action including disciplinary action, levying fines, withdrawal of privileges
related to usage of various resources, or possibly eviction from hostel premises and

14.11 Use of liquor, drugs, or any other intoxicants, prohibited substances in the Hostel premises is

GLOSSARY
Academics – activities concerned with teaching and research.
Academic Calendar – a Senate-approved official schedule of activities that specify the timeline,
sequence, and deadlines of these activities which are academic in nature.
Academic Credit or Credit – the numerical weightage assigned to a Course or other Senate-approved
activities, the accumulation of which is necessary for fulfilling the requirements of obtaining
Academic Degree.
Academic Degree – an undergraduate, postgraduate, or doctoral qualification, including diplomas
and other academic distinctions, conferred by the Institute upon a Student who has successfully
fulfilled all prescribed requirements for the corresponding Academic Programme in a Discipline
subject to the approval as per the guidelines of the Senate.
Academic Programme – the specific academic pathway as approved by the Board with structure
prescribed, and amended by the Senate from time to time, that shall lead to award of an Academic
Degree.
Academic Transcript or Transcript – an official document issued by the competent authority of the
Institute providing a complete and chronological record of a Student’s academic performance
covering all registered Courses, Academic Credits earned, Grades awarded, Semester Performance
Indices (SPI) and Cumulative Performance Index (CPI).
Credits Earned – the total number of Academic Credits successfully acquired by a Student upon
satisfactory completion of registered Courses and other Senate-approved activities.
Course – a subject or module of study within an Academic Programme that is imparted and evaluated
during a designated period in the Academic Calendar, and the successful completion of which a
Student may earn a defined number of Academic Credits towards an Academic Degree.
Cumulative Performance Index (CPI) – the ratio of total credit-weighted grade points earned to the
total Academic Credits registered across all completed Terms, subject to Senate-approved rules
governing inclusion, exclusion, or substitution of Grades.
Curriculum – the comprehensive academic framework and structured sequence of Courses and
approved academic activities prescribed for a specific Academic Programme in a specific Discipline.
Department – a unit of the Institute established by the Board of Governors or as specified in the
Statutes, responsible for administration of Academic Programme(s) in one or more relevant
Disciplines.
Discipline – a unit of the Institute responsible for academic management of specialized field of study
in which the Institute offers Academic Programme(s) administered by one or more Departments.
Exam 1 – a scheduled academic assessment conducted approximately midway through a Term as
prescribed by the Academic Calendar.
Exam 2 – a scheduled academic assessment conducted at the conclusion of the instructional period
of a Term as prescribed by the Academic Calendar.

Final Defense – the formal oral presentation and examination of a Student’s research work – as
embodied in a dissertation or a thesis – before a Senate-approved examination committee.
Final Defense Committee – a formally constituted academic committee responsible for conducting
the Final Defense of a PhD Student after the completion of Senate-approved thesis review or its
waiver if so approved.
Grade or Letter Grade – a letter symbol awarded to a Student in recognition of their performance in
a Course or approved academic activity, that may embody numerical Grade Points or qualitatively
convey Satisfactory / Unsatisfactory performance, as prescribed by the Senate.
Grade Points – Senate-approved numerical value assigned to a specific Grade mapped on a Senateapproved scale that is awarded to a Student upon completion of a Course.
Graduation Requirement – the prescribed set of Senate-approved academic and non-academic
conditions that must be successfully fulfilled by a Student for the award of an Academic Degree.
Honour Code – a framework of conduct based on honesty, integrity and mutual respect ensuring
Students uphold high moral and ethical standards in all their activities and pursuits in the Institute.
Institute – refers to the Indian Institute of Technology Gandhinagar.
Instructor – an individual entrusted with the teaching and evaluation of a Course during a Term.
Instructor-in-charge – the designated Instructor entrusted with execution and oversight of a specific
Course offered in a Term that involves multiple Instructors.
Make-up Examination – an alternative academic assessment specially administered to Students who
are officially absent from scheduled examinations (such as Exam 1 or Exam 2) during the Term based
on formal approval of the competent authority and as per the procedures and conditions prescribed
by the Senate.
Postgraduate Student – a Student enrolled in a Senate-approved Academic Programme of the
Institute for which a degree is required as a qualifying criteria.
Proficiency Transcript – a supplementary academic record issued by the Institute formally detailing
a record of Courses completed by a Student that are distinct from or over and above the Graduation
Requirements of their Academic Programme.
Quad – a short period contained within a Term, as stipulated in the Academic Calendar, for
administration of Courses and approved academic activities.
Registration – the mandatory, formal process conducted at the commencement of each Term, as
stipulated in the Academic Calendar, wherein a Student officially enrolls in Courses and remits the
applicable fees.
Semester Term or Term – the stipulated continuous period for the administration of Academic
Programmes, the exact duration, commencement, and conclusion of which are governed by
Academic Calendar.

Semester Performance Index (SPI) – the ratio of the total credit-weighted grade points earned to the
total Academic Credits registered in a specific Term subject to Senate-approved rules governing
inclusion, exclusion or substitution of grades.
Supervisor – an individual appointed by the competent authority of the Institute to guide, oversee,
and evaluate the thesis or project work of a Student.
Student – an individual admitted to the Institute through prescribed procedures to pursue a
designated Academic Programme and whose name is borne on the rolls of the Institute.
Summer / Winter Term – the academic sessions scheduled in the intervening periods between
regular Semester Terms in the Academic Calendar for administering Courses following Senateapproved guidelines.
