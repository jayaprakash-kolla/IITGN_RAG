COMMUNITY

PROGRAM

NEEV is an IIT Gandhinagar community outreach
program. It is actively working to bridge the knowledge
and communication gap by offering comprehensive
training and mentoring, and growing the employability
and entrepreneurship mind set of the surrounding
communities.
NEEV's mission is to empower grassroots communities
through workforce development, with a focus on
entrepreneurship and skills training. NEEV’s focus
beneficiaries include youth and women from rural and
urban localities from the areas surrounding IIT
Gandhinagar.
