IIT Gandhinagar (IITGN) has been making
impactful strides in the domains of academics,
research, industry collaborations, and
innovation. It has embarked on an ambitious
journey to become among the best researchintensive educational institutions globally.
The Institute has created an ecosystem where
inclusivity, diversity, equity, merit, and flat
governance structures are prioritized.
Here, faculty have the opportunity to build a world-class
research program, experiment with innovative and impactful
teaching, and contribute to institutional building through
their service.
