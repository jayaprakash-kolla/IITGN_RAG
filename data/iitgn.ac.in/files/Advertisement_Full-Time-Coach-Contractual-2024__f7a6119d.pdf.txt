INDIAN INSTITUTE OF TECHNOLOGY GANDHINAGAR

Full Time Sports Coaches (Program Assistant I)
The Indian Institute of Technology Gandhinagar (IITGN) Sports Section invites
applications from passionate and highly motivated sports coaches. The institute is
offering the five positions of Program Assistant I on contract basis, initially for a
period of two years, with the possibility of extension based on the incumbent’s
satisfactory performance and the institute’s requirements.

Job Responsibilities/ Duties:
●
●
●
●
●
●
●

Sports coaching students and staff.
Preparing a sports coaching plan and its execution.
Conducting Physical Education (PE) Classes.
Responsible for maintenance of fields/courts and equipment.
Maintaining records related to the associated sports.
Proactive engagement of students and staff in sports activities.
Other works assigned to by the sports head.

Required Qualifications
‒

Bachelor’s degree in physical education with at least 02 years of relevant coaching
experience.

Desirable:
‒ Diploma in sports coaching from NSNIS
‒ Specialization in coaching for Table Tennis, Lawn Tennis, Badminton, Athletics, or Yoga
