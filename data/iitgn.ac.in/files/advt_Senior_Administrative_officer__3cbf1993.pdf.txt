Job Description

Event communication and media coordination.

The selected candidate will lead the Media and Communication Office and be
responsible for planning and executing the Institute's communication strategy
to strengthen its national and international visibility. The responsibilities
include:
● Develop and implement the Institute's strategic communication and
branding initiatives.
● Lead media relations and serve as the primary point of contact for print,
electronic, and digital media.
● Plan and oversee media outreach, press conferences, interviews, and
public announcements.
● Prepare and review press releases, speeches, reports, newsletters,
brochures, annual reports, and other institutional publications.
● Oversee the Institute's digital communication channels, including the
website and social media platforms.
● Develop communication campaigns highlighting research achievements,
academic excellence, innovation, student success, and institutional
initiatives.
● Coordinate communication support for conferences, workshops,
convocations, and other major Institute events.
● Manage crisis communication and safeguard the Institute's reputation.
● Build and maintain strong relationships with media professionals,
government agencies, industry partners, alumni, and other stakeholders.
● Lead and mentor the Media and Communication Office team while
ensuring high-quality, timely communication outputs.
● Monitor media coverage and communication analytics, and recommend
strategies for continuous improvement.
● Perform any other communication-related responsibilities assigned by the
Institute from time to time.
