International Collaborative Research Opportunity For Undergraduate Students
A joint research initiative by Underwriters Laboratory (UL), Northbrook, USA and IITGN
attracted 80 IITGN students to take up the challenge to come up with innovative ideas to
reduce hazards for fire fighters in urban India, and to create a database for fire related disasters
in the country. Students visited Fire Services at Delhi, Mumbai and Ahmedabad, including a
10 days stay in Delhi during December 2009 and interacted with Fire Department officials
during these visits. The students were mentored in their research efforts by UL experts from
US and India and the Fire Chiefs of Delhi, Maharashtra and Ahmedabad. Students, working
in teams of 2 each, presented their ideas during 30-31 January 2010. The jury panel lauded the
research work of the young participants, and selected the following four teams as winners for
the Conceptualization Phase of the project:
Team 1:

Anchit Gaurav and Adnan Ahmad Ansari

Team2:

Vivek Yadav and Abhishek Umrao

Team3:

Saurya Prakash Sinha and Divyank Singh

Team4:
Shruti Jain and Ekta Prashnani
All participating students received certificates for their efforts.
The four winning teams are now working on developing the
technology/database. To facilitate their work, all eight students
have been granted three weeks internships at UL, Northbrook,
USA during summer 2010. The winning teams were also invited
to present their ideas at a dinner on March 28, 2010 during the
International Conference on Disaster Mitigation through Fire
Safety and Earthquake Engineering. The teams will submit their
Phase-II reports by October 2010. Phase-III of the project will
involve implementation. This collaborative research initiative
will continue next year with new challenges.

Blithchron '10
'BLITHCHRON' IIT Gandhinagar's Annual Cultural Festival

was held on January 23-24, 2010. The major attraction at
Blithchron 10 was the Pro Show by the band Parikrama,
India's most popular rock band. The two-day festival was
witnessed by a total of 6000 people from more than 40 colleges
across Ahmedabad and Gandhinagar. With the sponsors
having been promised a footfall of 4000+ the festival was much
larger than expected, with growth from last year's 1000
footfall. The title sponsor for the festival was SBI. Ceat and
ONGC were the co-sponsors with other major contributors
being Suzlon, Tata Steel, ITC, Inox and Coco Cola. The band
wars-“String Theory” featuring 6 of the best bands from across
India competed for the prize money, with the participants and
audience both agreeing that Blithchron has the best rock band
competition in Gujarat. A spectacular drama “She” was
performed by the theatre group Entertainment Engineers
consisting of alumni of IIT Bombay. The other Flagship events
“The Butler Did It” and “Jobless” were appreciated by the
numerous participants and media personnel. Technical events
were also introduced in this version of Blithchron 10.
