INDIAN INSTITUTE OF TECHNOLOGY GANDHINAGAR
Advertisement for Trainee positions at Institute Works Department
IIT Gandhinagar is looking for young, dynamic, tech-savvy, highly motivated and committed Diploma/Degree graduates to work as
Trainee purely on a temporary basis for a period of one year. This is an opportunity to learn while you work on different functions &
services of the Institute Works Department (IWD).
Positions: Trainee positions (03) in the areas of Civil/ Electrical/ Mechanical/ Horticulture. Check details in Annexure I.
Duration: One year
Remuneration: A monthly consolidated remuneration in the range of Rs. 18,000/- to 25,000/- per month (commensurate with
educational qualifications). No other allowances are admissible.
How to apply: Interested candidates for the above-mentioned positions may apply online by clicking here. The application form can
be filled latest by 15 July 2025, 18:00 Hours.
Terms and Conditions of Engagement
1. Selection will be based on interview and only the shortlisted candidates shall be intimated well in advance the date and time of
the interview. No TA/DA would be paid for attending the interview, in case the interviews are scheduled in person.
2. The incumbent will not have any legal right to claim his/her regularization/appointment by absorption or otherwise against any
regular posts or any other further contractual engagement on termination of the agreement.
3. The trainee has to work for six days a week (Monday to Saturday). He/she may also be required to work during evenings and
even on holidays, as the work demands.
4. Beyond the assigned regular scope of work, the trainee is required to attain the job consigned by the reporting officer or any
other concerned person from time to time.
5. The Institute reserves the right to reject any or all applications without assigning any reason. Institute reserves the right to
waive/relax any conditions. The Institute reserves the right not to fill-up any of these positions.
6. The applicant must have successfully completed their Diploma/Degree in the year 2025.
7. The applicant must be not above the age of 25 years as on 01.06.2025.
Accommodation: Bachelor accommodation may be provided to the selected candidates against payment of applicable charges, as
per institute norms, subject to availability.
