Medical Insurance Facility to the Students of IITGN
All on-roll students are covered under the Medical Insurance scheme for inpatient
treatment.
Following are the salient features of the Medical Insurance Policy:
1. The insurance covers hospitalization expenses of up to ₹ 1,50,000/-.
2. Room rent limit is 2% & 4% (per day) of Sum Insured for Normal & ICU/ICCU
respectively. Accordingly, the upper limit of room rent is Rs. 3,000/- per day for
normal hospitalisation and Rs. 6,000/- for ICU/ICCU treatment. Students can avail
rooms within these specified upper limits of room rent. Students may note that the
insurance claim covers only the expenses corresponding to these eligible room rent
charges. In case a student opts for a hospital room having rent charges more than
the above prescribed room rent limits, the difference of charges for the room rent as
well as other corresponding higher charges shall be payable by the respective
student only.
3. Students get cover from Day 1 with cashless hospitalization facilities in network
hospitals of Insurance company (including provisioning medical health cards for the
insured students). Ambulance Charges are covered upto Rs. 3,000/- per claim.
In case of any difficulty/concern in this regard, students may contact the Student Affairs
office.
