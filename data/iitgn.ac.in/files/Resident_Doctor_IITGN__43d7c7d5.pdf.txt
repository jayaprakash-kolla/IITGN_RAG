Resident Medical Doctor at IIT Gandhinagar
Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Gandhinagar invites applications from dedicated and skilled
medical professionals looking for an exciting opportunity to make a positive impact on a vibrant
community. The institute is offering the position of Resident Medical Doctor on contract basis,
initially for a period of one year, with the possibility of extension based on the incumbent’s
satisfactory performance and the institute’s requirements.
About IIT Gandhinagar:
IIT Gandhinagar is a premier institute dedicated to excellence in education, research, and
innovation. It is known for fostering an environment that encourages interdisciplinary learning,
cutting-edge research, and the holistic development of students. As a Resident Medical Doctor,
one will have the opportunity to play a crucial role in supporting the health and well-being of the
3500+ campus community.

Provide medical care and attend to patients at the Institute's Medical Centre.
Offer expert consultation, diagnosis, and treatment to students, faculty and staff.
Contribute to the Institute's outreach program and community health initiatives.
Attend to emergency cases on a call basis beyond stipulated duty hours.
Collaborate with the campus healthcare team to ensure comprehensive medical support.

Required Qualifications:
MBBS degree, including successful completion of a compulsory rotating internship, with at least
60% marks or equivalent grade. The applicant should be a registered medical practitioner with
qualifications recognised by the National Medical Commission (NMC). The applicant must be
first class throughout academics.
Desirable:
• Three years or more experience in a recognized hospital.
• MD or DNB in an appropriate branch of medicine.
Age Limit: Up to 35 years, or retired doctors with government or services experience.
Duration of Appointment: Initially for a period of one year, with the possibility of extension,
based on the incumbent’s performance and Institute’s requirements.
Remuneration: A fixed remuneration ranging from Rs. 70,000/- to Rs. 1,00,000/- per month
(based on qualifications and experiences).
Perks and Benefits:
• Access to facilities available to campus residents (Library, International standard sports
complex, campus events and activities).
• Suitable accommodation provided within the campus.
How to Apply: Interested candidates may apply online here.
Application Deadline: 25th November 2026.
Skilled medical professionals are invited to apply for this position to be a part of a dynamic
