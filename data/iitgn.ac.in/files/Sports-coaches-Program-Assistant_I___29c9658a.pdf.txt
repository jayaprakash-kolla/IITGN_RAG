INDIAN INSTITUTE OF TECHNOLOGY GANDHINAGAR

Full Time Sports Coaches (Program Assistant I)
The Indian Institute of Technology Gandhinagar (IITGN) invites applications from passionate and
highly motivated individuals for the position of full-time sports coaches in Football (one position),
Yoga (one position), Lawn Tennis (one position), Badminton (one position),Swimming (one
positions) and Athletics (one position). The institute is offering these six positions of full-time
sports coaches on contract basis, initially for a period of one year, with the possibility of extension
based on the incumbent’s satisfactory performance and the institute’s requirements.

Job Responsibilities/ Duties:
●
●

Sports coaching students and staff.
Preparing a sports coaching plan and its execution.
Conducting Physical Education (PE) Classes.
Responsible for maintenance of fields/courts and equipment.
Maintaining records related to the associated sports.
Proactive engagement of students and staff in sports activities.
Other works assigned to by the sports head.

Required Qualifications
‒

Bachelor’s Degree in physical education with at least 02 years of relevant coaching
experience.

Desirable:
‒ Diploma in sports coaching from NSNIS
‒ Specialization in coaching for Football, Yoga, Lawn Tennis, Badminton, Swimming and
Athletics.
