Institute News
Third Batch Joins: Orientation Programme
The Institute admitted its third batch of 128 students (112 boys and 16 girls) on July 22, 2010. An orientation
programme for the new students was held during July 22-27, 2010 for their smooth transition from school and home
atmosphere to the IIT campus. The chief guest, Dr A L Ravimohan, a former Vice President of Technology, Castrol
International, Mumbai, India in his inaugural address inspired the students to be 'Creative Scientist Engineers' and to
take leadership roles. Dr Ravimohan is a President's Gold Medal winning BTech (1967) from IIT Bombay and PhD
(1971) from California Institute of Technology, USA. Director Sudhir K Jain, emphasized hard work as the key to
success for the students.
A number of workshops formed the majority of Orientation
process. These included Sailing Through IIT by Ms Madhu
Chadda, aimed at stress management, time management,
creativity and development of IIT culture, Celebrating the
Unique ME by Ms Jasbir Thadhani, Counseling Psychologist
of IIT Gandhinagar on discovering own uniqueness, What is it
like to be an IITian: Facts and Fictions by Prof Jayant
Kanitkar, and Personality Development by Ms Rashmi
Datt, to develop teamwork, interpersonal communication skills
and crisis management. The students were also exposed to the
Cultural and Architectural Heritage of Ahmedabad by
Theater and Media Centre, Ahmedabad. The parents and the
Institute faculty discussed how parents could be part of IITGN's vision and mission. As a part of the Orientation programme, three faculty
members and the Institute Counseling Psychologist talked to all the students on one-to-one basis to understand their backgrounds, goals,
hopes, aspirations, and concerns. The exercise was aimed at preparing the new students share their issues, academic or personal, if and when
needed, with any faculty member without any hesitation. It also helps the Institute to discover and develop the talents of students.
Second Meeting of the Board of Governors
Second meeting of the Board of Governors was held on September 21, 2010. Besides routine matters, the Board considered and approved
important proposals helping push excellence in the Institute. These proposals included: a document Vision of the Institute; the document
Partnerships in Pursuit of Excellence: Fund Raising Strategy that outlines action plan for raising money and seeking active partners
who share our vision and are willing to help; Student Participation in Academic Senate of the Institute and the Senate Committees; Merit
Scholarships for Students based only on their performance in Academics, Sports & Games, Arts/Culture, or Social Works & Leadership;
and Excellence Awards to Faculty Members for outstanding contributions in the fields of Teaching, Research, Institution Building and
Outreach.

Activities at IITGN
IIT Gandhinagar Vision
A Vision Retreat was organized to evolve IIT Gandhinagar's Mission, Vision, Values, and Principles
with a view to make it a great place to live and work, and to make it a preferred destination for future
generations of students, staff and faculty. A select group of faculty, students, non-teaching staff,
invited guests and the Director discussed and debated the related issues on August 27 and 28, 2010
at a prominent hotel in Ahmedabad. Prof Jayant Kanitkar, primary motivator of the event, led the
discussions. A draft document thus prepared was later shared with the entire Institute community
for comments and suggestions. The final document was then presented to the Board of Governors
for consideration.
Non Degree Students at IITGN
With a view to enrich the learning environment through diversity of the student body, IITGN will admit, through a rigorous
process, a limited number of undergraduate and postgraduate students to pursue course work or research for one or two
semesters. This work can count towards the degree requirements at the student's own Institute or University within India or
abroad but not at IITGN. Mr Ashutosh Singla from L N Mittal Institute of Information Technology, Jaipur is the first to join
the Institute under this provision in July 2010 for one semester.
Inauguration of a New Building
A newly built 1000 sq.m building was formally inaugurated on July
15, 2010 by Prof Vedanth Kadambi, the senior most Visiting
Professor of the Institute in the presence of faculty, staff and
students of IITGN and VGEC and CPWD officials. It has been
built by IITGN on the VGEC campus through the Central PWD in
a record time; a total of seven months from preliminary proposal
from CPWD (December 18, 2009) to the inauguration (July 15,
2010). The building houses Mechanical Engineering and Physics
laboratories, offices of the faculty, research scholars and their
research facility.

Earn While You Learn Programme
The Institute encourages undergraduate students with good
academic performance to undertake academic responsibilities like
grading home work, helping users of Computer Centre, assisting
Institute faculty in book writing, and providing help in the Library.
The selected students are permitted to devote upto 10 hours per
week on this activity, and are paid an honorarium for their time so
that it is truly an “Earn While You Learn” Programme. A total of 22
students were selected to participate in this programme for the first
semester of 2010-11.

New Research Scholars
Eight new PhD scholars have been admitted to the Institute during

Women's Cell Constituted
A Women's Cell has been constituted at the Institute with Prof
Joycee Mekie as the Convener and seven members drawn from
faculty, staff and students. The primary purpose of the Cell is to help
the Institute protect people against sexual harassment and to
maintain/protect their dignity. The Institute's Women's Cell is
committed to serving everyone living or working on the campus
without a gender bias. This cell is also being called CARE (Cell And
Resource for Everyone).

Student and Staff Activities
Public Speaking and Leadership Club
A Public Speaking and Leadership Club, similar to the Toastmasters
Club, started at the Institute provides a platform to improve the skills
of speaking in public and turning situations around to one's
advantage. It meets every Saturday from 4:00 pm to 5:00 pm under
the leadership of Prof Jayant Kanitkar. The members deliver one
minute extempore or two minutes prepared speeches. The club is
attracting students in large numbers.
Foundation day on August 2, 2010
On the occasion of IITGN's foundation day, the Students Council
organized a Brainstorming Session on Student Vision for
IITGN on August 2, 2010. Fifteen faculty members and about 160
students participated in the discussions.

Janmashtami Celebrations
There was grand celebration for Janmashtami on September 2, 2010.
The traditional Matki Phod "Dahihandi" presented a real challenge
to the students. They had to make a four layer human pyramid to
reach the curd laden pot. Girls also attempted to break the pot
through a three layer pyramid of their own. The whole atmosphere
was fun filled, probably a good way to remember Lord Krishna.
Ganesh Chaturthi
Pratisthapana of Ganpati Bappa was carried out by the students in

escorted Ganpati back for 'Visarjan' in Sabarmati River.
