Dr Thirthankar Chakraborty
Assistant Professor
School of Humanities and Social Sciences, IIT Mandi
Dr Thirthankar Chakraborty is an assistant
professor in the School of Humanities and Social
Sciences at the Indian Institute of Technology
Mandi, and was recently a visiting fellow at KU
Leuven’s Centre for Irish Studies. He wrote his
PhD on ‘Samuel Beckett and Indian Literature’
at the University of Kent and later co-edited the
volume on Samuel Beckett as World Literature
(2020). He is currently working on a project
regarding the literatures of the Himalayan region
while exploring new ways of theorising contexts
and debates in world literature studies.
