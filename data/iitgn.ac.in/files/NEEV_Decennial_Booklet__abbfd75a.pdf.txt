Celebrating 10 Years of NEEV: From Roots to Fruits

This is the story of NEEV IIT Gandhinagar. What began in 2014 as a modest initiative has grown into a
transformative journey of empowerment, growth, and resilience. The idea then was simple: to nurture
local entrepreneurship. Partnering with | Create India, we started training grassroots entrepreneurs.
Small beginnings led to big dreams—a security guard opened a canteen, a janitor bought a rickshaw, and
women launched beauty salons. Each story added a stepping stone to our vision.

When we moved to our permanent campus in Gandhinagar, our neighbours—villages like Palaj and Basan
—brought new opportunities. With state-of-the-art labs and dedicated partnerships, we introduced
vocational courses in carpentry, wiring, and welding. 2016 marked a turning point. For the first time,
women from Palaj created curtains for IITGN hostels. This small project planted the seed for women
empowerment at NEEV, a journey we’ve nurtured ever since. Our partnership with the Desai Foundation
grew stronger. Together, we expanded programs to include sewing training, beautician training,
computer skills, and more. Soon, NEEV’s impact reached beyond Palaj and Basan to other nearby
villages. Women began crafting conference bags, stoles, and pouches—merging creativity with
livelihood opportunities.

When the pandemic struck, NEEV adapted. Women produced masks for the community, and we
distributed essential ration kits. With safety measures in place, we continued to offer skill training—
adding new courses like chocolate-candle making, specialised sewing courses, Tally Skills Course, and
Desktop publishing. During this time, we also played a crucial role in health awareness too—conducting
mask distribution drives and oximeter training sessions to support affected families.
Keeping pace with changing times, NEEV soon introduced advanced programs—Website design using
WordPress, and Graphic design using Canva, while also including Al tools in all of its technical courses,
in addition to all its regular programs. These courses are opening doors for youth to explore careers in
today’s digital economy. For women, our awareness sessions on menstrual health, savings, and banking
have been empowering steps toward self-reliance. Career development programs and entrepreneurship
programs are helping youth dream bigger and take bold steps forward. Through our sewing livelihood
project, women now produce artisanal products that tell a story of resilience and creativity. From tote
bags to cushion covers, every stitch reflects the dignity and confidence NEEV aims to foster.

Since 2014, NEEV has carried out over 170 projects, positively influencing the lives of more than 6,000
individuals across 40+ villages. Yet, beyond these numbers lies the true essence of our journey—the
empowerment, self-reliance, and opportunities that have blossomed through this initiative. NEEV
represents the collective efforts of IIT Gandhinagar's leadership, faculty, staff, students, and the
invaluable contributions of partners and supporters such as the Desai Foundation, Milacron, GSPL,
GIDC, | Create, SAP SoSa, and Masibus. Together, they have made NEEV a pillar of inspiration and
progress for the communities it serves.

This booklet brings together selected stories of impact and success from the last decade, showcasing

the breadth and depth of NEEV’s transformative journey. While it offers a glimpse into the lives changed

and dreams nurtured, it represents only a fraction of the many stories that define NEEV’s journey. As we

celebrate 10 years of meaningful impact, we honor the past, cherish the present, and look forward to
cultivating a brighter future together.
