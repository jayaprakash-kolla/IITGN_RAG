## Shah Scholarship

 The Shah Scholarship has been established by Aarushi Shah to support female M.Sc. students at IIT Gandhinagar. The scholarship amount is Rs. 1 lakh per student per year and is awarded to one eligible student every year. Female M.Sc. students with a minimum CPI of 6.5 are eligible. The scholarship supports internships, both international and domestic, and related expenses such as visas, flights and accommodation wherever applicable, as well as special projects and other opportunities.

 Ms. Aarushi Shah graduated from IIT Gandhinagar in 2019 with a Master of Science in Cognitive Science. Her time at IIT Gandhinagar was transformative, from teaching and peers to campus life. During her tenure, she also received two foreign scholarships that helped her expand her horizons. In her last appointment, she worked at the University of Cambridge, UK, for the Winton Centre for Risk and Evidence Communication. She currently works with Noora Health.

 The Shah Scholarship has been established by Aarushi Shah in honor of her mother, Dr. Avani Shah, and grandmother, Late Dr. Pushpa Shah, whose inspiring journeys as doctors and trailblazers deeply shaped her path. The scholarship is instituted to encourage young women to travel far and wide and pursue their dreams. Having received the support of her family and friends throughout her journey, Aarushi wishes to pay it forward through this scholarship and help students explore opportunities beyond the classroom.

 “This scholarship is for girls who’ve been enterprising and secured acceptance for an internship, domestically or internationally. I hope it helps you in seeing more of the world, ever curious, ever learning - beyond the books.”
