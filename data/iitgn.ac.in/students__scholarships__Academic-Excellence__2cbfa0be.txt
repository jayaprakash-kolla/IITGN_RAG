## Scholarship for Academic Excellence

 With an aim to reward academic excellence, each of the students who secure the highest CPI in their respective disciplines, are awarded ₹ 2,000 per month for ten months. However, if the awardee is also recipient of any scholarship of equal or higher value, then he/she is eligible only for a one-time receipt of ₹ 5000.
