Project: “Earthquake-Safe Buildings” (Technical Booklet) - Translated the original English booklet (and its Hindi version by Prof. Manish Kumar) into Urdu. This project required translating complex civil engineering concepts into simple, vernacular Urdu. The goal is to democratize safety knowledge, allowing a wider Urdu-speaking demographic to understand modern earthquake-resistant construction techniques.
