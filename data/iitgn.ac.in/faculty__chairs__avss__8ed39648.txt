## Dr. A.V. Anilkumar

Dr. A.V. Anilkumar is a professor of Aerospace and Mechanical Engineering Practice and Director of the Aerospace Design Laboratory at Vanderbilt University. He holds a PhD in Mechanical Engineering and Aeronautics from the Graduate Aerospace Laboratories at the California Institute of Technology. He has been a NASA investigator of microgravity fluid physics, and materials processing phenomena on the International Space Station and on Space Shuttle flights. As director of the Vanderbilt Aerospace Design Laboratory he has developed a highly-acclaimed cross-disciplinary and innovative rocket flight dynamics, reaction control and payload design program that is at the cutting edge of Space Exploration, preparing students for challenging careers in the contemporary Space Launch industries. Professor Anilkumar has been honored as the faculty advisor of the year, and the Abe Zarem educator of the year by the American Institute of Aeronautics and Astronautics. Vanderbilt University has honored him with the Chancellor’s Cup, for the greatest intellectual and academic contributions to undergraduate students outside of classroom teaching, and the Vanderbilt School of Engineering award for excellence in teaching.

## Dr. Krishna Kanti Dey

Dr. Krishna Kanti Dey is Associate Professor of Physics at IIT Gandhinagar, where he heads the Laboratory of Soft and Living Materials (LSLM). His research explores active matter physics, colloidal dynamics, and nanotechnology with a particular focus on designing small-scale, autonomous synthetic motors and machines that self-propel and exhibit coordinated behavior in fluidic environments without external control.

 He completed his BSc (2002) at Gauhati University, Guwahati, followed by an MSc in Physics (2004) and a PhD in Nanotechnology (2011) at IIT Guwahati. He then carried out postdoctoral research (2012–2016) at The Pennsylvania State University, where he investigated enzyme-powered micro/nanomotors and the physics of chemically propelled molecular machines with publications in journals including JACS, Angewandte Chemie, and Nano Letters.

 He joined IIT Gandhinagar as Assistant Professor in August 2016 and was promoted to Associate Professor in September 2023.

 His lab investigates how chemical energy is converted into mechanical fluctuations and directed motion at the micro- and nanoscale to understand and engineer dynamic systems inspired by biological active matter. Using microfluidics, spectroscopy, and advanced microscopy, the group studies the dynamics of biological and synthetic active systems, from individual particles to collective behavior in complex and crowded environments. Current research focuses on enzyme-driven active fluctuations and micro/nanomotor propulsion, as well as their implications for molecular transport and cellular processes. Recent work from the lab has shown that enzyme-generated mechanical fluctuations can enhance receptor-mediated endocytosis and cellular uptake in epithelial cells.

Research Interests: Active Matter · Colloidal Dynamics · Nanotechnology
