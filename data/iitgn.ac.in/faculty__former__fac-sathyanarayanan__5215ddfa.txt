## Sathyanarayanan Mundayoor

Scholar-in-Residence, Humanities and Social Sciences

- BSc: Bombay University, 1972
- PG Diploma in Journalism: Bhavans Mumbai, 1973
- MA Linguistics: Bombay University, 1979
- Post-MA Diploma: Delhi University, 1998

Email: sathyanarayanan.m -AT- iitgn.ac.in

Office:  AB 6/317

- 2012: authored a book “Arunachal Natodikathakal” (Mathrubhumi Books) on Arunachal Folktales in Malayalam. Mathrubhumi has released its 3rd edition in May 2022.
- 2020: co-author of “Monster of the Golden Valley”, the English translation of Arunachal folktales.

- Coordinator, Lohit Youth Library Network, HQ: Tezu , Arunachal Pradesh
- Member, Vivekananda Trust, Mysore
- Academic Advisor, RIWATCH, a cultural research institute at Roing, Arunachal Pradesh ( all positions honorary & non-salaried)
- Advisor, Reading Promotion Endowment Trust for Arunachal (RPETA) -a charitable Trust based in Delhi, supporting reading promotion activities in Arunachal
- Hon. Professor of Library Science at Arunachal University of Studies, Namsai, Arun. Pr.

Awards

- Feb 2008: Arunachal Pradesh Governor’s Silver Medal, for meritorious services to the youth.
- Mar 2007: Roll of Honour 2007 by the Association of Writers & Illustrators for Children (AWIC), New Delhi, for outstanding reading promotion efforts among Himalayan tribalsocieties.
- Dec 2102: Featured in the programme ‘Amazing Indians’ in the Times Now TV in Dec 2012.
- Nov 2016: Has been inducted into the Civil Society Hall of Fame in November, 2016, as Coordinator of the Lohit Youth Library Network.
- Sept 2018: Lifetime Achievement Award from Sree Narayana Guru Cultural Centre, Shillong
- Feb 2019 & 2020: Honour by the CALSOM, the apex body of the Mishmi tribal community of Lohit district.
- Jan 2020: Govt of India declares conferring Padma Shri Award. Award presented on Nov 8, 2021
- Jul 2022: Dakshina - Ravunni Menon Award for educational activism in Arunachal - by Dakshina, Chennai
- Apr 2022: Arunachal University of Studies, (AUS) Namsai, (Arunachal Pradesh) confers Honorary Professorship of Library Sciences of the University.
- Sep 3, 2022: Bhagwan Mahavir Foundation, Chennai 25th Mahaveer Award for Excellence in Human Endeavour in the field of Education.
