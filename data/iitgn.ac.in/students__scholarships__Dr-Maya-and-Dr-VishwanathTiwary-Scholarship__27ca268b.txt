## Dr Maya and Dr Vishwanath Tiwary Scholarship

 Dr Maya and Dr Vishwanath Tiwary Scholarship has been instituted in the year 2023 by Abhiroop Mishra and is open to all female BTech students of Materials Engineering. The scholarship amount is Rs. 1 lakh per student per year and is awarded to one female BTech student of materials engineering every year in order to support internships (international or domestic), special projects and opportunities, financial needs, etc. BTech students with a minimum CPI of 6.5 are eligible.

 Abhiroop Mishra is a BTech Materials Science and Engineering graduate of the Class of 2019. At IITGN, he pursued a research internship as an SN BOSE fellow at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign. Currently, he is pursuing a PhD at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign as a Link Foundation Fellow studying the degradation of Li-ion batteries. Dr Maya and Dr Vishwanath Tiwary Scholarship has been instituted in honor of his maternal grandparents, Dr Maya and Dr Vishwanath Tiwary. The scholarship of Rs. 1 Lakh per year, would support the female undergraduate students of materials engineering for their financial needs, funding internships (international or domestic), or special projects and opportunities, etc.
