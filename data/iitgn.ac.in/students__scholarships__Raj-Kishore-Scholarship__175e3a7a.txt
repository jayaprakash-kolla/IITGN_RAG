## Raj Kishore Scholarship

 Raj Kishore Scholarship has been instituted in the year 2022 and is open to all UG (B.Tech) and Master students. The scholarship amount is Rs. 1 lakh per student per year and is awarded to one UG (B.Tech) or Master student every year in order to support internships (international or domestic), special projects and opportunities, financial needs, etc. UG (B.Tech) and Master students with a minimum CPI of 6.5 are eligible.

 A well wisher of IIT Gandhinagar has instituted this scholarship.
