## Satyaram Scholarships

 The Satyaram Scholarships were instituted by the former Indian Cricketer and philanthropist V. V. S. Laxman in the year 2016 and are being facilitated through the NGO ‘Yuva Unstoppable”. Students with a minimum CPI of 6.5 (CPI is not applicable for first-year students) and whose family annual income is not more than 3 lakhs are eligible for this scholarship. The scholarship amount is Rs. 1 lakh per year per student and a total of 11 scholarships were awarded Satyaram Scholarships in the year 2018-19. The recipient student continues to get the scholarship support till completion of his/her B.Tech Program at IIT Gandhinagar, subject to meeting the eligibility criteria. The awardee is expected to financially help at least one needy IIT Gandhinagar student in future.
