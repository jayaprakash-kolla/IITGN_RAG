# Centre for Biomedical Engineering

- Careers
Centre for Biomedical Engineering

The Biomedical Engineering Centre was one of the first centres to be set up at IITGN. The Centre promotes collaborative research activities primarily in the domains of:

- Diagnostic/Therapeutic Tools and Techniques
- Automated Rehabilitation and Surgical Techniques
- Public Health Techniques
Several labs collaborate to work on projects along these three themes. More details can be obtained by taking a look at individual labs under the different disciplines.

The Biomedical Engineering Centre at IITGN seeks outstanding researcher with research interests aligned with the themes described above. Interested candidates are encouraged to apply through appropriate discipline at IITGN.
