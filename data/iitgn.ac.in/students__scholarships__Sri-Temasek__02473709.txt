## Sri Temasek@IIT Gandhinagar Scholarship

 Sri Temasek@IIT Gandhinagar Scholarship was instituted in 2016 by a well-wisher from Singapore who appreciates his opportunity to attend IIT Kanpur for his B.Tech Program in a field which he loves and also to serve as a Professor at IIT Gandhinagar. This Merit-cum-Means Scholarship is awarded to one undergraduate student every year (open for second, third and fourth year students). Students with a minimum CPI of 6.5 and whose family annual income is not more than Rs. 8 lakhs are eligible to avail this scholarship. The scholarship amount is Rs. 20,000 per academic year.
