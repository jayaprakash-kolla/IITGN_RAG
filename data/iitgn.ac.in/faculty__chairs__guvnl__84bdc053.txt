## GUVNL Chair

 IIT Gandhinagar set up a faculty chair, the GUVNL Chair in Next-Gen Energy and Intelligent Grid Infrastructure, through a generous endowment from Gujarat Urja Vikas Nigam Limited (GUVNL).

The Chair aims to advance excellence in research, education, innovation, and capacity building in the power and energy sector, with a focus on emerging areas such as renewable energy, intelligent grids, artificial intelligence, and machine learning for power systems, energy-system resilience, green hydrogen, energy storage, electricity markets, and energy-system optimization.

 Gujarat Urja Vikas Nigam Limited (GUVNL)

##### Endowment

 The Chair supports interdisciplinary research, nurtures outstanding researchers and students, facilitates international collaborations, and promotes capacity-building and innovation through expert engagements, conferences, workshops, training programs, hackathons, and research opportunities. It also encourages the development and validation of innovative solutions to real-world energy challenges, strengthening the connection between research, industry, and society.
