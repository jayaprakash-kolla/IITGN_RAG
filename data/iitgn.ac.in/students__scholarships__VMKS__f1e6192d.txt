## Vegshakti Mahila Kalyan Sangathan (VMKS) Scholarship

 The VMKS scholarship has been instituted in the year 2020 by Vegshakti Mahila Kalyan Sangathan (VMKS). The scholarship amount is Rs. 1.00 Lakh (Rs. 75,000/- supported by VMKS & Rs. 25,000/- by IITGN) per student per year and is awarded to two first-year B.Tech female students of IITGN towards supporting their educational expenses. The scholarship recipients shall continue to receive the benefit of this scholarship during their UG program every year, up to four years, provided they fulfil the academic criteria for annual renewal of the scholarship.

 VMKS is a registered society of lady officers of National High Speed Rail Corporation Limited and wives of officers working in NHSRCL (National High Speed Rail Corporation Limited).
