## Gauri Sugan Agarwal Scholarship

 Gauri Sugan Agarwal Scholarship has been instituted in the year 2021 and is open to all B.Tech female students. The scholarship amount is Rs. 1 lakh per student per year and is awarded to one female B.Tech student every year in order to support internships (international or domestic), special projects and opportunities, financial needs, etc. B.Tech students with a minimum CPI of 6.5 are eligible.

 Ravi Agarwal is a B. Tech alumnus of 2013 batch with a major in Mechanical Engineering. After graduating from IITGN, he worked in management consulting for six years. Then he did his MBA from INSEAD, France in 2019 and is currently working at Restaurant Brands International with their coffee brand Tim Hortons in Singapore. “Gauri Sugan Agarwal Scholarship” is set up by him in the honor of his late grandparents, Mr. Gauri Lal Agarwal and Mrs. Sugni Devi Agarwal. The scholarship of Rs. 1 Lakh per year would support the undergraduate female students for their financial needs, funding internships (international or domestic), or special projects and opportunities, etc.
