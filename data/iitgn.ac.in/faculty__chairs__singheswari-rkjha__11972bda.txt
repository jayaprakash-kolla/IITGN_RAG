## Singheswari and Ram Krishna Jha Chair

 IIT Gandhinagar has setup this faculty chair through a donation by Shri Jagdish Jha and Smt Seema Jha.

Smt Singheswari Jha

Smt Singheswari Jha (1922-2015) was an extraordinary homemaker who nurtured and cared for a large extended family with devotion and grace. She embodied the values of hard work, discipline, and faith, and was deeply respected for her strength and resilience. She stood as a pillar of support to her husband, enabling him to dedicate himself fully to his mission of advancing education and uplifting society. Through her quiet determination and unwavering encouragement, she played an essential role in shaping the lives and values of those around her.

Shri Ram Krishna Jha

Shri Ram Krishna Jha (1914-2002) was a distinguished high school principal and a visionary in the field of education. In 1968, he was honored with the President’s Award for his outstanding contributions and dedicated service. Throughout his career, he worked tirelessly to raise the standards of education not only in his school in Darbhanga but across Bihar, with a special focus on the Mithila region of North Bihar. Beyond academics, he actively engaged with several social and cultural organizations, reflecting his deep commitment to community development. He firmly believed that modern education was the key to uplifting society and improving quality of life. At the same time, he championed the integration of strong Indian social and cultural values within education. For his vision, dedication, and relentless efforts, he earned widespread respect and admiration.

Shri Jagdish Jha and Smt Seema Jha

##### Endowment

Shri Jagdish Jha is a software professional who witnessed and contributed to the industry’s remarkable evolution from its fledgling beginnings to its current prominence. After a career spanning many years, he chose to step away early to pursue his passion for social impact. Today, he is deeply involved with an education-focused NGO in Bangalore, where he works to empower underprivileged children through quality learning opportunities.

 A postgraduate in Physics, Smt Seema Jha has been a strong and dedicated homemaker, supporting her family through the ups and downs of life. For over a decade and a half, she has also been actively involved with Samriddhi Trust, an education-focused NGO in Bangalore that works to empower underprivileged children. As a trustee of the organization, she has contributed to its growth and impact, and today she continues her commitment by teaching Science to high school students at Samriddhi, combining her academic background with her passion for education.

## Arpan Bhattacharyya

 Dr. Arpan Bhattacharyya  has been a faculty member in the Department of Physics at IIT Gandhinagar since October 2019. He received his PhD from the Indian Institute of Science, Bengaluru, followed by a one-year postdoctoral appointment at Fudan University, Shanghai. He then spent two years as a JSPS Postdoctoral Fellow at the Yukawa Institute of Theoretical Physics, Kyoto University, before returning to India to join IITGN. He is an Associate of the Indian Academy of Sciences, Bengaluru for the term 2023–2026.

 Arpan’s research explores the deep principles governing the fundamental forces of nature, spanning phenomena across vastly different energy and length scales—from quantum systems at the smallest scales to gravitational dynamics at the largest. His work is inherently interdisciplinary: he leads a research group that brings together tools from high-energy physics, condensed matter physics, and quantum information theory, allowing insights from one domain to illuminate questions in another.

 He served as the Prime Minister’s Research Fellowship (PMRF) Coordinator at IIT Gandhinagar for three years. Outside the classroom and the blackboard, Arpan is an avid reader with interests well beyond physics and a self-professed foodie. As a teacher, he hopes not only to convey technical mastery but also to inspire curiosity—encouraging students to aspire toward becoming thoughtful and creative theoretical physicists.
