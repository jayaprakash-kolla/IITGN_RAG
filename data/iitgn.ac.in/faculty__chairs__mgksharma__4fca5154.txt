## Smt. Meera and Prof. Girish K Sharma Chair

 IIT Gandhinagar has setup this faculty chair through a donation by Mrs Rashmi Sharma and Mr Manish Sharma.

 Smt. Meera and Prof. Girish K Sharma
Mrs Rashmi Sharma and Mr Manish Sharma, Bangalore-based well-wishers of the Institute, have made a generous gift to create a faculty chair in honour of their parents  Smt Meera and  Prof Girish K Sharma.

 Prof Girish K Sharma is a Visiting Professor of Mechanical Engineering and Professor-in-Charge of Faculty Affairs at IITGN, with vast experience in teaching and research at various prestigious institutes.

 During his illustrious academic career spanning more than 55 years, he has been Head of the Department of Mechanical Engineering at IIT Bombay during Dec.1987-Dec.1990, Dean (SA) at IIT Bombay during Sept 1991-Feb 1995 and served as the Chairman of the Graduate Aptitude Test in Engineering in 1997.

## Dr. Dhiraj D. Bhatia

Dr. Dhiraj D. Bhatia is Associate Professor in Biological Sciences and Engineering at IIT Gandhinagar, where he leads the DNA & Biomaterials (DB) Lab. His research sits at the interface of DNA nanotechnology, biomaterials, and biosensing — engineering DNA and carbon quantum dot–based nanosystems (including programmable DNA hydrogels and nanocarbon composites) for diagnostic, therapeutic, and regenerative medicine applications, spanning in vitro, in vivo, and translational settings. . He is currently the member of INYAS-INSA, Program Director for the ANRF-PAIR Gujarat and also scientific advisor for the startup company Q-Nano-Sol Biotech (QNANOSOL Pvt Ltd).

 He completed his BSc (2005) and MSc (2007) in Chemistry from the University of Pune, followed by a PhD (2013) from the Tata Institute of Fundamental Research (NCBS-TIFR), Bangalore, in structural DNA nanotechnology, for which he received outstanding thesis awards. He then pursued postdoctoral research at the Institut Curie, Paris — first as a Curie Postdoctoral Fellow (2013–14) and then as a Human Frontier Science Program (HFSP) Fellow (2014–17) in Ludger Johannes's group, where he studied the cellular and biological applications of DNA nanodevices.

 He joined IIT Gandhinagar as Assistant Professor in July 2018, holding the DST-SERB Ramanujan Fellowship (2018–2023), and was promoted to Associate Professor in September 2023. He is currently a member of INYAS-INSA (Indian National Young Academy of Science, 2023–2028).

 His doctoral and postdoctoral work with Prof. Yamuna Krishnan produced landmark papers on DNA-based nanodevices for cellular imaging, including studies in Nature Nanotechnology (2016) and Nature Communications (2011) on DNA icosahedra for tracking endocytic pathways and functional bioimaging in living systems.

Research Areas: DNA Nanotechnology · Tissue Engineering · Hydrogels & Biomaterials · Bioimaging · Drug Delivery · Biosensing
