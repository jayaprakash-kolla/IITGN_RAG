## Free Basic Messing Assistance

 This assistance is aimed at extending financial support to SC/ST students from economically weak backgrounds. The Institute facilitates free basic messing and a nominal pocket allowance for SC/ST category students of B.Tech., M.Sc. and M.A. programs whose annual parental income is not more than ₹ 4,50,000. This scholarship expires at the end of the academic year and is renewed on a yearly basis provided the student fulfills all the terms and conditions for the renewal of the scholarship.

##### Instituted By

 IIT Gandhinagar

##### Amount of Scholarship per Year

 Free Basic Messing and Rs. 250/- as a pocket allowance for 10 months
