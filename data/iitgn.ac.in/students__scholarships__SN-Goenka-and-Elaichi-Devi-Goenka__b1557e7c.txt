## SN Goenka and Elaichi Devi Goenka Scholarship

 SN Goenka and Elaichi Devi Goenka Scholarship has been instituted in the year 2025 by Mr Naman Bansal, and are open to all UG (BTech) students. The scholarship amount of each scholarship is Rs. 1 lakh per student per year and is awarded to one UG (BTech) student every year in order to support internships (international or domestic), special projects and opportunities, financial needs, etc. UG (BTech) students with a minimum CPI of 6.5 are eligible.

 Mr Naman Bansal graduated from IIT Gandhinagar in 2016 with a BTech in Electrical Engineering. He recently completed his MS and PhD in Computer Science and Software Engineering at Auburn University. He has joined Google in Kirkland, USA, as a Software Engineer. SN Goenka and Elaichi Devi Goenka Scholarship has been established by him in honor of his teachers, Shri Satya Narayan Goenka and Smt. Elaichi Devi Goenka, whose teachings and values have profoundly inspired Naman, providing him with a renewed sense of purpose and direction in life. The scholarship of Rs. 1 Lakh per year would support the undergraduate students for their financial needs, funding internships (international or domestic), or special projects and opportunities, etc.
