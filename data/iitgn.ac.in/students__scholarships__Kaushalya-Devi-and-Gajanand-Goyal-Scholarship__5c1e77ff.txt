## Kaushalya Devi and Gajanand Goyal Scholarship

 The Kaushalya Devi and Gajanand Goyal Scholarship has been established by Dr. Yogesh Goyal to support B.Tech students at IIT Gandhinagar. The scholarship amount is Rs. 1 lakh per student per year and is awarded to one eligible B.Tech student every year. B.Tech students with a minimum CPI of 7.5 are eligible.

 Dr. Yogesh Goyal is a B.Tech alumnus from the pioneer batch of IIT Gandhinagar. After graduating from IITGN, he completed his PhD at Princeton University, USA, and his postdoctoral training at the University of Pennsylvania, USA. He is currently an Assistant Professor at Northwestern University, USA, and a Biohub Investigator. Yogesh credits IITGN with an irreplaceable role in shaping both his personal and professional trajectory. He met some of his closest friends there and has remained a patron of the institute, regularly hosting IITGN gatherings at his home in Chicago.

 Yogesh is joined in this endeavour by his wife, Patty Lan, who completed her undergraduate studies at Yale University, USA, and her Master's at the University of Pennsylvania, USA, and is currently pursuing her PhD at the University of Wisconsin-Madison, USA. Patty was herself a first-generation, low-income student and has built a deep bond with and appreciation for her in-laws.

 Smt. Kaushalya Devi and Shri Gajanand Goyal raised their four children in rural Jammu and Kashmir. Neither had the opportunity to pursue much formal education themselves, as financial hardship and family circumstances placed it out of reach. They resolved that their children would not face the same limits, and they sacrificed a great deal in that belief, ensuring that every one of the four went on to college and to higher studies.

 Dr. Goyal has established this scholarship in gratitude for their conviction and sacrifices, and in the hope that it will extend the same opportunity to young students embarking on their career journeys at IIT Gandhinagar.
