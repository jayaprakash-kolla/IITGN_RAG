## Ajodyabai Gulabchandji Randad Scholarship

 Ajodyabai Gulabchandji Randad Scholarship was instituted in the year 2019 and is open to all B.Tech students. The scholarship amount is Rs. 1 lakh per student per year and is awarded to one B.Tech student every year in order to support internships (international or domestic), special projects and opportunities, financial needs, etc. B.Tech students with a minimum CPI of 6.5 are eligible.

 Akshay Randad is a B.Tech alumnus of Class of 2015 with a major in Mechanical Engineering and a minor in Management. He has also done Masters in Mechanical Engineering from the University of Washington, Seattle. He works as a Transducer Engineer at Applied Physics Lab at University of Washington Seattle & will be joining as an R&D engineer with a medical device company Sonomotion Inc. in the Bay Area (US). He has instituted this scholarship in the memory of his late grandmother.
