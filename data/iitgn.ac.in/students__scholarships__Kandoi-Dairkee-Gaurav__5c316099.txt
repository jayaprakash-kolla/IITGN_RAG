## Kandoi Dairkee Gaurav KDG Scholarship

 The Kandoi-Dairkee-Gaurav Scholarship was instituted by three alumni of the Pioneer Batch (B.Tech graduates of 2012) at IIT Gandhinagar : Mr. Abhishek Kandoi, Mr. Maisum Ali Dairkee and Mr. Anchit Gaurav. This Merit-cum-Means Scholarship is awarded to a second or third year B.Tech student who is actively involved in non-academic activities of the Institute and showing trajectory towards all rounded growth. The gross annual parental income of the student from all sources should not exceed Rs. 8 lakhs per annum. Student should secure a minimum CPI of 6.0. The scholarship amount is Rs. 50,000 per academic year.
