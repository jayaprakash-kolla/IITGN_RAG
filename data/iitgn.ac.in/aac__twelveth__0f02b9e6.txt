# Twelveth Academic Advisory Council

##### Academic Advisory Council Meetings

##### The Academic Advisory Council comprises of eminent leaders from academia and other allied domains, and meets every year to discuss the academic priorities of IIT Gandhinagar. The Academic Advisory Council provides strategic external inputs on issues of key importance as the Institute grows in academics and other spheres. On January 10, 2025, the twelveth Council meeting will discuss The Rise of Women Leaders in Higher Education: Breaking Barriers and Shaping Futures; Bridging the Gap: Advancing Translational Research in Academia; AI Intervention in Teaching and Research: Opportunities, Challenges, and the Way Forward; and The New Frontier of Faculty Hiring: Balancing Excellence, Diversity, and Institutional Goals.
