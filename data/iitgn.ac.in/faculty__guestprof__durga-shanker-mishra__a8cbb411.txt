## Durga Shanker Mishra

Guest Professor, Humanities and Social Sciences

- PG Diploma: Governance, Democratization and Public Policy, ISS, The Hague, Netherlands, 2007
- MBA: International Business, University of Western Sydney, Australia, 2000
- PG Diploma: Human Resource Management, AIMA, New Delhi, 1994
- B.Tech.: Electrical Engineering, IIT Kanpur, India, 1983

- Published extensively on urban development, governance, and smart cities in journals like Yojana, e-Governance Magazine, and policy platforms
- Contributed to books including “Viksit Bharat @ 2047: Governance Transformed”
- Articles featured in national newspapers such as The Indian Express and Millennium Post

- Guest Professor, Indian Institute of Technology Gandhinagar (2026 – Present)
- Chief Secretary, Government of Uttar Pradesh (Dec 2021 – June 2024)
- Secretary, Ministry of Housing & Urban Affairs, Government of India (June 2017 – Dec 2021)
- Additional Secretary, Ministry of Urban Development, Government of India (July 2014 – June 2017)
- Principal Secretary, Urban Development Department, Government of Uttar Pradesh (2011 – 2012)
- Vice-Chairman, Kanpur Development Authority (1998 – 1999)
- IAS Officer, Uttar Pradesh Cadre (1984 Batch)
