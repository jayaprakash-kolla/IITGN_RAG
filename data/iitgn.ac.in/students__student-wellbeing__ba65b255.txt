## Student Social Network

 The IIT Gandhinagar Student Social Network is driven by a dedicated team of faculty members who offer assistance to students with a broad range of concerns. We look forward to working with you directly, and we will either help connect you with the right group of advisors or brainstorm our way to a solution. Please don't hesitate to reach out! This is for every single student of IIT Gandhinagar. You are doing well and you can still come to us for any advice.
We are here for you!
