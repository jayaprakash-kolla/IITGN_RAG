# Post Doc Fellowship

- Academics
- Programs
- Post Doc Fellowship

 Indian Institute of Technology Gandhinagar seeks to appoint post-doctoral fellows (PDFs) in different disciplines of engineering, sciences and humanities. The terms and conditions of PDFs at IITGn are listed below. Interested candidates may approach the faculty members in their area of research directly.

### Terms and Conditions

Minimum Qualifications:
 Recent PhD or waiting for Defence after submission of thesis (if admissible). Candidates who have completed their earlier post-doctoral fellowship assignment are also eligible.

Fellowship Amount:
 Fellowship amount for a candidate will be fixed depending on qualifications and experience. This range may be revised upwards periodically, typically once a year, to keep up with inflation.

Medical Benefits:
 Medical benefits for self, spouse and children may be provided at par with those provided to the faculty of IITGN.

Leave:
 PDF will be provided with 2.5 days leave per month; this may be accumulated up to 90 days.

Reimbursement of travel expenses and accommodation at the time of interview:
 Candidates called for interview would be provided accommodation and would be eligible for reimbursement of train fare by II A.C.

Duration of the appointment:
 One year and may be extended on mutual requirement and availability. Duration cannot exceed three years.

Discipline of Research:
 In all areas of engineering, sciences and humanities subject to the interest of the concerned faculty.
