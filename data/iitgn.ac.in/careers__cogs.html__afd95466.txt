Centre for Cognitive and Brain Sciences

IITGN became the first IIT to offer a Master's degree program in Cognitive and Brain Sciences in 2013. The centre’s research activities currently are in the domain of:

Experience, Agency and Privacy
 Learning and Decision Making
 Consumer Behavior
 Attention, Emotion and Embodied Cognition
 Affective Computing, Artifical Intelligence and Adaptive Rehabilitation Systems
 Sensorimotor control and learning, brain lateralization and movement disorders
 The research facilities, which are continuously being upgraded, include:

State-of-the-art motion capture systems
 Eye trackers
 High density EEG
 EMG recording equipment
 Virtual reality systems
 Behavioral cubicles
 TMS (coming soon!)
 In order to meet current and future research and teaching requirements, the Centre for Cognitive and Brain Sciences seeks outstanding researchers from all areas of Cognitive and Brain Sciences and allied fields. Interested candidates are encouraged to apply through appropriate discipline at IITGN.
