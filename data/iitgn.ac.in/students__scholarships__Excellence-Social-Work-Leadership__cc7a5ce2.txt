## Scholarship for Excellence in Social Work & Leadership

 Two scholarships have been instituted to encourage students who demonstrate outstanding leadership skills and social outreach initiatives. Students in second, third and fourth year of their undergraduate program can apply and each recipient is awarded ₹ 2,000 per month for ten months. However, if the awardee is also recipient of any scholarship of equal or higher value, then he/she is eligible only for a one-time receipt of ₹ 5000.
