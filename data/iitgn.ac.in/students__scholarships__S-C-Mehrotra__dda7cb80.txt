## S.C. Mehrotra Scholarship

 The S.C. Mehrotra’s Scholarship was instituted in the year 2010 and is open to all the B.Tech students. The scholarship amount is Rs. 1 lakh per student per year and is awarded to one B.Tech student every year in order to support internships (international or domestic), special projects and opportunities, financial needs, etc. B.Tech students with a minimum CPI of 6.5 are eligible. Preference will be given to students of Civil Engineering discipline.

 Shri S.C. Mehrotra, an IIT Delhi alumnus has professional experience of more than four decades in the field of structural designs for commercial and residential multistory buildings including industrial structures and public infrastructures. He was President of Consulting Engineers Association of India during the period December 2003 to December 2005.
