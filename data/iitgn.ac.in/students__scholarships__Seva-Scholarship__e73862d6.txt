## Seva Scholarship

 Seva Scholarship has been instituted in the year 2022 and is open to all B.Tech students. The scholarship amount is Rs. 1 lakh per student per year and is awarded to one B.Tech student every year in order to support internships (international or domestic), special projects and opportunities, financial needs, etc. B.Tech students with a minimum CPI of 6.5 are eligible.

 Yash Kotak is a B. Tech alumnus of 2014 batch with major in Electrical Engineering and Minors in Management and Computer Science. He is currently working as a Product Manager at Google India, Bangalore. He was the recipient of the Director’s Gold Medal for Outstanding Overall Performance and the Medal for Outstanding Innovation. Seva Scholarship is instituted by him in honor of his parents, Nina Kotak and Prashant Kotak.
