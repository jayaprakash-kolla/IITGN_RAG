## Sudhir Kumar Gupta & Sunita Rani Gupta Scholarship

 The Sudhir Kumar Gupta and Sunita Rani Gupta Scholarship has been established by Anshul Gupta (BTech/ME/2015) in honor of his parents. The scholarship amount is Rs. 1 lakh per student per year and is awarded to one student across B.Tech, M.Sc or M.A students of all years. The scholarship can support opportunities such as national or international internships, special projects, sports, conferences, or other academic pursuits, while also providing financial assistance. Preference will be given to students who have lost either or both parents.

 Anshul Gupta (BTech/ME/2015) earned his MBA from Indian School of Business (ISB) Hyderabad in 2020 and is currently working as a Group Product Manager with Physics Wallah. Prior to this he worked with DeHaat and Flipkart. The Sudhir Kumar Gupta and Sunita Rani Gupta Scholarship has been established to honor all those parents (including his) who selflessly sacrifice their own needs and aspirations to prioritize and support their children's education. Anshul and his sister really feel grateful to have parents like Late. Sudhir Kumar Gupta and Sunita Rani Gupta who showed unwavering commitment towards educating both their children not only academically but also with moral values. It is because of their commitment, their children have become what they are today. Their boundless compassion, selfless desire to serve others, and unwavering dedication to the betterment of society remain an enduring source of love and inspiration.
