## N Rama Rao Chair

 IIT Gandhinagar has setup a faculty chair in honour of Shri N Rama Rao through a donation by his son Shri N R Narayana Murthy.

### Shri N Rama Rao

Shri N Rama Rao
 Infosys founder Shri N R Narayana Murthy has established the N Rama Rao Chair in memory of his father. The chair is open to faculty members in the disciplines of Computer Science and Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Mathematics and Physics at IIT Gandhinagar.

Shri N Rama Rao (1913-1974) spent most of his career as a high school teacher of physics, mathematics and English in Karnataka. Throughout his career, he demonstrated excellence in everything he did. He inspired his students and children to aim high in life.

Shri N R Narayana Murthy

##### Endowment

Shri N R Narayana Murthy is founder of Infosys and served as its first CEO from 1981-2002, as chairman of the Infosys board from 2002-2006 and as chief mentor until his retirement in 2011. He is widely credited for being instrumental in shaping the Indian IT sector and the global outsourcing model.

## Atul Abhay Dixit

Prof Atul Abhay Dixit has been on the faculty of IIT Gandhinagar since August 2015 in the Department of Mathematics. He received his PhD from University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign and then had a 3-year postdoctoral stint at Tulane University before returning to India to join IITGN. He is a recipient of Swarnajayanti Fellowship (2022-2027) and Gábor szegö prize in 2021 awarded by the Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics, USA. He is on the editorial boards of several Mathematics journals.

Atul's research interests are in analytic number theory, special functions, theory of partitions and q-series. He likes to work in the areas of mathematics influenced by Srinivasa Ramanujan.

 Atul was the Discipline coordinator from 2018-2020 and is currently the Faculty in-charge Student events (Technical, Cultural etc.). Apart from Mathematics, he loves singing and composing music.
