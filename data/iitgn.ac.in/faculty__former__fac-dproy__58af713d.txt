## Debi Prasad Roy

Visiting Professor, Mechanical Engineering

- BSc (Hons): Utkal University, India; 1960
- Dipl-Ing.: T.H. Aachen, Germany; 1970
- Dr.–Ing. : T.H. Aachen, Germany; 1976

- S. S Taliyan, D.P. Roy, 1995, “An Experimental study of the Discharge coefficient of Pipe perforations, ” Experiments in Fluids, Vol. 19.
- S. S Taliyan, D.P. Roy, 1994, “Dynamics of shut-off rod drop in a PHWR,” Nuclear Engineering and Design Vol. 147.
- R. Chaturvedi, D.P. Roy, 1990, “Non- Reflecting Boundary conditions And their use in estimating the Effects of coolant channel failure in a PHWR,” Nuclear Engineering and Design, Vol. 188.
