## Kausalya and Tukaram Vaichal Scholarship

 The Kausalya and Tukaram Vaichal Scholarship has been established by Saurabh S. Vaichal (BTech/ME/2016) in honor of his grandparents. The scholarship amount is Rs. 1 lakh per student per year and is awarded to one 2nd or 3rd year B.Tech student every year. Students with a minimum CPI of 7 are eligible. This need-based scholarship provides financial support to deserving and underprivileged students and helps them achieve their educational aspirations.

 Saurabh S. Vaichal (BTech/ME/2016) earned his Master's degree from Texas A&M University, College Station, in 2020 and is currently working as a Machine Learning professional in the US. The Kausalya and Tukaram Vaichal Scholarship was established to honor the enduring and impactful memory of his grandparents. Throughout their lives, Kausalya and Tukaram served as a continual source of inspiration for Saurabh and those around them. Their selfless love, desire to help others, and devotion and dedication to the betterment of society continue to be lovingly cherished by their family and community. This need-based fund honors their beautiful legacy by providing financial support to deserving students and helping them pursue their educational dreams.
