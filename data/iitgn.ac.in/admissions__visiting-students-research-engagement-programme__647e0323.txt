# Visiting Students Research Engagement Programme

 Click Here to Apply

 Applications are invited for the newly launched Visiting Students Research Engagement Programme of IIT Gandhinagar for Semester II of AY 2026-27. The programme enables select final year students from IITs/IISERs/IISc and NITs to pursue semester-long internship at IIT Gandhinagar in the form of a research project, working along with IIT Gandhinagar faculty.

#### Salient Features

- ❑ Selected students can spend a semester at IIT Gandhinagar working on a research project with IIT Gandhinagar faculty members.
- ❑ The students will be considered visiting students at IIT Gandhinagar and must register for Research Engagement credits at IIT Gandhinagar.
- ❑ Research Engagement credits can be earned in 4-credit modules (IN 497 - Research Engagement), with a student permitted to earn a maximum of 4 credits in one quad and a maximum of 16 credits of IN 497 during their stay at IIT Gandhinagar [A semester is divided into 4 quads].
- ❑ IN 497 will be graded using the usual letter grades by the host department at IIT Gandhinagar at the end of each quad.

Eligibility Criteria

#### Eligibility Criteria

- Final-year students in the BTech (4-year programme) / BS (4-year programme) / Integrated Master of Science (5-year programmes) from IITs/IISc
- OR
- Final-year students in the BTech (4-year programme) / BS (4-year programme) / BS-MS (5-year programme) from NITs/IISERs
- AND
- Having more than 8.0 CPI/CGPA at the end of their pre-final year are eligible to apply.
Financial Support

#### Financial Support

Students will be provided a stipend of Rs. 5000/- for every quad for which they have secured a grade of B or above in IN 497.

Fees

#### Fee

- Students selected for the Visiting Students Research Engagement Programme need NOT pay any tuition fees at IIT Gandhinagar for the registration in IN 497 (Research Engagement) during their stay.
- Hostel and mess fees are applicable and need to be paid by the student.
