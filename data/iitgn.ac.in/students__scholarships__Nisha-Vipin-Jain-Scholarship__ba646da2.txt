## Nisha and Vipin Jain Scholarship

 Nisha and Vipin Jain Scholarship has been instituted in the year 2021 and is open to all B.Tech students. The scholarship amount is Rs. 1 lakh per student per year and is awarded to one B.Tech student every year in order to support internships (international or domestic), special projects and opportunities, financial needs, etc. B.Tech students with a minimum CPI of 6.5 are eligible.

 Dr. Shruti Jain is a B.Tech alumnus of 2013 batch with a major in Chemical Engineering, and did her PhD from the University of Texas at Austin, USA. She is currently working as a RET Design Engineer at Micron Technology, USA. She was the recipient of the President's Gold Medal, Institute Gold Medal, and was also awarded for best performance in core courses in Humanities and Social Sciences. She was also the recipient of the scholarship for excellence in Academics; and scholarship for excellence in sports for exceptional performance in badminton. Nisha and Vipin Jain Scholarship is instituted by her in honor of her parents, Mrs. Nisha Jain and Mr. Vipin Kumar Jain. The scholarship of Rs. 1 Lakh per year would support the undergraduate students for their financial needs, funding internships (international or domestic), or special projects and opportunities, etc.
