# Faculty Recruitment

 Advertisement  Priority Areas  Apply

## Who Should Apply?

 IITGN is looking for applicants with a passion for teaching and demonstrated excellence in research. Successful candidates will be expected to conduct independent research in their fields of expertise, teach undergraduate and postgraduate courses, and contribute to Institute service. Candidates whose work is interdisciplinary are especially encouraged to apply.

 IITGN is committed to meeting national goals of diversity and inclusion. In the past, we have also held special drives to improve the diversity of our faculty body.
