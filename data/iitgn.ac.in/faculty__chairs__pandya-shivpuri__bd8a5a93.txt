## Pandya-Shivpuri Chair in Population Dynamics

IIT Gandhinagar has setup this faculty chair through a donation by Dr. Darshan Pandya.

#### ABOUT THE DONORS

Dr. Darshan Pandya, an IT industry leader and philanthropist, along with his wife, Dr. Chandra Shivpuri, a neonatal medicine specialist, has provided generous support to establish the "Pandya-Shivpuri Chair in Population Dynamics" at IIT Gandhinagar. This chair aims to promote excellence and leadership in interdisciplinary research in the ground-breaking and specialised field of population dynamics through various collaborative and scholarly activities.

Dr. Darshan Pandya

##### Endowment

Dr. Darshan V Pandya completed his BSc (Eng) (Hons) and PhD in Chemical Engineering and later an MSc in Operational Research and Management Studies from Imperial College, London. An avid traveller, he has visited forty-five countries and lived in five. After working in the British Petroleum in London, he taught Operational Research and Statistics at the National University of Singapore for three years before switching to the IT industry and working in the USA and Canada until his retirement in 2015.

## Udit Bhatia

 Prof Udit Bhatia is an Associate Professor with joint appointments in the Department of Civil Engineering and the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at the Indian Institute of Technology Gandhinagar, where he has been a faculty member since 2019, and currently holds the Pandya–Shivpuri Faculty Chair in Population Dynamics. He received his PhD from Northeastern University, Boston. His research focuses on population-level dynamics in coupled human–natural–infrastructure systems, with particular emphasis on nutrient surplus, sustainable agricultural restructuring, and the restoration dynamics in ecological interaction networks. In parallel, his research on urban flooding and climate extremes develops physics-guided AI and data-driven flood models to study how hazard dynamics interact with population exposure, vulnerability, and adaptive capacity in rapidly urbanizing regions. Together, his work integrates population dynamics, network science, hydrology, and artificial intelligence to support evidence-based planning for sustainability and climate resilience.
