The Center for Creative Learning at the Indian Institute of Technology - Gandhinagar (CCL IITGN) Lab is dedicated to improving the quality of STEM education for students in Gujarat Government schools. The lab's main objective is to make the learning experience more captivating and interactive. This initiative, as reported by the Indian Express, aims to actively involve students in the learning process and promote a deeper understanding of science, technology, engineering, and mathematics subjects.

This article was published in the Indian Express newspaper on November 18, 2023, appearing on page no 9.
