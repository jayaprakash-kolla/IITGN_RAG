# Imroz Khan

Imroz Khan is an artistic soul with a scientific mind. He is a versatile learner who can integrate concepts from different fields and apply them to projects. He pursued his Bachelor’s and Master’s Degrees from St. Xavier's College and School Of Science. There he was fortunate enough to do many projects, one of which got 3rd Prize in the National Conference. During his Master's, he joined a science museum, where he worked as a science communicator, got the opportunity to use his creativity to lay out the content and development of numerous activities for the museum, and facilitated workshops and coordinated events. Currently, he is associated with CCL, working on research and documentation for STEM toys and activities.
