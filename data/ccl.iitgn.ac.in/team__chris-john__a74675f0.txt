# Chris John

Chris John is a passionate STEM educator dedicated to making math and science meaningful, visual, and deeply engaging for children. With experience teaching at both the school and university levels, he brings a rich blend of academic insight and classroom creativity.
His work explores the beauty of mathematics through hands-on, inquiry-based learning. He is now part of CCL, where he wants to continue designing experiences that spark curiosity and invite young minds to see math as an art form woven into the world around them.

He did his BS-MS degree from IISER Pune. He then worked with Uravu, Wayanad, and IISc on a bamboo-based math-toy design project. He worked as a lecturer at Azim Premji University for 2 years, and then he worked at The Sahyadri School, KFI, Pune for 4 years.
