# Curiosity Program

Bringing back the gleam in the eyes of students & teachers

## About the program

A large scale online program running in 1,685 KGBVs of 9 Indian states with about 1,50,000+ girls, designed to nurture the inherent curiosity of the students using hands-on activities and exciting toys and models and achieve three objectives:

Engagement

Observations

Reasoning

The program is conducted live, twice a week - every Tuesday and Friday at 5:30 p.m. The language of instruction is Hindi. Sessions are available on YouTube and can be watched using the following link.
