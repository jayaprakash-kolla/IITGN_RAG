# Abhijit Das

Abhijit Das is an alumnus of Utkal University ( Chem. Hons), Odisha. He believes in the degree of BEE (Bachelor of Energy & Enthusiasm). Before joining CCL, he worked as a Master Trainer Lead with Agastya International Foundation and led Agastya’s Academic work in 11 states across North, East and North East India. He has over 8 years of experience in the field of education. He has worked as a teacher, Content Developer, TQA Assistance & Teacher educator. His areas of interest are creating curiosity, innovation, science education, developing content, exploring pedagogy, and quality education.
