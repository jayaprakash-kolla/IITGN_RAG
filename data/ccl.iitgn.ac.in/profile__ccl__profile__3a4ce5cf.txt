### About

We came into being in order to nurture a scientific temperament among adults and children alike. Our journey began from an empty 400 sq feet room, and today, 5 years later, we have 3 rooms full of 1000+ unique STEM models and activities, created by us. Having conducted 100+ STEM and hands-on learning workshops for over 10,000 teachers & engineering college professors and 50,000 students from all across the country - our mission is to bring back the gleam in their eyes!
