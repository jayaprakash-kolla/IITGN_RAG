# Rakesh Pachaya

Rakesh Pachaya completed his Diploma in Computer Application and ITI from Alirajpur, Madhya Pradesh, showcasing his dedication to the field of technology. However, his true passion lies in the realm of design and prototyping. This passion drove him to work tirelessly on the creation of Chandrashekhar Vigyan Park. The opportunity to work on such a project fuelled Rakesh's enthusiasm even further, leading him to pursue a career at CCL—a place where he feels his dreams have been realized. At CCL, Rakesh finds immense satisfaction in his role as he gets to indulge in creating STEM-based models, designing puzzles, and conducting workshops on the art of making.
