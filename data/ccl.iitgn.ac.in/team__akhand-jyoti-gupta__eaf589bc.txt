# Gupta Akhandjyoti Ashok Kumar

Akhandjyoti Gupta pursued a bachelor's degree in Mechanical Engineering from RK University, Rajkot and interned at Ajanta Industries as a Program Management Intern. Since school, he has been interested and a frequent visitor of the CCL lab, and he joined the CCL lab after completing his internship. He is intensely interested in ideating and creating innovative products. His projects have received awards at various national and international platforms and his paper on a Fourier Machine he made has been accepted into the BRIDGES conference in 2023. He experienced summer schools hosted by Entrepreneurship Hub - Germany, where he created problem-solving solutions, and led an international team. His innovation got featured in Braunschweig Zeitung, a German news outlet. At CCL, he evolves creative products that make education joyful.
