# Ghutukade Vijay Dinkar

Animator & Video Editor

Ghutukade Vijay Dinkar is a talented animator and video editor at the Centre for Creative Learning (CCL), IIT Gandhinagar. He specializes in creating captivating animations and editing high-quality videos that simplify complex science and math concepts, making them accessible and engaging for learners worldwide. His creative expertise is pivotal to CCL’s mission of fostering innovative education.
