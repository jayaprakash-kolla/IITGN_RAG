# Tirthanand Chute

Tirthanand Chute holds a postgraduate degree in Mathematics and a degree in Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics. He is passionate about activity-based learning and began his journey by teaching secondary and higher secondary students through his own coaching classes.

He has served as Incharge of the Mathematics Laboratory at Bajaj Science Education Center, Wardha, and is currently working with CCL , where he contributes to developing engaging, hands-on Mathematics content and learning experiences.
