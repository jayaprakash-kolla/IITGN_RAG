# Toys and Activities

We design and create puzzles, simple toys, math related art, science experiments and demonstrations for children of all ages. Each member of our team has a creative spark that pushes them to constantly innovate on new toys. Some of our toy designs have won awards in conferences and been registered with the Patent Office, Govt. of India.
