# Shradhda Jain

Shradhda completed her Postgraduate degree in Botany from Dayalbagh educational Institute, Agra, and her passion for working with people led her to pursue a second master’s degree in Sociology. She also secured 53rd rank in GATE 2021 (Sociology). She has worked with NEEV, a social outreach program of IIT Gandhinagr for 7 years where she worked closely with grass root communities. She has been working in different research projects for the past 3 years which provided insight in both qualitative and quantitative research in Social Sciences. Currently, she is working with CCL to understand the effects of the Curiosity Program on middle school students.
