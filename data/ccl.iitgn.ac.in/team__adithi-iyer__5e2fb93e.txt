# Adithi Iyer

Adithi Iyer finished her master's in mathematics from Nagpur. While trying to understand mathematical ideas she enjoyed sharing its beauty with students. After completing her master's degree, she taught mathematics to grade 9 and 10 students at PSBB school in Chennai. In Chennai, she was a volunteer at the Student Sea Turtle Conservation Network which worked on the Conservation of the Olive Riddley Turtle. While teaching she came in contact with CCL through the 3030 STEM program and volunteered to make hand-drawn illustrations converting the online sessions into a comic book. She has been working in CCL for the last two years trying to develop math content. Apart from working with students and teachers in workshops, she is involved in making videos and illustrations for the videos. She is currently also pursuing her PhD at IIT Gandhinagar in the field of Education Design.
