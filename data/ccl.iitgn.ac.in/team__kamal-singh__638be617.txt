# Kamal Singh

Kamal holds a BSc in Science (Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics) from DSB Campus KU Nainital and a B.Ed from Bajaj College of Management and Technology. His educational journey started with an internship at the Azim Premji Foundation, focusing on elementary education. He then spent three years at Agastya International Foundation, where he spearheaded programs like Mobile Science Labs and the Lab-on-Bike Innovation Hub to inspire underprivileged students.As an Instructor Master Trainer in the Academic and Content Research team, Kamal led induction training, refresher training, and workshops for teacher educators for four years, significantly enhancing teaching strategies and classroom effectiveness. Currently, at the Center for Creative Learning at IIT Gandhinagar, he specializes in STEM education, physics content research, workshop facilitation, educational documentation, and assessment design. His innovative approach to education blends hands-on learning with creative exploration, making science and math accessible, engaging, and fun for all learners.Kamal is deeply passionate about fostering creativity and discovery through interactive models and experiments, creating a dynamic and joyful learning experience for students and educators alike.
