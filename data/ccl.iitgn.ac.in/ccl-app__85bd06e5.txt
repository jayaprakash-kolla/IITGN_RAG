# The CCL App

Designed to extend the Center for Creative Learning’s workshop experience into classrooms, the app provides easy access to essential resources, enabling teachers to bring creative STEM education to life.

Features include detailed resource libraries, progress tracking, photo-sharing for showcasing classroom engagement, and timely CCL updates via an announcement channel, all crafted to inspire and empower educators.
