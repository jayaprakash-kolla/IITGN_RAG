# Achievements

CCL IIT Gandhinagar has received widespread recognition for its work in STEM education for children and adults. CCL has been involved in the following on a national level:

Part of the National Committee on Textbook and Syllabus development

Member of the National Focus Group on Mathematics and Computational Thinking under NEP 2020

Member of Committee on Toy-based Pedagogy by MoE

Member of NCERT committee drafting the syllabus and textbooks of Foundational Numeracy for grades K-3

MoU with Science City Ahmedabad and Science City Bhopal, to popularise STEM among citizens

Jury member of National Award to Teachers 2022

Mous with MPCST, Delhi SCERT & SSA, UP SSA & SCERT and Gujarat SCERTs for Teacher Training and Capacity building, delivering over 10,000 STEM kits to schools in these regions.

Our most prized feedback has been from participants in the form of long letters and poetry as well!
