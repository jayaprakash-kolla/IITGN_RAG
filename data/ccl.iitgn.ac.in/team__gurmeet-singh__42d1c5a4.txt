# Gurmeet Singh

Sr. Program Manager

Gurmeet is a dynamic Product Designer and STEM Educator with over 10 years of experience shaping the next generation of innovators, from scientists and engineers to designers and mathematicians. He specializes in blending creativity with practical learning, using project-based approaches to bring real-world applications into the classroom. An expert in both curriculum and product development, Gurmeet has successfully designed and implemented nationwide STEM programs that have significantly enhanced student engagement and performance.
With a commitment to fostering a growth mindset, he equips students with the critical and creative problem-solving skills necessary to tackle tomorrow’s technological challenges. Gurmeet has collaborated with leading EdTech players, EPCH India, and IIE Guwahati, and he served as a 'Mentor of Change' under the NITI Aayog’s Atal Innovation Mission, helping to drive innovation and learning at scale.
