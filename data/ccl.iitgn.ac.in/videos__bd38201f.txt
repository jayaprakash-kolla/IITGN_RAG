## Watch, Make and Play with CCL!

We make videos for children in India, in Hindi and English, to observe scientific and mathematical principles in the simple things and common materials available around them. Our Curiosity series is a set of short videos that will encourage children and teachers to explore concepts in depth, all by using some simple materials. Curious to know more?
