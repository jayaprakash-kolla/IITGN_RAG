# KV Teachers In-Service Course

## Project type

Teacher Workshop

## Date

21-12-2022 to 2-1-2023

## Location

IIT Gandhingagar

Teachers the Kendriya Vidyalaya Sangathan from all across India attended two spells of In-Service training, in the summer and winter sessions of 2022. 50 teachers who were Post Graduates in their fields of teaching, arrived in Gandhingar for two weeks in the summer and two weeks in the winter. All the teachers were either Maths or Science teachers. They attended common sessions by the CCL team and professors at IIT GN and were also split up into Maths and Science specialty sessions. They were encouraged to go deeper into their subject and introduced to many new pedagogical tools that they could use in their classrooms.
