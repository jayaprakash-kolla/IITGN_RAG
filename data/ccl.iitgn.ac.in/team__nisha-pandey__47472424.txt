# Nisha Pandey

Nisha Pandey is a technology management professional with a diverse educational and professional experience. She holds a B.Pharma and an M.Tech degree in Biotechnology, along with LLB and PG Diploma in IPR. With over a decade of experience in Intellectual Property management and innovation protection, she has worked at esteemed scientific and technological organizations such as IIT Delhi, CSIR HQ, and the National Innovation Foundation (NIF). Nisha is also a registered Patent Agent. She currently contributes to creating engaging STEM content, focusing on science primerily chemistry, biology, and related subjects, at the Center for Creative Learning, IIT Gandhinagar.
