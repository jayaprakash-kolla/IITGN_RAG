# Nihar Pandya

Nihar Pandya did his masters in Animal Studies from Australia. After his master's degree, his inherent interest in the field of education led him to work on major projects such as Science City, Science Express, and now in Center for Creative Learning [CCL], IIT Gandhinagar - Gujarat. He has been part of the CCL Team for almost 3 yrs. During almost 15yrs in the education field - he has been STEM Educator, Content developer, Teacher Trainer and Academic Consultant. His mission has always been - Making Education Interesting and relevant for students.
