# Shreesha Bhat

Shreesha Bhat holds a PhD in chemistry from University of Lincoln, UK. Having been involved in many science outreach activities in the UK, he found the process of learning and doing science with kids very fulfilling. This paved his return to India with a vision to contribute to the Indian education system. He started with leading weekend science camps in Ahmedabad in low income schools. He was also selected as one of the 55 RSC teacher developers as part of the Yusuf Hamied Inspirational Science Program to train science teachers in active learning techniques. He currently heads the chemistry content team in CCL and is involved in creating engaging and fun chemistry activities, meanwhile also focusing on inquiry based learning.
