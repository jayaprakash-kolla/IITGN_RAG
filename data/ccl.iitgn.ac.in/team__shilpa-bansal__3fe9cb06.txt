# Shilpa Bansal

Shilpa Bansal completed her PhD in Statistics from IIT Kharagpur. Her desire for getting involved in practical teaching methods instead of traditional teaching led her to join the Center for Creative Learning, IIT Gandhinagar in April 2022. She has been contributing to content development in Mathematics and facilitated many workshops. She believes innovative, interesting and engaging content can lead to a much-needed change in today’s education system.
