# Ghutukade Vinod Dinkar

Vinod Ghutukhade did his Information Technology Diploma from Dr S & S. S. Ghandhi College of Engineering & Technology, Surat. After attending a CCL workshop for creative STEM training for 4 months in 2019, he was awarded by Hon’ble Chief Minister Shri Vijaybhai Rupani for his work on creating models and toys. Then in 2020, he joined CCL as a STEM toy maker but fell in love with the world of cameras. He now works in CCL as a videographer, editor and cinematographer. He blends creativity and technical prowess to weave enchanting visual tales. Vinod creates stories around Maths and Science content at CCL, but also passionately captures life's poetry harmonizing visuals and sound. Fueled by boundless passion, Vinod approaches each project with enthusiasm, fascination and interest. He loves creating reels and videos that use the tricks of editing to create magic from the world around him.
