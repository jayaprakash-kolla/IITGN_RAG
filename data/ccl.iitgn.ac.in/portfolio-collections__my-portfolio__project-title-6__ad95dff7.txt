# Agastya Workshop

## Project Type

Master Trainers' Workshop

## Date

January 2023

Agastya International Foundation is an NGO that works on bringing science models and hands on activities to children in government schools all over India. The team of Master trainers who trains facilitators who work directly with children, was here to learn about new science experiments and demonstrations that they could implement with children in their sessions.
