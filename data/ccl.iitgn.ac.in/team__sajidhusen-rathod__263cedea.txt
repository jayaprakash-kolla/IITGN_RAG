# Sajidhusen Rathod

Sajidhusen Rathod completed Mechanical Engineering from GTU University, Gandhinagar. He worked as an Apprentice and Graduate Engineer Trainee in Research & Development Department in Indo German Tool Room. After 2 years of work in Indo German Tool Room, he joined IITGN research park where he was working on Adani & Balaji wafer plant project. He is always keen on learning and making new things. He has 3 application patents in his name. In CCL, he works with the design department and does 2d & 3d modelling. He manages all the 3d printing activities at CCL. He makes interesting puzzles and educational toys.
