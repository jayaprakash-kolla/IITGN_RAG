# Swati Shelar

Swati Shelar is a designer and educator, and she loves working with children. She ran a design studio called Jump Design India, created the Banao Bus program at Agastya Foundation, worked on research in Life Skills and Social Emotional Learning and has created toys, books and worked in EdTech. She acquired a MA in Education from Stanford University in the Learning Design Technology program and presented a paper at the IDC conference in Trondheim in 2018. She acquired a B.Des in Product Design from the National Institute of Design, Ahmedabad in 2014. She currently heads the design team at the Center for Creative Learning.
