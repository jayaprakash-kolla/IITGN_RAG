# Yasmeen Bhasin

Yasmeen is a Post Graduate in Life Sciences with a degree in Human Genomics from Panjab University. Her professional journey spans a period of 12 years and has been an amalgamation of education, embryology, stem cell research, and entrepreneurship in molecular diagnostics. She has taught science to students of grade 4 to grade 10 in CBSE & ICSE Schools. During her role of Chairperson at Genexplore Diagnostics she has been actively engaged in designing and delivering hands-on workshops and trainings to students of Life Sciences. Currently, she is a part of Biology content team at CCL and is involved in creating engaging biology activities for teacher training workshops & online students' sessions.
