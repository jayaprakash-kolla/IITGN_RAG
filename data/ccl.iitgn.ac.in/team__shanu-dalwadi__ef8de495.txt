# Shanu Dalwadi

Shanu Dalwadi completed his graduation in Electronics & Communication from the Gujarat Technological University from Engineering College, Tuwa in 2015. After graduating, he started working in the district community science centre as a coordinator. Since then, he has developed engaging content in the form of activities, workshops, seminars and science project models with the CSC [Community Science Centre]. He dreams of making project models and preparing content that explains the basic concepts of Science & Electronics to all. He currently works at CCL on many interesting electronics-related projects and programs.
