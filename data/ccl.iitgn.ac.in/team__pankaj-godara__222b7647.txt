# Pankaj Godara

"Born and raised in Bikaner, Pankaj Godara has always been fascinated by science and technology, which led him to pursue a degree in Electrical Engineering. Inspired by his late mentor, Dr. H.P. Vyas, Pankaj dedicates his career to creating hands-on learning experiences that ignite curiosity and creativity in students. After attending a hands-on workshop by Prof. Manish Jain on April 23, 2016, at Kendriya Vidyalaya Sangathan Delhi, he decided to work with him. He joined the CCL in July 2017.
Now, as the Program Coordinator at CCL, he manages the CCL in Bhopal, a collaborative initiative of the M.P. Council of Science and Technology, the Department of Science and Technology (Govt. of Madhya Pradesh), and CCL, IIT Gandhinagar."
