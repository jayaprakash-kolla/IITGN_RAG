# Parvathy Subramanian

Parvathy Subramanian is a visual artist with a large TBR pile. She has completed her BSc. Electronic Media from MOP Vaishnav College, Chennai and M.A. in History of Fine Arts with a specialization in Graphic Design from Stella Maris College, Chennai. Parvathy has many incomplete sketchbooks, and a large collection of art materials. She also runs her merchandise store called Shore City, which is currently out of stock. At CCL, IITGN she is a graphic designer and illustrator working to add sparkle and joy in every project she gets to be a part of. She has illustrated a picture book, “The worst best Friends” published by Pratham books and a non-verbal story called “Bite” which is published both in Chakmak magazine(Sep.2025) and Hatipoti Magazine, 2025. Her favourite font is Garamond!
