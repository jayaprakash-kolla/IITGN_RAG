# Human Resources (HR) Officer

Indian Institute Of Technology Gandhinagar (IIT Gandhinagar), Indian Institute of Technology, Palaj, Gujarat, India

Job Type

Full Time

https://docs.google.com/document/d/1d3JPX1JDrlcPsBWNuH81_aNf5NxCgDJd7TDKnXwbzs4/edit?usp=sharing

Responsibilites

Develop and implement HR strategies, policies, and programs in alignment with the Center’s goals and objectives.

Oversee the administration of employee benefits, including health insurance, retirement plans, and paid time off.

Conduct performance appraisals to ensure that employees are meeting job expectations and achieving company goals.

Develop and implement employee engagement programs to improve employee satisfaction and productivity.

Develop and implement training and development programs to ensure employee growth and development.

Manage employee relations, including conflict resolution, disciplinary actions, and grievance procedures.

Collaborate with other departments to ensure that HR policies are in alignment with IITGN goals.

Conduct new employee orientations and ensure compliance with all necessary documentation.

Provide HR-related reports and analysis to center leadership as needed.

Other duties as assigned.

Essential Qualifications

PG in HR or a related field from a reputed institute

5+ years of experience in HR management, including experience with HR policies, procedures, and programs.

Knowledge of applicable employment laws and regulations.

Excellent communication and interpersonal skills.

Strong problem-solving and decision-making skills.

Ability to work independently and as part of a team.

Strong organizational and time-management skills.

Proficiency in MS Office applications and HRIS systems.

Ability to work in a fast-paced, dynamic environment.

The candidates who don’t meet the above qualifications need not apply.

CCL has the right to relax qualifications & experience for deserving candidates with the approval of Competent Authority.

Desirable Qualifications

A candidate with the following skills is preferred:

Demonstrated excellent interpersonal relationship building, teamwork, and employee coaching skills, excellent active listening, negotiation, and presentation skills.

Should be capable of promoting hiring advertisements on Social Media and LinkedIn platform and various Job portals.

Ability to multitask, prioritize and manage time efficiently.

Strong phone, email and in-person communication skills

Ability to adapt to a fast-paced environment.
