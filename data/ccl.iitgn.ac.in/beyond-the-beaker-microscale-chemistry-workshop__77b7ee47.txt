### Who is eligible to attend this workshop?

This workshop is meant for Chemistry Educators, Teachers, Researchers and Resource Persons.

### What can you expect from this workshop?

Hands-on experiments covering various chemical concepts from grade 8-12.

### In what language would the workshop be conducted?

The workshop would be conducted mainly in English / Hindi.

### Is this workshop online or offline?

This workshop is offline to make it more interactive and engaging.

### Is this free or chargeable?

The workshop fee is Rs. 1800/-.

The charges include lodging and boarding.

### When will the accommodation be available ?

The accommodation will be available for participants from 2pm 10th February 2025 till 2 pm 16th February 2025.

### What is the process to attend this offline workshop?

Registration in the workshop is compulsory. Once registered, every individual has to notify the CCL team by an email ([email protected]) with Subject Line : "CCL Microscale Chemistry Workshop" confirming your registration.
