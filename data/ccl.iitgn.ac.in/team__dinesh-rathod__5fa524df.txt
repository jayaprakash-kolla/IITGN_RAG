# Dinesh Rathod

Dinesh Rathod - Dinesh is a B.Com graduate of Gujarat University. He completed his fitter course from ITI Saraspur, Ahmedabad. He was selected from a group of 250 students who attended a CCL workshop for further training for 4 months in 2019. After the completion of his training, he was awarded by Hon’ble Chief Minister Shri Vijaybhai Rupani for his work. Then in 2020, he joined CCL as a STEM toy maker. His work involves delivering workshops, building 3D toys using CAD software and large-scale exhibits on science and math that inspire teachers and children.
