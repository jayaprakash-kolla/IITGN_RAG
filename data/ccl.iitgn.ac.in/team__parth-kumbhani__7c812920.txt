# Parth Kumbhani

Project Assistant

Parth Kumbhani did his Automobile Engineering from Industrial Training Institute Surat. After attending a CCL workshop for creative STEM training for 4 months in 2019, he was awarded by Hon’ble Chief Minister Shri Vijaybhai Rupani for his work on creating models. In 2023, he joined CCL as a Project Assistant. His major responsibilities include operation of tools, controllers and machinery. He is always keen on learning new things and bringing ideas to reality via physical models.
