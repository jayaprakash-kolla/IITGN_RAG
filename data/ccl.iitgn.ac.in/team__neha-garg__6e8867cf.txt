# Neha Garg

Neha Garg completed her BSc. and MSc. in Mathematics from Panjab University and is passionate about creating Happy Math classrooms. She has been working in the field of education for the last 7 years. She started her teaching career while volunteering with Make a Difference, an NGO that catered to children from shelter homes and spent two years with them. She then worked as a teacher in a private school for two years, moving to UnMath School as a Math mentor and Content Developer for one and a half years to currently being a part of the Maths team in CCL. She is responsible for curriculum design, development of new content and conducting workshops.
