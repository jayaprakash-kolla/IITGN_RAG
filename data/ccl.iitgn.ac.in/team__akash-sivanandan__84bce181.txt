# Akash Sivanandan

Akash Sivanandan is a dedicated physicist with a strong background in materials science and sustainable energy. He holds an M.Sc. in Physics and has extensive research experience as a research student at Providence University, Taiwan, and as a Project Assistant at the Central University of Tamil Nadu. His research areas include, 2D nanomaterials, carbon nanomaterials, electrocatalysis, supercapacitors and solar cells. At CCL, Akash translates his technical expertise into impactful learning experiences by facilitating sessions, conducting content research in physics, and developing engaging hands-on activities for the students. He believes in equal opportunity for everyone, working as a STEM educator to ensure that education reaches all students.
