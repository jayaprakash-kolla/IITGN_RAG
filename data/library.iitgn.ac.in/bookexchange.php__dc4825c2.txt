## Book Exchange: Leave a Book - Take a Book!

 The Library houses the self-managed activity i.e. Book Exchange: Leave a Book - Take a Book! for many years now. The IITGN Community members who have finished reading personal copy of a book and are willing to share the same with others may leave it at the Library, Mini Library and Academic Block 8(dedicated book shelf near the Cafeteria) and the same way pick a book which is left by others in the community. This way, we can all be part of this book exchange program and circulate as many books as possible. We request the members of the community to be a part of this small step taken by the library and help promote reading among our own community.
