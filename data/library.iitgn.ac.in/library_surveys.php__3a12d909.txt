## Library Use/User Surveys

 Library carries out an Annual Library Use Survey during April- July every year seeking the inputs and feedback from the graduating students on various aspects of library space, infrastructure, resources and services. An online questionnaire designed for this purpose is circulating among the graduating students. Then after category wise list of necessary possible actions is being taken care for making an incremental improvement in the services. You may give your feedback through this online form.
