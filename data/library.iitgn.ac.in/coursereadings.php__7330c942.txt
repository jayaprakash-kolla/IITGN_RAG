## Course Readings

 Library compiles & provides a list of books recommended by the faculty members for courses being taught during a semester. These listed books are usually placed under Course Reserve- CR (not to be issued) and Short Loan- SL (issued for three days only). Such a list for the current Semester is given here. This list will enable you to find out the status of the book and will be updated from time to time. If you have any questions or need assistance, please feel free to contact Circulation Desk or send an email on [email protected]
