## Reference and Information Services

 Library offers individualized service to assist students, faculty, researchers and staff in locating the required information, or information sources available in the Reference Collection (RC) or elsewhere. Library staff at the Information Desk and in the reading area are available to help readers to,

-  discover the rich collection of reading materials.
-  identify and use electronic and print reference tools.
-  formulate information search strategies.
-  locate information in the library and elsewhere.
-  answer specific information questions. This service is provided in person or by email or in chat mode. Users are encouraged to get in touch with library staff on [email protected] or [email protected].
