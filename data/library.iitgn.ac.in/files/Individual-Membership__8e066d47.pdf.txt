WELCOME TO THE LIBRARY AT IIT GANDHINAGAR!
Welcome to the Library at IIT Gandhinagar - your window to the
world of latest information in science, engineering, technology,
humanities & social sciences. Being an integral part of academic
and research work on the campus, it provides information services
to support the teaching, learning, research and outreach activities
of the Institute by creating state-of-the-art facilities and offering
innovative services. The Library has a fast growing collection of
books, journals, magazines, CDs, DVDs and digital resources. Most
of the services of the Library are fully computerized and provides
access to a large collection of subscribed online resources and
open access resources. The Library also extends its membership
to Academics, Researchers & Students, affiliated to public funded
academic and research institute by offering access to its resources
and services.

LIBRARY GUIDELINES
Only books from the general collection are issued to the
Individual members. Reference books, course books, journals
(current issues and back volumes), reports, standards, theses,
CDs, etc are not issued to them.
Member shall be responsible for the library card issued to them.
Loss of the card should be reported immediately to the Librarian
in writing.
In case the borrowed book is damaged or lost by the member,
they have to either replace the book with a new copy or to pay for
the cost of the book at twice the prevailing market price with
additional charges as determined by the Librarian.
The borrowed books have to be returned within the stipulated due
date. In case of books returned after due date, the late fee will be
charged at the prevailing rates. The Library may recall books
issued to Individual members, if these are required by our
students, faculty and staff.
The Library reserves the right to decline an application for the
membership without assigning any reason.
Most communications from the Library with members and
vice-versa will be over the emails .
Library Online Catalogue is accessible to all members at
https://catalog.iitgn.ac.in/
