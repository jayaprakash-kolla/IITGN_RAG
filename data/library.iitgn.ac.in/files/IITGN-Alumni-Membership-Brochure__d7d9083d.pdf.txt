Digital Resources

Digital Repository

The library has an enriched collection of digital
collections that strives to cover as many academic
and research topics housed by the Institute, and
some of these e-resources can be accessed by
the Alumni members as well.
To know more about specific resources, write to us
at librarian@iitgn.ac.in /
libraryservices@iitgn.ac.in.

The Digital Repository serves as a platform for
hosting the metadata of scholarly content such
as books, book chapters, journal articles,
conference papers, presentations and other
intellectual materials, published by IIT
Gandhinagar community.
https://repository.iitgn.ac.in/
