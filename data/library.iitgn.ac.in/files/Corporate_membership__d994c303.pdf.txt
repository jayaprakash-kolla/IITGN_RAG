WELCOME TO THE LIBRARY AT IIT GANDHINAGAR!
Welcome to the Library at IIT Gandhinagar - your window to the
world of latest information in science, engineering, technology,
humanities & social sciences. Being an integral part of
academic and research work on the campus, it provides
information services to support the teaching, learning, research
activities of the Institute by creating state-of-the-art facilities
and offering innovative services. The Library has a fast growing
collection of books, journals, magazines, CDs, DVDs and digital
resources. Most of the services of the Library are fully
computerized and provides access to a large collection of
subscribed online resources and open access resources. The
Library also extends its membership to Industries and
Corporate Organizations, by offering access to its resources
and services.

BORROWING PRIVILEGES
Against this fee, the members enrolled with borrowing facility will be
issued 4 membership cards which can be used by any of their
authorised staff to avail library services. Against these cards they
can borrow one book on each card for 30 days. Membership will be
effective for one year from the date of enrolling by the Library.

SERVICES OFFERED
In addition to the borrowing privileges, the Library will provide the
following services, without affecting its primary users:
Reference, Referral and Information Service.
Consultation of Library resources & guidance within the premises
of the Library.
Bibliographic search assistance on topics of interest.
Assistance in Literature Survey.
Alert services for new addition of resources and services, and
important information dissemination.
Photocopying & articles printing services will be available at
prevailing cost (i.e. Rs. 2/- per page+ actual postage/courier
charges).
Any other assistance & guidance required in getting required
information.

LIBRARY GUIDELINES
Only books from the general collection are issued to the
Academic and Govt. Dept. members. Reference books, course
books, journals (current issues and back volumes), reports,
standards, theses, CDs, etc are not issued to them.
Member shall be responsible for the library cards issued to
them. Loss of the cards should be reported immediately to the
Librarian in writing.
In case the borrowed book is damaged or lost by the member,
they have to either replace the book with a new copy or to pay
for the cost of book at twice the prevailing market price with
additional

charges as determined by the Librarian.
The borrowed books have to be returned within the
stipulated due date. In case of books returned after due
date, the late fee will be charged at the prevailing rates. The
Library may recall books issued to members, if these are
required by our students, faculty and staff.
The Library reserves the right to decline an application for
the membership without assigning any reason.
Most communications from the Library with members and
vice-versa will be over the emails.
Library Online Catalogue is accessible to all members at
https://catalog.iitgn.ac.in/.
