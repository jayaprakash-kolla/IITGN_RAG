What is Inter Library Loan?

No library can be self-sufficient or own every
resource as it would be highly impossible to add
every item published or of interest to our user
community. Therefore, to meet the needs of our
users and fill the gaps in our collection, the Library
is offering Inter-Library Loan.

Inter-Library Loan enables you to obtain book(s)
and other reading materials which are not
available in our library collection, from other
libraries. This is done on an_Institution-to-
Institution basis. The loan period will be stipulated
by the lending library. Such materials will have to
be handled with utmost care and returned to the
library well on time.

Who can use the services?

All faculties, researchers, students, and staff
who are associated with IIT Gandhinagar are
eligible to avail this service.

What you can request?

Users may request book(s) and _ other
documents which are not available in our
collection. We request you to use our online
catalog: https://catalog.iitgn.ac.in/ before
making a request for ILL.

The lending library reserves the right to lend.

How to send a request?

Please send an email with full

How can you get your requested
reading materials delivered?

On receipt of such requested reading
materials we will inform the respective user

by an email for borrowing the same from
Library Information Desk.

How much time will it take?

It usually takes 7-10 days for arranging
reading materials through ILL. Library will
make every effort to arrange the requested
material as soon as possible, but in some
cases, it may take little more time in obtaining
the document, depending on the lending
library.
