WELCOME TO THE LIBRARY AT IIT GANDHINAGAR!
Welcome to the Library at IIT Gandhinagar - your window to the
world of latest information in science, engineering, technology,
humanities & social sciences. Being an integral part of academic
and research work on the campus, it provides information services
to support the teaching, learning, research, enterpreneurial and
innovative activities of the Institute by creating state-of-the-art
facilities and offering innovative services. The Library has a fast
growing collection of books, journals, magazines, CDs, DVDs and
digital resources. Most of the services of the Library are fully
computerized and provides access to a large collection of
subscribed online resources and open access resources.

INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY FACILITY
In-house operations of the Library are fully computerized. A good
number of computers are available for accessing the library resources
including online catalogue of holdings and e-resources. The Library
has Wi-Fi facility for accessing to the internet and Institutes’
resources, and has implemented RFID system. The Library also has
the latest photocopier & scanner for providing service.

LIBRARY GUIDELINES
Only books from the general collection are issued to the
Academic and Govt. Dept. members. Reference books, course
books, journals (current issues and back volumes), reports,
standards, theses, CDs, etc are not issued to them.
Member shall be responsible for the library cards issued to
them. Loss of the cards should be reported immediately to the
Librarian in writing.
In case the borrowed book is damaged or lost by the member,
they have to either replace the book with a new copy or to pay
for the cost of book at twice the prevailing market price with
additional

charges as determined by the Librarian.
The borrowed books have to be returned within the
stipulated due date. In case of books returned after due
date, the late fee will be charged at the prevailing rates. The
Library may recall books issued to members, if these are
required by our students, faculty and staff.
The Library reserves the right to decline an application for
the membership without assigning any reason.
Most communications from the Library with members and
vice-versa will be over the emails.
Library Online Catalogue is accessible to all members at
https://catalog.iitgn.ac.in/.
