KNOW YOUR LIBRARY
WELCOME TO THE LIBRARY AT IIT GANDHINAGAR
The library at IIT Gandhinagar is your window to the world of the latest information in sciences, engineering, technology, humanities
& social sciences. Being an integral part of academic and research work on campus, the Library provides information services to
support the teaching, learning, research and outreach activities of the Institute by creating state-of-the-art facilities and offering
innovative services. The library has a fast-growing collection of books, journals, and magazines, both in print and digital format
and occupies two floors in the newly built Academic Block 13. Most of the in-house services of the library are fully computerized
and provide access to many subscribed online and open access e-resources. An RFID system has been implemented facilitating selfcheck-out and check-in of library materials. A redesigned Library website serves as one-point access to all resources and services.

COURSE READING (CR) MATERIALS
All books recommended by the faculty members for the courses
taught by them during a semester are procured on a priority basis
and organised separately under Course Reserves (CR) and Short
Loan (SL). Books placed under CR are meant for use within the
Library, and SL is issued for 3 days. The library is exploring the
possibility of licensing many of these titles in electronic format
and providing access both on and off campus.

RFID SYSTEM
The library uses an RFID-based system where users can selfcheckout and check in library materials. Besides offering several
other facilities, this system also detects unauthorised movement of
library reading materials.

RESOURCE SHARING
The library is a member of the e-ShodhSindhu Consortium for Higher
Education (INFLIBNET), Gandhinagar, Developing Library Network
(DELNET) New Delhi, Advance Information Network of Libraries
in Gujarat (ADINET), and other professional bodies. The library
cooperates with most of the IIT/IISER and many other resourceful
libraries and library networks in the country. Inter-Library Loans
(ILL) and Document Delivery Services (DDS) are offered to faculty
members, research scholars and students through this arrangement.

IT RESOURCES
Library has many computers to access library resources, including
an online catalogue of holdings and e-resources. The library has a
Wi-Fi facility for connecting to the Internet and accessing Institutes’
resources. A Photocopier machine, scanners & printers for common
use are also available. Also five interactive touch screens have been
added recently.

The Mini Library is an extension of the Main Library. Located in the

a seating capacity for 30+ readers, computer systems for accessing
online resources, availability of magazines and newspapers, etc.
