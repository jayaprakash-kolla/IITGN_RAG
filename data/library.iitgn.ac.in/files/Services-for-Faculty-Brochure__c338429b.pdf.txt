LIBRARY MEMBERSHIP FOR
FACULTY AT IITGN‌

Your gateway to a world of knowledge and innovation.‌
We are your dedicated partner in every stage of your
academic & research journey. Our services are designed
to support your research, teaching, and professional
growth, with access to rich scholarly resources, cuttingedge digital tools, and opportunities for collaboration.
Together, let's advance discovery, drive innovation, and
shape the future of scholarship.‌

Library at IIT Gandhinagar offers a range of services to
support faculty in teaching, research, collaboration,
outreach, and other activities. Library staff engage with
each faculty member from joining, working closely to
understand the information needs. Recognizing faculty
as central to the Institute’s academic work, the library
seeks your ongoing support in building scholarly
resources, designing new services, creating facilities,
and encouraging students to make optimum use of the
library for learning and research.

ELECTRONIC RESOURCES‌‌
The Library has license to over 80 core electronic
resources and has access to resources offered by One
Nation One Subscription (ONOS). This collection is
growing every year and accessible to IITGN community
on the campus network, as well as off-campus. The list
of these subscribed e-resources is available on library
website. ‌
Visit: https://library.iitgn.ac.in/digitalresources.php‌

Faculty members gain access to library
facilities upon joining the institute and are
requested to fill the membership form. The
Institute ID card serves as the library card.
Membership is also available for family
members (parents, spouse, and children) of
all IITGN faculty.
