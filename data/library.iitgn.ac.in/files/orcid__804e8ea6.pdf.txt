ORCID

Connecting Research
and Researchers

ORCID is an open,
non-profit, community-
driven organization that
provides a unique and
persistent identifier to
researchers, connecting
them with their activities
through integration in key
research workflows.

Through linkages with
other systems, ORC!D
improves discoverability
and reduces repetitive
data entry. Researchers
control the privacy of the
information displayed in
their ORCID record, and
determine what, how and
with whom information

is shared.
