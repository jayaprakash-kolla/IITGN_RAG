LIBRARY
MEMBERSHIP

Staff members on joining the Institute are entitled
to use the library facilities by default and are
requested to fill the membership form. Institute ID
cards issued to each employee serve as library
cards. Library also offers its membership to the
Family Members (parents, spouse & children) of
IITGN employees & students. Library also offers
external memberships to facilitate institutional
linkages & interactions for mutual benefit.
