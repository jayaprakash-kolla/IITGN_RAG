LIBRARY GUIDELINES
Only books from the general collection are
issued to the family members. Reference books,
course books, journals (current issues and
back volumes), reports, standards, theses, CDs,
etc are not issued to them.
Member shall be responsible for the library
card issued to them. Loss of the card should be
reported immediately to the Librarian in
writing.
In case the borrowed book is damaged or lost
by the member, they have to either replace the
book with a new copy or to pay for the cost of
the book at twice the prevailing market price
with additional charges as determined by the
Librarian.
The borrowed books have to be returned
within the stipulated due date. In case of books
returned after due date, the late fee will be
charged at the prevailing rates. The Library
may recall books issued to the family members,
if these are required by our students, faculty
and staff.
The Library reserves the right to decline an
application for the membership without
assigning any reason.
Most communications from the Library with
members and vice-versa will be over the
emails .
Library Online Catalogue is accessible to all
members at https://catalog.iitgn.ac.in/

WELCOME TO THE LIBRARY AT IIT
GANDHINAGAR!
Welcome to the Library at IIT Gandhinagar - your
window to the world of latest information in
science, engineering, technology, humanities &
social sciences. Being an integral part of academic
and research work on the campus, it provides
information services to support the teaching,
learning, research nad outreach activities of the
Institute by creating state-of-the-art facilities and
offering innovative services. The Library has a fast
growing collection of books, journals, magazines,
CDs, DVDs and digital resources. Most of the
services of the Library are fully computerized and
provides access to a large collection of subscribed
online resources and open access resources. The
Library also extends its membership to the family
members of the employees and students by offering
access to its resources and services.

COLLECTION

ELECTRONIC RESOURCES
The Library provides access to over 70 core
electronic resources. This collection is growing
every year and accessible to IITGN community on

subscribed e-resources is available on library
website.

FAMILY
MEMBERSHIP
COMMUNITY

TO

IITGN

Library is happy to offer its membership to the
Family Members (parents, spouse & children) of
IITGN employees & students. This facility is offered
only to the family members of the employees &
students, who are currently on the roll of IIT
Gandhinagar. Family members who are keen to
enroll themselves as members will require to fill up a
prescribed form and the same must be endorsed by
the
concerned
employee/students
for
their
membership and agree to take responsibility for any
loss, damage or dues. The minimum age to avail the
family membership is 12 years.

MEMBERSHIP FEE
This membership facility is offered without any
charges.

BORROWING PRIVILEGES
One membership card will be issued per Family
member on a formal request for membership,
against which two books will be issued for 30
days from the regular collection.
This membership will also entitle them to consult
library resources and avail services within the
premises of the library.

INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY FACILITY
In-house operations of the Library are fully
computerized. A good number of computers are
available for accessing the library resources
including online catalogue of holdings and eresources. The Library has Wi-Fi facility for
accessing to the internet and Institutes’ resources,
and has implemented RFID system. The Library also
has the latest photocopier & scanner for providing
services.
