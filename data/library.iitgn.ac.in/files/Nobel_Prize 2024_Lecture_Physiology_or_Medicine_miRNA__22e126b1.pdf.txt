Caenorhabditis elegans nematode, roundworm
small size (1mm), short life cycle (~3.5 days), small genome, transparent body
several organ systems akin to those found in humans
useful model for investigating how tissues develop & mature in multicellular organisms
Fundamental discoveries: cell death, RNA interference, aging, olfaction!
