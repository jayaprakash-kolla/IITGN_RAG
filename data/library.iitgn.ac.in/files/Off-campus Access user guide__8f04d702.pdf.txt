Off-campus access to e-Resources
User Guide

Visit URL
https://idp.iitgn.ac.in/

Select the Resource which you want to access
For example ACM Digital Library

Give the IITGN SSO credentials and Click Sign In

Please select the desired option and accept

Note: If you are using your personal system, you may choose “Do not ask again.” However, if you are accessing the
resource from a public or shared system, please select “Ask me again at next login” for security purposes.

Begin Your Exploration

Note: During a session, you only need to log in to one resource at a time. Access to other
resources will be automatically enabled through cookies for the duration of the session.
