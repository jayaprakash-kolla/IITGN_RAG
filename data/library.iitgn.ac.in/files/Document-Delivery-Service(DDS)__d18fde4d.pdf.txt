Library at IIT Gandhinagar

Welcome to the Library at IIT Gandhinagar -your
window to the world of latest information in sciences,

engineering, technology, humanities & social sciences.
Being a major service center on the campus, it provides
library and information services to support the teaching,

learning»research activities of the Institute by creating
state-of-the-art facilities and offering innovative

services.

While the library at IIT Gandhinagar makes every effort to
build the core collection, both in print and digital form in
the areas of interest to the institute; it would be highly
impossible to add every published item or of interest to
our user community. In any case, no library can be self-
sufficient/own every resource. Therefore, to meet the
increasing and diverse needs of our users and fill the gap
in our collection, the library is offering Document
Delivery Service.

Who can use the services?

All faculty, researchers, students, and staff who are
associated with IIT Gandhinagar are eligible to use this
service.

What can you request?

Users may request book chapters, journal articles,
conference papers, and other reading materials. We insist
users to go through our subscribed list of e-resources
before requesting for a document. (see overleaf)

How to send a request?

Please send an email with full bibliographic details of the
items required on any of the following emails:

« libraryservices@iitgn.ac.in

¢ librarian@iitgn.ac.in

How will the requested material be delivered?
Normally, the requested material will be supplied at your
workplace. In special cases, we may request you to
collect it from the library circulation desk.

How much time will it take?

The library will make every effort to deliver the requested

material as soon as possible, but in some cases, it may
take little more time in obtaining, as we depend on the
lending library from where we obtain the requested
articles.

Cost

This service is delivered to the IIT Gandhinagar
community at no cost. In some cases, the requester may
be asked to pay the actual cost of the service.

Where from we obtain the materials?

The library is associated with national and international
libraries, library networks, and library consortia. The
library is also a member of E-SodhSindu and other
library networks such as ADINET (Advance Information
Network of Libraries in Gujarat), DELNET (Developing
Library Network), INFLIBNET (Information & Library
Network), and national documentation centres. Library
also has an excellent arrangement with IITs, IISERs,
NITs, IIMs, and other resourceful libraries in the
country to meet our requirements.

Precautions

Kindly note that the requested items delivered to you are
covered by copyright and should be used for your
academic and research purposes only.

(Note: Full-text e-books are not available through this
