Library at IIT Gandhinagar offers Five group study rooms for
the IITGN community for academic and professional
collaboration and brainstorming.
Location: The rooms are located on the first floor of the library
at the end of the reading hall and digital access area.
Timings: Group Study Rooms are available only during the
Library Opening Hours. Kindly check the timings of the Library
before booking.
Capacity:
● Group Study Room No. 1 (201E): 4 people
● Group Study Room No. 2 (201D): 4 people
● Group Study Room No. 3 (201C): 8 people
● Group Study Room No. 4 (201B): 6 people
● Group Study Room No. 5 (201A): 4 people
Facilities: Group Study Room No. 1. 2, 3 & 5 are equipped with tables, chairs, AC and
Smartboard with an Integrated Camera for video conferencing. Group Study Room No. 4 (201B)
is equipped with tables, chairs and AC.
Group Study Room Reservation Policies
● All students, staff and faculty members of IITGN can reserve the group study rooms for
collaborative projects for groups of two or more. For solo use, exceptions can be made
for users appearing for interviews, meetings and other remotely interactive activities.
● Requests for reservation must be made by using Google Calendar. To learn how, click
here.
● One group can reserve a room for 3 hours at a time.
● If one does not show up to a reserved room after 10 minutes, they will lose their
reservation. When not reserved, rooms are first come, first served. Any cancellations of
bookings need to be communicated over the email at librarycirculation@iitgn.ac.in at
least 60 minutes prior to the booking time.
● The institute ID card of one of the group members needs to be submitted at the
Information Desk.
● If all the group members are absent from the room for more than 15 minutes, Library
will consider that the users no longer require the room and their belongings from the
