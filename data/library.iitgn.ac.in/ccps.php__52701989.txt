Library has number of computers in both libraries for accessing to e-resources, searching of book(s), CDs/DVDs, Theses and Technical Reports. User can use of those computers as when they required during Library opening hour.

## Computers, Copying, Printing, Scanning

### Copying and Printing Service

 The self-operated photocopying and printing facility is available free of cost in the library. This service is limited to library materials and particularly for copying and printing of book chapter, research papers published in journals, conference proceedings for academic and research work without violating the Indian Copyright. The facility is located in the left side of lobby area in the library. Please bring papers with you while coming for photocopying purpose.

### Scanning

 Library has the following two scanners available for use. You are welcome to avail this facility for scanning any official documents, reading materials (subject to copyright restrictions) and other important documents relating to teaching, research & learning activities.

#### The Bookeye® 4 Basic- Book scanner by Image Access GmbH, Germany

#### ScanSnap SV600 by Fujitsu Ltd. Japan

 It would be good to have a prior intimation from you, if you have more materials to scan & also require more time.
