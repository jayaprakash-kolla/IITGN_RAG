## Library Membership for Alumni

 The Library has been offering its services to the Alumni of the Institute. All registered members of IITGN Alumni Association (holding membership card) by default are entitled to use the library resources (with some restrictions) and services. In addition, those alumni who would like to use the books borrowing facility will require to fill in a separate form. The details of the membership, fee to be paid and services offered are given in the brochure.
