## New Additions of Books

 Library brings out a weekly list of books and other reading materials added to the collection. This list is arranged under broad subject headings and is sent to the institute community over the email. These new addition of books are kept on display for reference and consultation for a week. These are normally displayed on every Monday and will remain on display till the end of week. However, they can be reserved when they are on display and will be issued after Friday afternoon.
