## Resource Sharing

 The Library has been actively involved in resource sharing from the very beginning. It is a member of E-Shodh Sindhu (ESS-MoE/INFLIBNET) - Gandhinagar, Developing Library Network (DELNET), ADINET and other professional bodies. Complete list of institutional membership is available here. Library has made cooperative arrangement with most of the IIT, IISER Libraries, many other resourceful libraries and library networks in the country. Through this arrangement, Inter Library Loan (ILL) and Document Delivery Services (DDS) are offered to faculty members, research scholars and students.
