- Roundtable discussion on “Critical AI Literacy for Librarians": On August 25th, 2026, The IITGN Library organised a roundtable discussion on “Critical AI Literacy for Librarians”, bringing together academic librarians and library professionals from prominent institutions, including IIMA, CEPT University, NID, Nirma University, and INFLIBNET, together with senior colleagues from IIT Gandhinagar and Visiting Professional Aditi Gupta, Engineering & Science Librarian, University of Victoria, Canada.The programme provided a platform for participants to share their experiences, current practices, future plans, suggestions, and challenges, both existing and anticipated in adopting AI in libraries.
