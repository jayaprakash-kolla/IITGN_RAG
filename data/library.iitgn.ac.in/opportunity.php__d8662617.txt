## Working with Library

 Library offers the following need-based opportunities to interested candidates to who meet the stipulated requirements.

-  Library Professional Internships: Fresh Library Science graduates are given the opportunity to work with the Library, and learn on the job.

- Lib-TAN: The Alumni Association of all past trainees of IIT Gandhinagar Library.

-  Library Internship (as part of Course requirement): As a part of the course requirement, students of Library and Information Science gets acquainted with the workflow of our library for a few weeks.

-  Summer Research Internships Program (Institute): As a part of Summer Research Intership Program (SRIP), Library appoints people with technical background to assist in various projects with the Library.

-  On Campus Employment Opportunities (Institute): The IITGn Students can work in the oCEO opportunities and collaborate with Library for developing various Library services.

-  Social Service (Institute): Students get an opportunity to work in the Library for a stipulated period of time. If you are interested in working our library, you may please write to [email protected]
