## Visitor Information

 Visitors are welcome to visit our Library to consult in person and onsite any of the print and digital resources available as part of our collection. Library staff will be happy to assist you with required information and guidance in making your visit fruitful. To use the resources and services, the Library provides a temporary Library Visitor Card for a limited period. Visitors planning to avail this service will need to produce their identity and affiliation proof and complete the formalities to get the card. There will be no borrowing facility extended to the visitors. It is advisable to write in advance email at [email protected] and seek permission and accordingly plan the visit to avoid any last minute inconvenience.
