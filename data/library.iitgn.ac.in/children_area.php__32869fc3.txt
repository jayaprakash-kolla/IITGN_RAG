## About Children’s Area

Welcome to the magical realm of the Children's Area at IITGN Library!

 The library at IITGN invites the young readers to explore a vibrant space and dedicated webpage designed especially for children. It offers engaging books, reading platforms, podcasts, magazines, and more to spark imagination and curiosity. Whether you're just beginning your reading journey or already a passionate young explorer, this space is designed to inspire creativity, joyful learning, and a lifelong love for reading. Dive into colourful picture books, thrilling graphic novels, and age-friendly content that makes reading both fun and meaningful.

 Discover even more! Join us for captivating storytelling sessions, lively reading events, and productive workshops that inspire creativity. Explore the excitement and learning in store for you at the Children's Area. Click here for upcoming events.

 Are you ready to embark on your reading adventure? Click here to explore the children’s collection.

#### Family Membership (Who can avail?)

 Since the beginning, the library has been offering memberships to the family members (parents, spouses, and children) of our faculty, staff, and students. The membership allows one to borrow 02 general books and 06 children’s books for 30 days. The minimum age to avail the family membership is 12 years. The membership form is available here.

## New Arrivals in Children’s Collection

The library brings out a weekly list of books and other reading materials added to the collection. This list is arranged under broad subject headings, including Children’s Collections. These new additions are displayed for reference and consultation for a week. These are displayed every Monday and will remain on display till the end of the week. However, they can be reserved when they are on display and will be issued after Friday afternoon.

 This list of new additions, for the convenience of our users, is hosted here.
