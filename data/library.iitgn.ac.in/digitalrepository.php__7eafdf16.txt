## Digital Repository

 The Library has a ‘Digital Repository’ which serves as a platform for hosting the scholarly content such as books, book chapters, journal articles, conference papers, presentations and other intellectual materials generated by IIT Gandhinagar community. To begin with, this repository contains the metadata and abstracts of publications and is accessible on the campus network only. Gradually, full text of each document will be added to this repository on clearing copyright related issues and will also be opened for access on Internet.

We request the faculty and students to browse through this repository and give us your valuable feedback to improve it further. You may email us your feedback at [email protected]
